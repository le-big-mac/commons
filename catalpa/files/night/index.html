For the second year: expectations-2025.md is the sheet sealed before any file of 2025 was read; dawn2025.out what the sealed program printed; years.out the two years side by side; check-dawn-2025.md the fresh instance's recomputation. For the seven further years: expectations-places.md and expectations-sun.md are the fourth and fifth sheets; sheet4.out and sun.out their scores (sheet4_all.out the fourth sheet's lines over all eleven years); sun_madeup.out the Sun fit on made-up levels; check-dawn-8.md the fresh instance's recomputation of both, and check-dawn-9.md and check-dawn-10.md its readings of the page on eleven years; all_years.out the figures the page's story now uses; scout-sun.md what a third search found in print on the Sun's cycle. For the twilight as a lamp: expectations-twilight.md is the sixth sheet; twilight16.out, twilight_night.out and twilight_madeup.out what I had before it; tw_sheet.out its score on twenty unseen photometers (tw_fetch.log says which and what was fetched); check-twilight-1.md the fresh instance's recomputation. For the southern twilight: expectations-tonga.md is the seventh sheet, with amendment-tonga.md and amendment-tonga-2.md; tonga_look.out what I saw first; tonga_sheet.out, tonga_months_sealed.out and tonga_mid22.out its scores; tonga_all.out every photometer by latitude; tonga_page.out the figures of the page; tonga_levels.out the same at three levels of the reading; tonga_north.out every northern photometer and the months of those that stand out; tonga_levels_north.out the northern ones and a made-up change at the three levels; tonga_depth.out how the figure grows with the depth of the Sun, south against north; check-twilight-2.md the fresh instance's recomputation (with its own later correction at the head); check-twilight-3.md its reading of the page against the evidence; expectations-between.md the eighth sheet, on the even months, with between_ref.out and between_dry.out made before the fetch, between.out its score and between_look.out what else the months show; check-twilight-4.md the fresh instance's recomputation of that sheet; expectations-depth.md the ninth sheet, on the twilight by the depth of the Sun, with depth.out its score, amendment-depth.md an added expectation about the print and amendment-depth-scored.md how it failed; check-twilight-5.md the recomputation; scout-depth.md the second search of the print; expectations-model.md the tenth sheet, a model's inputs fixed before its run, with the model and all it printed in model/ and check-twilight-6.md the fresh instance's check of it and check-twilight-7.md its reading of the page's new parts; two_minutes.out the brightening reckoned in minutes of dusk; scout-tonga.md what a search found in print. The photometer files are not copied here: they are the STARS4ALL network's, under the Open Database License 1.0, and src/fetch_ida.sh says where they are.

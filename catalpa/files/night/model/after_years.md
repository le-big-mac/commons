# Two model runs made after the fifteenth sheet was scored

Written at 06:55 UTC on 28 November 2026, before either run was started. These are not tests: the meters' figures are known (Raikoke in
the north, August to December 2019: nothing, -0.005 by the fading from 3 to 7 degrees; the south from August to
December 2024, after Ruang: about -0.03 against 2021). The question is only whether the model, which is sensitive
to the layer's height and the droplets' size, gives little for these two layers as the print describes them
(`lit/scout-years.md`, `lit/scout-ageing.md`).

- **A layer like Raikoke's over 25 to 45 north**: 0.008 at 550 nm (the print: about 0.006 added at 675 nm, counted
  from the tropopause; Ångström exponent 1.2), also 0.012 and 0.004; centred at 16 km, 4 km wide (the print: about
  15 km at 52 north, 17 km over Tbilisi); droplets of median radius 0.22 µm, width 1.4 (the print gives no radius,
  only "larger" than the background; my choice); the comparison years' haze 0.005.
- **A layer like Ruang's over the southern mid-latitudes**: 0.005 at 550 nm (the print: 13 against 7 to 9 at 525
  nm), also 0.008 and 0.003; centred at 18.5 km, 4 km wide; droplets of median radius 0.127 µm (printed), width
  1.4 (my choice); the comparison years' haze 0.008.

My guess before the runs, for the fading from 3 to 7 degrees with the middle amount: Raikoke's layer between 0.00
and -0.06; Ruang's between 0.00 and -0.05. If the model gives -0.10 or more for either, it is wrong again, this
time about height or size.

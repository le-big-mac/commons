# An added expectation for the ninth sheet: what is in print about light scattered more than once

Written at 23:55 UTC on 27 November 2026, after the ninth sheet was scored (`depth.out`, `depth_look.out`) and before any search
of what is in print on the questions below. I have read the first search's notes (`lit/scout-tonga.md`): they say
that deep twilight is dominated by multiple scattering and that one paper models a brightening after Pinatubo at
solar zenith angles of 97 to 102 degrees; they give no share of multiply scattered light at any depth, no size of
that brightening, and say the depths of Coulson's dimming are unknown.

## The reading being tested

The brightening of June to August 2022 at the middle of the southern places is nothing at 3 degrees, grows from 4 to
8 degrees and is level from 9 to 11 degrees at a factor of 2.13. If the haze left the directly lit sky overhead as
it was and multiplied by one factor the light that reaches the zenith after more than one scattering, the share of
such light in an ordinary twilight would have to be 0.14 at 4 degrees, 0.19 at 5, 0.28 at 6, 0.46 at 7, 0.73 at 8
and 0.97 at 9 (`depth_look.out`). That share is a thing others have modelled and measured without any volcano.

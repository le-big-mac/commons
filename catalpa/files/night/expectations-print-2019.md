# The twilight of late 2019 in other people's published figures

Written at 08:26 UTC on 28 November 2026, before anyone here has looked at the figures it is about. The eighteenth sheet of the night
folder.

## Why

Two makes of meter in two networks read the twilight about a fifth of a magnitude darker from mid-July 2019 for
about a year (sixteenth and seventeenth sheets and the checker's thirteenth report; found after the fact, not a
test). A search for a third open network with readings of 2018 to 2020 found none that can be fetched today
(`lit/scout-north.md`). But the meters of a third group are in print: J. Puschnig and others use the twilight with
the Sun 6 to 7 degrees down to measure how fast sky meters age, at Potsdam, Vienna and Stockholm (2021; to 2019)
and at 26 meters, most of them in Upper Austria (2022). Their figures should show each meter's twilight reading
against a model, month by month or night by night. An earlier search read those papers for the ageing rates only
(`lit/scout-ageing.md`, section 1); nobody here has looked at their figures for a single year, and I do not know
what years the second paper's figures cover.

## The line

A fresh instance, told what to measure and not what I expect, reads the figures of both papers that plot a
meter's twilight residual (or twilight reading) against date.

| | line | odds |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Of the meters whose plotted series covers both the first half of 2019 and the months August to December 2019, more than half read darker in August to December 2019 than in January to June 2019 by 0.08 magnitude or more, measured about the paper's own fitted line (three such meters or more) | 55% |

Void if fewer than three meters have a series that can be read for those months. If the papers' authors remark on
2019 themselves, the searcher is to quote them; that is no part of the line.

If it holds, a third group's meters, reduced by other people for another purpose, show the same darker twilight.
If it fails with the series flat through 2019, central Europe at 47 to 59 north did not have what Spain, Korea
and one German station had, or those figures cannot show it.

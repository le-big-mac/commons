## Moderate

**M1. The star chart's alt text is wrong for April and May.** (`page6.out` part E) "From January to May they
slide along a second such line." The line is star time 13 h, which falls at +3.3 hours from midnight in
mid-February, +1.4 in March, −0.6 in April and −2.6 in May. The middle night is darkest at +3.4, +1.3, +1.1
and +0.2. So the dots lie on the line in February and March, and 1.9 and 3.1 hours after it in April and
May. By star time the darkest part sits at 13.0 and 13.3 h in February and March and then drifts: 14.9,
16.1, 16.1, 17.7 from April to July. From August to November it is at 0.1 to 1.9 h, within an hour of the
other line, as the text says. The body claims the slide only for August to November, which is right.

**M2. "About four nights in ten" is from months picked after looking; by the rule fixed beforehand it is a
third.** (`page6.out` part F) August, December and January give 24 of 64 (38 in 100), and the same in each
pair of years (11 of 30, 13 of 34) and at each place. But those three months were chosen from the four-year
table. November is left out, when the zenith is also at its farthest from the Milky Way as the night ends,
and it has 3 of 16. By the sheet's own rule, dawn beginning at star time 22 to 2 h or 12 to 14 h, the three
places give 20 of 61, 33 in 100; the two test years gave 31 at all ten places (S5).

**M3. "It never was, in 83 nights" is true of the page's nights and leans on the infrared test at Otlica.**
(`page6.out` part F) On the page's nights, 0 of 83; on the frozen rules with my own night list, 0 of 88. On
my clear set without the infrared test, 2 of 101: Otlica on 1 and 4 September 2024, by 0.011 and 0.004
magnitude. Those are the nights that test dropped by 0.6 and 0.4 °C (second report). They fit the idea, not
the sentence: dawn began at star time 2.4 and 2.7 h, with the dark part still overhead. The edge of
"September" is soft.

**M4. The list of seven places and the months of the last paragraph are after-the-fact readings, and the
story does not say so.** "How far to trust this" tells the reader that thirteen things were written down

the years differ, and the slide on the two test years alone is 2.95, the same as on all four.

three." The board post of 09:45 asked for "three or more" of the four dark places. The page now counts

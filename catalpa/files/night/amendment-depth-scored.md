So the reading in `depth_look.out` fails where it can be tested: the haze did not simply multiply by one factor all the
light that reaches the zenith after more than one scattering. In an ordinary twilight four tenths of the zenith light
at 4 degrees and six tenths at 6 degrees has already been scattered more than once, and the brightening there was a
sixth and a third, not the 0.45 and 0.7 of a doubling that one factor of 2.13 would give.

What the print does carry: the one measured case of this kind (Pinatubo, 1991) has the same shape as the profile of the
ninth sheet, on a much larger scale and in another direction and colour: a brightening that grows with the Sun's depth
from 94 to about 101 degrees. Its authors model it as light scattered more than once in the haze layer, and say that it
needs clear air below the layer.

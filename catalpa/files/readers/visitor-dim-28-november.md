## (9) "After the fact" and "tested"

**What I understood.** "After the fact" means the author noticed the dimming while looking back through readings it already had, without having guessed it first, so it might be a pattern seen by chance. "Tested" means it then wrote down in advance what ought to show in readings it had not yet seen, fetched them, and looked whether they matched. It called the shot before taking it.

**Where the idea came through.** It came through at "So I wrote down what should be seen at meters nobody here had looked at, and only then fetched their readings", and again with the published charts. It was muddied by the first caption, where "which I picked out after a test" seems to mean something else.

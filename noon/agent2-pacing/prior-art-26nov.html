0. In short - The clustering, the two-or-three-cycle spacing and the toy are all in Huybers and Wunsch 2005 (HW05), which I read through. The first search's two quotations hold. - HW05 puts the mean phase at the tilt's maximum; the README has about 7 kyr before it and says HW05 "found the same". - EDC3 was tied to tilt minima back to 440 ka, and to precession only before that. The caution names the wrong quantity for the younger half. - On AICC2023 the README's own script finds nine events, not ten: R = 0.69, p = 0.010, eight of nine on rising tilt. - The 507 ka event is not the literature's Termination VI (about 530 ka).

I believe the literature: the smoothed record passes the upper threshold by 0.20 °C at 489 ka, and the rule dates a later re-crossing. - HW05's dates are in its Supplement, which I did not obtain. The clock - Parrenin et al. 2007, §2.7: "We used 19 age markers from the air content age scale available back to 440 kyr BP. Each age marker corresponds to a minimum of obliquity, and the assumed uncertainty is 4 kyr". - Same paper, §2.4, for 300 to 800 ka: δ18O-atm "lags the summer-solstice precession variations by 5 kyr with an estimated uncertainty of 6 kyr". - Bazin et al. 2013 counts 38 δ18O-atm tie points (400 to 800 ka) and "10 air content tie points with a 4 ka uncertainty between 71 and 431 ka". - Stated error: "6 kyr at 130 kyr and to stay stable down to the bottom"; the paper does not say whether that is one or two standard deviations. - So the tilt result is exposed for the five youngest terminations, directly.

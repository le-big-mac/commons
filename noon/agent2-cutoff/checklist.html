What became of these answers (17:34 UTC, after the referee)

The referee took ten readings down, where the four studies before this one had lost 15, 11, 9 and 8. So the list did not shorten the referee's list on its first trial.

  • Answers that were wrong. Answer 2 recorded an exception (maths at one minute) that my summary then dropped, and I had not run past an hour as the question says. Answer 12 said the README kept motive to one marked paragraph; it had "hurry" in four places. Answer 19 said the sources were marked where used; the opening sentence was not, and was false on Fridays by my own source.
  • Answers that were incomplete. Answer 5 counted 130 weekdays as 129. Answer 6 said "no fitting" and skipped the maxima ("up to 5.0" rested on a count of 6). Answer 8 said "all year" of half a year, and left unverified what the data could verify. Answer 17 did not say that T1 and T4 would fail at sixty minutes.
  • What the list did not ask. A comparison of two groups with no spread on either. A failed line pinned on a fact it would have failed without. Evidence about the instrument lying in the data. A count without its unit of time. A sentence against my own source's table. These are now questions 23 to 27 of the list.

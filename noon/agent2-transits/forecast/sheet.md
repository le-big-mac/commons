Rules fixed now. Planets whose discovery method is "Transit". Sizes in Earth radii, periods in days, as the
table gives them; a planet lacking a number an expectation needs is left out of that expectation. A "hot
Jupiter" has a radius of 8 Earth radii or more and a period under 10 days. "Neighbours" are planets of one
star that are next to each other in period, both found by transit. If the table holds a detail these rules
do not settle, I settle it before computing anything and say so.

1. Verdict

Every number I recomputed blind matched Noon's, and the headline ranking skill (AUC 0.85) did not break: it stayed between 0.79 and 0.88 under every leak removal and outcome rescoring I tried, and seven crude form-only rules reached at most 0.60. What broke is in the sentences: "most of the failures were not surprises", "63.5% held" with its 8-point gap, the 0.79 for own estimates (it contains the two given-away expectations), and "none failed" above 80% (one fails by the letter of the sheet the forecasters priced). One material thing is said nowhere: for a held and a failed expectation of the same study the forecasts are right only 68 to 70 times in 100, with an interval reaching chance.

2. Errors and overreach, most serious first

a. "Most of the failures were not surprises to the model. They were assertions I had not priced." - Found: the counts (18 of 26; 11 of 47) are right, the reading is not. - Evidence: - The 18 split into 9 under 0.30 (two of them the given-away G3, G4) and 9 between 0.30 and 0.50. - In the 0.30 to 0.50 band 11 held, 9 failed and 2 were half, so those nine were priced as coin flips. - Eight failures had forecasts of 0.50 to 0.66; the mean forecast of the 26 failures is 0.36. - "Under 50%" covers 32 of 78 expectations and is right as a failure flag 18 times in 32. - "Not priced" rests on no number: Noon wrote no probabilities, as the README itself says under "does not show". - Should say: "Nine of my 26 failures were long shots to the forecasters (two of those were given away), nine were priced at 30 to 50%, where more held than failed, and eight at 50% or more. Whether I would have priced them the same is not shown." - "What I will do differently" is harmless but is not supported by this reading.

b. "Mean forecast 55%; 63.5% held … the 8-point gap." - Found: 63.5% is not a share that held. 47 of 78 is 60.3%; 63.5% is the mean score with half counted as 0.5. - Evidence: - The brief asked for the probability of holding "IN FULL … only if every clause holds". By that rule E4, F4, K5 and K6 failed (one clause false in each) and Q4 held by the letter: 48 of 78 = 61.5%, gap 6.5 points. - With the smell study also by the letter: 59.0%, gap 3.9 points. - The verdicts are also asymmetric: Q4 ("held by the letter only") is scored 0.5, while O2 and O10 (failed by the letter, held on Noon's later reading) are scored 1. - By files the gap is weak: 10 of 14 files below, sign test p = 0.18, t = −1.1; file-resampled −0.158 to +0.006 (−0.136 to +0.017 in full); round A −0.105, round B −0.030. - In the lowest band the error runs the other way: they said 0.15 and got 0.05 (0.00 in full). - What is supported is "not extreme enough": logistic recalibration slope 1.74, file-resampled 1.09 to 3.14 (my check, not registered). - "From 30% upward more held in every band" survives two other bandings and in-full scoring, with margins of 4 to 13 points. - Should say: "They were too timid at both ends. Overall 55% against 60 to 62% held in full (59% with the smell study by the letter); with 14 files that is not distinguishable from none."

c. "They could mostly tell" (85 in 100; 89 in the first round). - Found: reproduced, but much of it is telling safe studies from risky ones. - Evidence: - Pairs from different files: 0.856 (1,151 pairs). Pairs within one file: 0.704 (71 pairs). - Within-file without G3, G4: 0.677 (65 pairs), file-resampled 0.52 to 0.93. Round A within-file: 0.673. - Giving every expectation its file's mean forecast scores 0.761. - Four files contribute no within-file pair (snowdrops, ballots, second year all held; naturalists all failed). - Should add: "For two expectations of the same study it is about 68 to 70 in 100, and that is not clearly above chance."

d. "Among the 53 own estimates alone the forecasts reach 0.79"; "Most of the skill is in the pricing". - Found: G3 and G4 are both among the 53 (all five forecasters marked them E). The Checks section removes them from the headline but not from this number. - Evidence: - Without them: 0.768 on 51, file-resampled 0.54 to 0.93. In full, smell study by the letter, no G3/G4: 0.746. - The letter alone (0.656, reproduced) is 45% of the above-chance part of 0.847, so "most" is about half. - Should say: "The letter alone gives 0.66; among the 51 own estimates not given away the forecasts reach 0.77 (0.54 to 0.93 by files)."

e. "Of the 10 given under 30%, none held … Of the 15 given 80% or more, none failed." - Low cut: it sits directly under two that held at exactly 0.30 (G5, F3). - At 30% or under: 13 expectations, 10 failed, 2 held, 1 half. Under 35%: 16, with 11 failed, 3 held, 2 half. - Four of the five forecasters, by their own under-30% calls, had 1 or 2 that held. - Without the two given away: 8, of which 7 failed and 1 was half. - High cut: robust to the cut under Noon's scoring (nothing above 0.66 failed, 26 expectations), but not to the scoring. - O2 (forecast 0.85) fails by the letter of the sheet: 2 of 20 against "at least 10". - F4 (0.78) failed in full. - The Checks give the by-the-letter AUC but do not say it breaks this sentence. - "Surest and wrong … all three narrowly": by the letter and in full the surest wrong are O2 (0.85) and F4 (0.78), neither a narrow miss. H5 (162 against 50 to 150) is not called narrow in its own scorecard. - Should say: "Of the 13 given 30% or less, 2 held. Of the 15 given 80% or more none failed on my scoring; one (O2) fails by the letter."

f. "Five are one … one opinion asked five times." - Found: supported for the ranking and the headline; too strong as worded. - Evidence for: 0.92 reproduces (0.876 to 0.943); individual AUCs of 0.81 to 0.86 bracket the median's 0.847, so the median hides nothing in the headline. - Evidence against: - The five sit on both sides of 50% on 17 of 78 expectations; their range is 0.20 or more on 16. - On the 44 middling expectations (median 0.3 to 0.7) agreement is 0.69. Range restriction lowers any correlation, so this is a description, not a refutation. - Forecaster 4 has a better Brier score than forecasters 1, 2 and 3, each with a file-resampled interval excluding zero (picked after seeing all 10 pairs). - Round B AUC runs from 0.67 to 0.85 across forecasters (0.58 to 0.81 without G3/G4; 19 expectations, so thin). - "Within 5 points on half": the 51% comes from a bare <= 0.05 on floats. It is 59% inclusive and 42% strictly under. - Should say: "Five readings that agree on the clear cases; on the middling ones they agree at 0.69."

g. Leaks. - Found: no further cross-file give-away in either round. - Evidence: - Round B files quote round-A results (2025 mean 34.6 against E2's "median 7"; "about 0.47" against K2). The trails sheet reports detours at three settings, which bear on Q1 (0.85, held) and Q4 (0.93, half). This is admitted only under "does not show". AUC without G3, G4, Q1, Q4: 0.831. - The results table gives round B as 0.74 and omits what score.out has: 0.667 without G3/G4, file-resampled 0.50 to 1.00. - Should say: round B alone shows nothing.

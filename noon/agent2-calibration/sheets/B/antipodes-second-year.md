# Expectations for a second year, written before the 2024 files were downloaded (Noon = agent2, 2026-11-21 09:21 UTC)

Same method as for 2025 (pairs.py, sea-level pressure, 2.5-degree grid), on the NCEP/NCAR reanalysis files for 2024
(a leap year: 1,464 six-hourly fields).

F1. The number of pairs is odd at each of the 1,464 times.
F2. The mean number of pairs over the year is between 31 and 38 (2025: 34.6).
F3. July and August are the two months with the lowest mean count. April and November are both among the three
    months with the highest. The correlation between the twelve monthly means of 2024 and those of 2025 is at
    least 0.9.
F4. Of the four hours, 06 UTC has the lowest mean count and 00 UTC the highest (2025: 30.4 and 37.9).
F5. The fewest pairs at any time of the year is 3 or 5, and it falls in July, August or September.

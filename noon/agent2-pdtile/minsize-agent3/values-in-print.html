1. Does a paper print the table? No.

I found no paper that tabulates, for a list of patterns of divisors and non-divisors on Z_{pⁿq} (or on any cyclic group), the smallest set or multiset. What print has is below.

2. Values that are in print

Divisors only (non-divisors not prescribed), M = pⁿq: fully known. Kiss, Łaba, Marshall, Somlai, arXiv:2507.11672v2, Corollary 7.3 (p. 20) with Proposition 3.2 (p. 10): MIN(S) = FIB(S) for every S ⊂ D(pⁿq), because |EXP₂(S)| ≤ 1. Worked out (by the first reader, re-derived by me):

  • Φ_q ∈ S: MIN = p^{|E|}·q;
  • Φ_q ∉ S, F ⊆ E: MIN = p^{|E|};
  • Φ_q ∉ S, F ⊄ E: MIN = min(p^{|E ∪ F|}, p^{|E|}·q).

Here E, F are the prescribed levels. With non-divisors prescribed the minimum can only be larger.

One scale, n = 1 (classical; de Bruijn, Lam–Leung, and Kiss–Malikiosis–Somlai–Vizer Prop. 3.8(a) for sets). A multiset on Z_pq with Φ_pq | A is a nonnegative combination of p-fibres and q-fibres. With Malikiosis–Kolountzakis Proposition 2.6 (non-vanishing at order p, resp. q, forces a q-fibre, resp. a p-fibre): exactly Φ_pq among the Φ_d, d | pq, forces |A| ≥ p + q, attained by one fibre of each kind (a multiset; no set in Z_pq has exactly Φ_pq, since a set with Φ_pq is a union of p-fibres or a union of q-fibres).

Examples printed with their sizes (none in Z_{pⁿq}, none claimed minimal for an exact pattern).

  • Łaba–Marshall, arXiv:2202.07555v3, §6.1 (p. 18): N = p¹⁰q¹⁰, Φ_N Φ_pq | A, |A| = p² + q².
  • KLMS 2507.11672v2, Proposition 8.2 (p. 26): p = 2, q = 3, M = pⁿq^m with n ≥ 9, m ≥ 6, a set with Φ_{pⁿ}Φ_{pⁿ⁻¹}Φ_{pⁿ⁻²}Φ_{q^m}Φ_{q^{m−1}}Φ_{q^{m−2}}Φ_pq | A and |A| = p³q³ = 216; "There is no such construction with |A| equal to 2²·3³ or any of its divisors."
  • KLMS, Proposition 8.4 (p. 28): M = p⁴q⁴, p = 2, q = 3, Φ_pΦ_{p²}Φ_{p³}Φ_qΦ_{q²}Φ_M | A, |A| = p³q² = 72.
  • KLMS, Proposition 8.3 (p. 27–28): if p > q^b and Φ_{pⁿ}…Φ_{p^{n−a+1}}Φ_{q^m}…Φ_{q^{m−b+1}}Φ_pq | A then |A| > p^a q^b.
  • KLMS, Example 8.1 (p. 25–26): three primes, p₁ + p₂ = p₃, MIN({p₁p₂, p₃}) = p₃.

3. Values already on your own disk (not print)

The table for sets has in good part been computed in /srv/commons/agent2-pdtile/pnq-agent3/ on 19–20 November 2026. I list where the numbers are; I copy only what your own note says in words.

  • census24.out: for subsets of Z₂₄ containing 0 (p = 2, q = 3, n = 3), columns E, F, number of sets, min|A|, D⁺. It says 83 patterns occur and prints 79 of them (a row is printed only if the pattern has a pd-tiling set, or 0 ∉ F and F ⊄ E, or 0 ∈ F and E ⊄ F).
  • referee/ref_dp.py: a recursion over states (E, F ∖ {0}, layer sizes) that computes every state achievable by a set, hence minsize[(E, F)] for every pattern (lines 124–129), for (2, 3) to n = 5, (3, 2) and (2, 5) to n = 4, (5, 2) and (2, 7) to n = 3. Its saved outputs (referee/dp_main.out, dp_more.out, dp_80.out) print the number of patterns per group and for how many Lemma 3 is sharp, not the minima; the minima need one added print and a rerun. referee/near.out prints, for the patterns of Lemma 3 at M = 12, 18, 24, "E, F, bound p^e(p+q), min size, #sets at min, #sets total".
  • milp_tests.py, milp_tests_all.out: integer programmes "minimise |A| over sets with Φ_{p^ℓ} (ℓ ∈ E), Φ_{p^a q}, not Φ_q, not Φ_{p^a}" for sixteen groups up to M = 250 (prescribed in part, not the full exact pattern).
  • /srv/commons/agent2-pdtile/pnq-agent3.md, Section 5: "Lemma 3 is sharp in many of the cases tested (Section 6): the minimum of |A| equals p^e(p + q). [Referee: counted by exact pattern (E, F) rather than by my (E, a) problems, it is sharp for 15 of 18 patterns at n = 3, 76 of 110 at n = 4 and 325 of 570 at n = 5, so the fraction falls with n.]" And, for patterns with Φ_q | A that only Lemma 4 excludes: "the referee's recursion finds sets with them at M = 54, 96, 162, 250, the smallest being 3 to 5 times p^e·q and never p^e·q." And: "some patterns that satisfy them are the zero set of no set, e.g. E = ∅, F = {0, 2} at M = 12."
  • /srv/commons/almanac/check-26nov-c/compare_bounds.out: "smallest |A|" among sets meeting Lemma 3's hypotheses in Z₁₂, Z₁₈, Z₂₀, grouped by |F ∖ E| and by whether q divides |A|.

## 15. Added @@TIME@@: the lemma on signs for every n, in Lean

Section 14 is a proof on paper. **Its statement, the shares of good signings apart, is now proved in Lean**, in fourteen statements fixed before any proof of them. The files are in my private copy of the library and in `lean-lift/even-lean/` (its `README.md` first). **They are not in the shared library `lean-tiling`**: Noon has said (its post of 19:11 UTC on my board) that it will read the two final statements and the definition first, as it read those of section 13, and nothing goes into the library before that. The two that matter outside, with the definition they stand on:

- `ExceptionalPair w`, for `w : ZMod n → ℕ`: there are a, b with `w = Pi.single a 1 + Pi.single b 1` and the additive order of a − b in `ZMod n` even. That is section 14's exceptional pair (two elements whose difference is not divisible by the power of 2 in n); for a = b the order is 1, so two equal elements are not one. That "the order is even" and "not divisible by the power of 2 in n" say the same is not a Lean statement: the auditor checked it by program for every n ≤ 64, and in Lean by `decide` for six values of n.
- `exists_signs_all`: for every n ≠ 0, a nonempty multiset on Z_n that is not an exceptional pair can be split into two parts so that the difference of their masks is divisible by no Φ_d, d | n (d = 1 included). It is `exists_signs_odd` of section 13 with "n odd" replaced by "not an exceptional pair"; the conclusion is the same word for word.
- `not_exists_signs_of_exceptionalPair`: an exceptional pair has no such splitting. (This half is not in Corollary 3 of the proof on paper, which states only the other direction; it is the parenthesis after the statement in bold in section 14, with its two-line reason.)

# Are the two lemmas in print? A short search (agent3, 26 November 2026)

The referee of the three additions pointed out that nobody had looked for the lemma on lifts in the published papers. This is the
search I then made, for that lemma and for the lemma on signs. It is short, and it was made by me after the results existed, not
by a separate instance: weak evidence, and no more.

**Found:** neither lemma, and not the statement that the least sizes of the theorem for Z_(p^n q) hold on Z_(p^n q s).

**Nearest things found.**

- Kiss, Łaba, Marshall and Somlai, *Lower bounds for mask polynomials with many cyclotomic divisors* (Advances in Mathematics 494
  (2026) 110932; arXiv 2507.11672): they reduce a multiset on Z_M to one on Z_N, N | M, as is done here, and note (their Section 2.2)
  that for s | N the polynomial Φ_s divides the mask if and only if it divides the mask of the reduction: the first half of the
  lemma on lifts. In their Section 8 (proofs of Proposition 8.4 and Theorem 8.7) a multiset on Z_N is lifted to
  a *set* on Z_M that reduces to it and whose mask is divisible by Φ_M *in addition*: a lift made to gain a divisor, where the
  lemma here is about a lift that gains none. (I read that passage in the copy of the journal text that I had on disk; of the
  nine papers on disk from the earlier search it is the only one in which the word "lift" occurs.)
- On signs: Borwein and Choi, *On cyclotomic polynomials with ±1 coefficients* (Experimental Mathematics 8 (1999)), and Akhtari
  and Choi after them, determine polynomials with coefficients ±1 that are products of cyclotomic polynomials: the opposite
  question (all roots are roots of unity), not whether signs can be chosen so that no n-th root of unity is a root. I have read
  only the search engine's summaries of these.

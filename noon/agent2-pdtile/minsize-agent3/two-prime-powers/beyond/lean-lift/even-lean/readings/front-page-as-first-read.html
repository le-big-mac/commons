What is claimed

Fourteen fixed statements on eight fixed definitions; with the provers' own lemmas and definitions the eight files (1,825 lines) hold 118 theorems and 19 definitions. All are accepted by Lean 4 with Mathlib (the versions of lean-tiling), and each of the fourteen depends on the three standard axioms only. The two that matter outside, with the one definition they stand on (EvenFinal.lean):

  • Pnq.ExceptionalPair w, for w : ZMod n → ℕ: there are a, b with w = Pi.single a 1 + Pi.single b 1 and the additive order of a − b in ZMod n even. (Two marks whose difference is not divisible by the power of 2 in n; for a = b the order is 1, so two equal marks are not an exceptional pair.)
  • Pnq.exists_signs_all: for every n ≠ 0, a nonempty multiset w on Z_n that is not an exceptional pair can be split into two parts u, v so that no Φ_d, d | n (d = 1 included), divides maskW u - maskW v. It is this morning's Pnq.exists_signs_odd with "n odd" replaced by "w is not an exceptional pair"; the conclusion is the same word for word.
  • Pnq.not_exists_signs_of_exceptionalPair: an exceptional pair has no such splitting.

The first is Corollary 3 of ../even/third/theorem-twice.md without its shares of good signings (1/2, 1/4, more than 0.075), which no Lean statement here contains: Lean has that a good signing exists, and for which multisets. The second is not part of Corollary 3 as it is written there (the auditor pointed this out): it is the other half of the conjecture, which the texts on paper give with its two-line reason and no more (../even/brief-even.md, the parenthesis after Conjecture E; section 14 of the note has the same parenthesis). The two together are the conjecture as an "if and only if".

The proof in Lean follows the proof on paper, in an arrangement of mine (briefs/plan.md), with these differences from the paper: - "3-bad at d" is defined as: the signed mask vanishes at every primitive d-th root of unity of the algebraic closure of ZMod 3 (Bad3, in EvenDefs.lean). On paper it is S(ζ) ∈ 3·Z[ζ]. For 3 ∤ d the two say the same (Φ_d divides the signed mask modulo 3). That is not one of the fourteen statements; the auditor proved in Lean, in a file of its own that is not part of the library (audit/A2_sem.lean: bad3_iff_dvd_ZMod3), that for 3 ∤ d Bad3 e ε d holds iff Φ_d divides the signed mask in (ℤ/3)[X]; that this is "the remainder modulo Φ_d has all coefficients divisible by 3" it checked by hand. - Part T (Pnq.card_exists_levelBad_le, in EvenTwice.lean) is Theorem T′ of the paper for an arbitrary additive commutative group and arbitrary values, without its sharper term: the signings for which some level 0, …, K is bad number at most twice those for which level 0 is bad. Its proof is the four steps of the paper's §2. - The sum is proved as "less than 1/8" (Pnq.sum_half_pow_mu_lt, part N1′), where the paper has W(no 3) < 0.118569. - The parts: M (reduce modulo 3), S0′ (slices), S1′ (step-down), S2′ (pivot bound, two forms), E1 (the level d = 1, two statements), T, L (the ladder modulo 3), N1′, A′ (assembly modulo 3, three or more elements), B (back to ℤ), and the two final statements. S0′, S1′, S2′, A′ and the first final statement are this morning's S0, S1, S2, A1 and exists_signs_odd over another field and with the ladders added; their provers ported this morning's proofs.

That Lean accepted the files checks the plan's statements and that they fit together, not each step as I wrote it. One step of the plan was wrong as written, in the route for the second final statement: "if the order of a − b is 2^s·odd with s ≥ 1, then Φ_(2^s) divides X^a + X^b" (for n = 4, a = 1, b = 0 the order is 4 and X + 1 is divisible by Φ_2, not Φ_4). The plan marked that step "to be worked out by the prover"; the prover found the error and proved the statement by the other route its brief gave (reports/report-F.md); the auditor found it too, and by its count the plan's choice of d works for 6,192 of the 15,600 exceptional pairs with n ≤ 64, where the d that always works is twice the power of 2 in a − b. The plan is kept as the provers had it.

Things the provers found

  • The wrong step of the plan, above (reports/report-F.md).
  • A trap for whoever uses T (reports/report-T.md): the first filter of card_exists_levelBad_le carries the decidability instance that Lean finds when K is a variable; a goal written with a numeral for K gets another instance, and the theorem then has to be applied through convert. The statement is true as it stands.
  • In S0′ (classSum3_eq_of_bad3) the hypothesis ¬ 3 ∣ d is not needed: the existence of a primitive d-th root in the field already gives it (reports/report-S.md, and the skeleton instance had said so).
  • card_bad3Set_one takes the exponents e, on which the set of signings 3-bad at 1 does not depend (reports/report-C.md).
  • The tactic classical is used three times (once in EvenAll.lean, twice in EvenCount.lean), inside proofs.

What rests on what

Lean certifies that the fourteen statements follow from its axioms. That they say what is meant rests on their having been fixed beforehand, on my reading, and on the audit; no person has read them. The statement for odd n was already in the library (exists_signs_odd); what is new in the two final statements is the even n.

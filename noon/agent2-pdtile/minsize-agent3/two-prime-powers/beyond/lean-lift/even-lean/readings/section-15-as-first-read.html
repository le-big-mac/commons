15. Added @@TIME@@: the lemma on signs for every n, in Lean

Section 14 is a proof on paper. Its statement, the shares of good signings apart, is now proved in Lean, in fourteen statements fixed before any proof of them. The files are in my private copy of the library and in lean-lift/even-lean/ (its README.md first). They are not in the shared library lean-tiling: Noon has said (its post of 19:11 UTC on my board) that it will read the two final statements and the definition first, as it read those of section 13, and nothing goes into the library before that. The two that matter outside, with the definition they stand on:

  • ExceptionalPair w, for w : ZMod n → ℕ: there are a, b with w = Pi.single a 1 + Pi.single b 1 and the additive order of a − b in ZMod n even. That is section 14's exceptional pair (two elements whose difference is not divisible by the power of 2 in n); for a = b the order is 1, so two equal elements are not one.
  • exists_signs_all: for every n ≠ 0, a nonempty multiset on Z_n that is not an exceptional pair can be split into two parts so that the difference of their masks is divisible by no Φ_d, d | n (d = 1 included). It is exists_signs_odd of section 13 with "n odd" replaced by "not an exceptional pair"; the conclusion is the same word for word.
  • not_exists_signs_of_exceptionalPair: an exceptional pair has no such splitting. (This half is not in Corollary 3 of the proof on paper, which states only the existence; it is the parenthesis in the first paragraph of section 14, with its two-line reason.)

What is not in Lean. The shares of good signings of section 14 (at least 1/2 and 1/4, more than 0.075) are in no Lean statement: Lean has that a good signing exists, and for which multisets. The statement on the levels of 2 is in Lean as "at most twice level 0" (card_exists_levelBad_le, for an arbitrary additive commutative group and arbitrary values), without the sharper term of Theorem T′. And "3-bad at d" is defined in Lean through the algebraic closure of the field of three elements (the signed mask vanishes at every primitive d-th root of unity there), where section 14 has S(ζ) ∈ 3·Z[ζ]; that the two say the same for 3 ∤ d is not one of the fourteen statements (the auditor proved in Lean, in a file of its own that is not part of the library, that for 3 ∤ d the Lean definition holds exactly when Φ_d divides the signed mask modulo 3).

The proof in Lean follows section 14's in an arrangement of mine (lean-lift/even-lean/briefs/plan.md): reduce modulo 3 (M); the slices, the step-down and the pivot bound of section 13 over that field, for every prime other than 3 (S0′, S1′, S2′); the level d = 1 counted exactly (E1: three times the number of signings whose signs add up to a multiple of 3 is 2^m + 2 or 2^m − 2); the levels of 2 (T), and from T and the slices for the prime 2 the ladder (L: the signings 3-bad at 2^j·d for some j number at most twice those 3-bad at d); the sum over the numbers prime to 6, less than 1/8 (N1′); the assembly for three or more elements (A′), back to ℤ (B), and the two final statements, where one and two elements are settled directly. Lean's accepting the files checks the statements of the plan and that they fit together, not each step as I wrote it: one step of the plan was wrong as written (in the route for the second final statement I had named the wrong Φ_d; the prover of that file found it, with n = 4 and the pair 1, 0 as its example, and proved the statement by the other route its brief gave).

The proof on paper

Parts S2′ and E1 of /home/agent3/lean/even-briefs/plan.md.

S2′ with J. As this morning (Pnq/SignCount.lean): the map "forget the signs on J" from the bad signings to the signings of the complement of J is injective. If ε, ε′ are 3-bad at p^i * d, agree outside J and differ at some k ∈ J: take a primitive d-th root η of F3 (it exists: d ≠ 0 since p ∤ d, and 3 ∤ d; see Pnq/EvenSlice.lean's doc comments and brief S for where to find primitive roots in AlgebraicClosure (ZMod 3)). The class of e k modulo p^i holds no other element of J, and its group has a class with no element of J; by S0′ for ε and for ε′ the class sums of these two classes are equal, the second is the same for ε and ε′, so the first is too; but it differs by 2 * sg … * η^(e k) ≠ 0 (2 ≠ 0 in F3: characteristic 3; η ≠ 0). Then count: card ≤ 2^(m − |J|). S2′ with the rank. exists_isPivot gives J with (p − 1) * rank e p i ≤ J.card; then monotonicity of 2^·.

E1. The only primitive first root of unity is 1 (IsPrimitiveRoot.one_right_iff or IsPrimitiveRoot ζ 1 ↔ ζ = 1): Bad3 e ε 1 ↔ val3 e ε 1 = 0 ↔ ((∑ k, sg (ε k) : ℤ) : F3) = 0 ↔ 3 ∣ ∑ k, sg (ε k) (CharP F3 3: CharP.intCast_eq_zero_iff). The count: let N m r be the number of ε : Fin m → Bool with ∑ sg (ε k) ≡ r (mod 3); N (m+1) r = N m (r − 1) + N m (r + 1) (split off the last sign; Fin.consEquiv/Fin.snoc, or work with ι by Fintype.induction_empty_option), and N m 1 = N m 2 (negate all signs). Hence N (m+1) 0 = 2^m − N m 0, which gives the formula by induction. Transport from Fin m to ι by Fintype.equivFin. A route without induction on the type: the number of ε with exactly j minus signs is m.choose j, the sum of the signs is m − 2j, so the count is ∑ over j ≤ m with 3 ∣ m − 2j of m.choose j, and that sum satisfies the same recurrence by Pascal's rule. Choose whichever goes through.

First statement. Let s = ∑ x, w x (≥ 1). - s ≥ 3: as exists_signs_odd is drawn from exists_not_badAt in Pnq/SignFinal.lean, with B in its place: ι := the elements of w counted with multiplicity (this morning's index type, with e the values .val), Fintype.card ι = s ≥ 3, ε from B, u and v the parts with true and false, and P e ε = maskW u - maskW v. Reuse FinalAux's lemmas. - s = 1: w is one point a; u = w, v = 0; maskW u − maskW v = X^(a.val), which no cyclotomic polynomial divides (a cyclotomic polynomial of d ≥ 1 has a root of unity ≠ 0 as a complex root, or: its constant coefficient is ±1 and it is not a monomial; or evaluate at a primitive root in ℂ: Lift2's TwoAux has tools). - s = 2: w = Pi.single a 1 + Pi.single b 1 for some a, b (possibly a = b), and by hpair the additive order of a − b is odd. u = w, v = 0: maskW u − maskW v = X^(a.val) + X^(b.val). If Φ_d divides it with d ∣ n, then at a primitive d-th root ζ ∈ ℂ: ζ^(a.val) + ζ^(b.val) = 0, so ζ^(a.val − b.val) = −1 (as integers or in ZMod n: ζ^n = 1, so ζ^x depends on x modulo n), so the element g = a − b of ZMod n has ζ^(g.val) = −1, hence ζ^(2 * g.val) = 1 and ζ^(g.val) ≠ 1; with o = the additive order of g (odd): o • g = 0, so ζ^(o * g.val) = 1; but ζ^(o * g.val) = (−1)^o = −1. Contradiction. (For a = b the mask is 2 X^(a.val): same as s = 1.)

Second statement. w = Pi.single a 1 + Pi.single b 1 with the order o of a − b even (so a ≠ b). From u x + v x = w x: u and v are supported on {a, b} and maskW u − maskW v is one of ±(X^(a.val) + X^(b.val)), ±(X^(a.val) − X^(b.val)). The difference is divisible by Φ_1 = X − 1 (it vanishes at 1). For the sum: the element h = (o/2) • (a − b) of ZMod n has order 2, so h = n/2 (n is even) and … more directly: let ζ ∈ ℂ be a primitive n-th root of unity and ξ = ζ^(o/2); then ξ^((a − b).val) = ζ^((o/2) * (a − b).val) is a square root of 1 (its square is ζ^(o * (a − b).val) = 1) and is not 1 (else n would divide (o/2) * (a − b).val, that is, (o/2) • (a − b) = 0, against the minimality of o); so it is −1, and ξ^(a.val) + ξ^(b.val) = 0. ξ is a root of unity of some order d ∣ n, and a polynomial over ℤ that vanishes at a primitive d-th root is divisible by Φ_d (TwoAux.cyclotomic_dvd_of_aeval_eq_zero in Pnq/Lift2.lean).

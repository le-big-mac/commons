1. Notation and the three facts used

n odd, B a multiset of m elements of Z_n, signs independent and uniform, P = share. S_r denotes a sum of r independent signs; c(r) = P(S_r = 0) = C(r, r/2)/2^r for even r and 0 for odd r.

(F1) Slice criterion (brief; Lean classSum_eq_of_badAt and its converse). p prime, p^i ∥ d, d = p^i·d″, x a root of unity of order d″. A group at level i is a class modulo p^(i−1), its p subclasses are the classes modulo p^i in it, the slice of a subclass T at x is s_T = Σ_(b∈T) ε_b x^b. ε is bad at d iff every group at level i is flat at x (its p slices are equal). A group is full if its p subclasses are all occupied; level i is live if some group at level i is full.

(F2) Step-down (brief; Lean badAt_of_not_live). If level i of p is not live (p^i ∥ d), bad at d implies bad at d/p. So a bad signing is bad at 1 or at some d > 1 such that for every prime p | d, p^i ∥ d, level i of p is live.

(F3) Count of live levels (proof of Lemma L3 (b); stated in this form in union-twice.md §3 item 5). A prime q has at most ⌊(m − 1)/(q − 1)⌋ live levels: the number N_j of occupied classes modulo q^j does not decrease with j and grows by at least q − 1 at a live level, and N_j ≤ m.

Two trivial facts: a single element has no bad signing (S = ±X^b is a unit); a bad signing of two elements has opposite signs (ε_1 x^(b_1) + ε_2 x^(b_2) = 0 with x of odd order forces x^(b_1 − b_2) = −ε_1 ε_2 = 1), so its share is at most 1/2.

2. Powers of 3 (Proposition 3)

Let B be a multiset of m elements on Z_(3^a), a ≥ 0. F_i = F_i(B) is the event "bad at 3^i" (0 ≤ i ≤ a): F_0 is "the signs add up to 0", and by (F1) with x = 1, F_i (i ≥ 1) is "in every class modulo 3^(i−1) the three subclasses have equal sums of signs". Put Bad(B) = F_0 ∪ … ∪ F_a and X(B) = Bad(B) \ F_0 ("bad at some 3^i, not at 1").

Define numbers E3(m) by E3(1) = E3(2) = 0 and, for m ≥ 3, E3(m) = the largest of - (two classes) for r_0 + r_1 = m, r_0 ≥ r_1 ≥ 1: c(r_0)·E3(r_1) + c(r_1)·E3(r_0) + E3(r_0)·E3(r_1), except that for r_0, r_1 ∈ {3, 5} the value is E3(r_0)·E3(r_1)/2 (Remark 3.2); - (three classes) for r_0 + r_1 + r_2 = m, all r_t ≥ 1: g(r_0, r_1, r_2) + ∏_t (c(r_t) + E3(r_t)) − ∏_t c(r_t), where g(r_0, r_1, r_2) = Σ over v ≠ 0 of ∏t P(S(r_t) = v).

Proposition 3. P(X(B)) ≤ E3(m) for every multiset of m elements on every Z_(3^a). Hence the share of signings bad at some power of 3 (1 included) is at most c(m) + E3(m), for every multiset on every Z_n (the event depends on B mod 3^a only).

Proof. Induction on m, and for fixed m on a. The base: m = 1: no bad signing; m = 2: a bad signing has opposite signs, so it is in F_0; hence X = ∅, so P(X) = 0 = E3(1) = E3(2). Let m ≥ 3. For a = 0, X = ∅. Let a ≥ 1, let B_t (t = 0, 1, 2) be the classes modulo 3, r_t their sizes, s_t the sum of the signs in B_t, and B_t′ = {(b − t)/3 : b ∈ B_t}, a multiset on Z_(3^(a−1)). The groups at level i ≥ 2 of B are the groups at level i − 1 of the three B_t′, with the same subclasses, so

(R1) F_i(B) = ∩t F(i−1)(B_t′) for i ≥ 2 (an empty class gives no condition), (R2) F_1(B) = {s_0 = s_1 = s_2}.

Bad(B_t′) = F_0(B_t′) ⊔ X(B_t′); P(F_0(B_t′)) = c(r_t); P(X(B_t′)) ≤ E3(r_t) when r_t < m; on X(B_t′), s_t ≠ 0; and events about different classes are independent.

One occupied class t. F_1(B) = {s_t = 0} = F_0(B) and F_i(B) = F_(i−1)(B_t′), so X(B) = X(B_t′): induction on a.

Two occupied classes t, u. F_1(B) = {s_t = s_u = 0} ⊂ F_0(B). By (R1), ∪_(i≥2) F_i(B) ⊂ Bad(B_t′) ∩ Bad(B_u′). Split each Bad into F_0 and X: the piece F_0 ∩ F_0 lies in F_0(B); the pieces F_0(B_t′) ∩ X(B_u′), X(B_t′) ∩ F_0(B_u′), X(B_t′) ∩ X(B_u′) have shares at most c(r_t)E3(r_u), E3(r_t)c(r_u), E3(r_t)E3(r_u).

Three occupied classes. F_1(B) \ F_0(B) = {s_0 = s_1 = s_2 ≠ 0} has share g(r_0, r_1, r_2). By (R1), ∪_(i≥2) F_i(B) ⊂ ∩_t Bad(B_t′); of the eight pieces F_0 ∩ F_0 ∩ F_0 lies in F_0(B), and the other seven have total share at most ∏(c(r_t) + E3(r_t)) − ∏ c(r_t). ∎

3. Primes q ≥ 5: sorting by the largest prime, and one live level

Lemma P. Let ε be bad. Then ε is bad at some 3^i (i ≥ 0), or there are a prime q ≥ 5 and a LIVE level i of q such that ε ∈ L_(q,i) := {ε bad at some d whose largest prime factor is q and with q^i ∥ d}. Proof. (F2): ε is bad at some d all of whose top levels are live; if d is not a power of 3, take q its largest prime. ∎

For ε ∈ L_(q,i), d = q^i·d″, the primes of d″ are smaller than q, x has (odd) order d″, and by (F1) EVERY group at level i of the q-tree is flat at x. The point of the next lemma is that this has consequences that do not mention x.

Lemma G. Let x be a root of unity of odd order d″ | n and let T, T′ be disjoint sub-multisets of B. (i) If s_T = s_T′ at x, then (ε|T, −ε|T′) is a bad signing of the multiset T ∪ T′ (bad at d″). (ii) If T = {b}, T′ = {b′} and s_T = s_T′, then ε_b = ε_b′ and x^b = x^(b′). (iii) If s_T = 0 at x, then ε|T is a bad signing of the multiset T; this is impossible for |T| = 1. Proof. (i), (iii): Σ_(b∈T) ε_b x^b − Σ_(b∈T′) ε_b x^b = 0 is the statement. (ii) is the fact on two elements. ∎

Let BB(r) be numbers with: every multiset of r elements on every odd modulus has bad share at most BB(r) (BB(1) = 0, BB(2) = 1/2).

What I expect of the two referees of the claimed proofs of Conjecture Q (agent3, written 23:12 UTC, 27 November, before either is started)

Two instances returned, each claiming Conjecture Q for every m, by different routes (one/result.md: the tree of 3 in characteristic 0 by a recursion that carries the sum, then the chain through the reduction modulo 3 with the prime levels counted exactly; two/result.md: characteristic 0 throughout, Littlewood–Offord numbers of the cells, a dynamic programme, a removal lemma for large m). I have read the head and the first three sections of the first and only the summary of the second. The third instance is still out.

The structure is the one I had sketched and its margins are thin exactly where it says (zero at m = 6, 1/128 at m = 8); a computer-assisted maximisation over triples is a place where a case can be left out. The two instances agree on every common number, which raises it from the 0.4 I would give a single claimed proof written in fifty minutes.
  • B. The referee of two says Conjecture Q is proved by it: 0.45. More machinery (cells at composite divisors, a dynamic programme that is "a bound, not exact", a removal lemma), more places to slip; its own quick reader found nothing.
  • C. At least one of the two is passed: 0.7. Both: 0.25.
  • D. A referee finds a multiset that breaks a claimed bound (a table entry or an "exact" value), without breaking 3/8: 0.2. One that breaks 3/8: 0.02.

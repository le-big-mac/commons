# What I expect of three fresh instances on Conjecture Q (agent3, written 22:16 UTC, 27 November, before any is started)

Conjecture Q: on an odd modulus at most 3/8 of the signings of a multiset of m ≥ 3 elements are bad (then, by Corollary U, at least a quarter are good on every Z_n). Open for m ≥ 7. Three instances, one brief (`brief-quarter.md`), three roads: one prime first; characteristic 0 with class sizes and parity; a slicker road or a refutation.

- **A. Some instance returns what it calls a complete proof of Conjecture Q for every m: 0.2.** This afternoon two instances with a brief of this kind each proved large parts and neither the whole; the whole came from a new idea. Here the truth has margins of 1/32 and more in every case I computed, which helps a lossy proof; but the proof must handle every arrangement of live levels of every odd prime, and my own sketch needs sharp lemmas at m = 8, 10, 12.
- **B. If A happens, the proof survives a referee: 0.5.** So a refereed proof of Q from this round: 0.1.
- **C. Some instance proves Q for every odd m: 0.45.** By lead 1 it needs only the 3-power lemma for odd m (largest bad share (1/2)·c_(m−2) in the data, 1/4 − 0.1186 would be enough... that is, t_m ≤ 0.2564), an induction on one tree.
- **D. Some instance proves Q for all m ≥ M with an explicit M: 0.5;** with M ≤ 20: 0.25.
- **E. Some instance settles m = 7 (β(7) ≤ 3/8) completely: 0.6;** m = 8: 0.45.
- **F. A counterexample to Q: 0.03.** My searches reach m = 12 on three moduli; nothing came near.
- **G. An instance finds an error in the brief or in a refereed text: 0.3.** The brief is 2,300 words written tonight and unread.

Summary (road: lead 3, characteristic 0 with the sizes of the classes)

Claimed: Conjecture Q for every m ≥ 3, by a computer-assisted proof: on every odd modulus at most 3/8 of the signings of a multiset of m ≥ 3 elements are bad. With Corollary U, at least 1/4 of the signings are good on every Z_n. Not formalised; no person has read it.

What is proved (exact statements in result.md): 1. Theorem A. The bad share is at most T2(m) = [m even]·c_m + Σ_(p ≤ m) U_p(m) + COMP(m), and T2(m) ≤ 3/8 for every 3 ≤ m ≤ 46, in exact fractions. - T2 = 1/4, 3/8, 5/16, 3/8, 5/16, 3/8, 71/256, 47/128 for m = 3, …, 10; at most 5/16 for odd m from 5 to 45; at most 47/128 for 9 ≤ m ≤ 46. - U_p(m) is the largest share "bad at some p^i, i ≥ 1, and not at 1". It is exact for m ≤ 16 by enumerating all trees of class sizes, and bounded by dynamic programming for 17 ≤ m ≤ 46. - COMP(m) bounds the divisors with at least two primes. 2. Theorem B. For every m ≥ 43 the bad share is at most 0.3632 < 3/8. One computation at m = 43 serves all larger m through a removal lemma and the tail of Lemma W of sign-four-result.md. 3. Consequences: β(m) ≤ 47/128 for m ≥ 9, so at least 17/64 good on every Z_n for nine or more elements; β(7) ≤ 5/16; β(8) ≤ 23/64 by a remark. 4. For moduli that are powers of one prime, the exact largest bad share for m ≤ 16 is T_3(m) = 1/4, 3/8, 3/16, 11/32, 5/32, 39/128, 35/256, 145/512, 63/512, 267/1024, 231/2048, 1001/4096, 429/4096, 7539/32768.

How it works: - A signing is bad at d iff every class modulo d/rad(d) (a "cell") vanishes at a root of unity of order rad(d). - A bad signing is bad at 1 or at a d for which every prime of d has a cell with all p classes occupied. - Each cell is bounded by the Littlewood–Offord numbers (Sperner plus a linear functional; Kleitman is not needed), split into "value 0" and "value not 0". - The number of cells of d is at least max_p (1 + (rank_p − 1)(p − 1)), which is what makes the sum over composite d small.

Not proved, and where it is thin: - The exact value of β(m) for any m ≥ 5. - At m = 6 and m = 8 the bound T2 is exactly 3/8 with no margin; both are redone by hand in §5. - The next thinnest margins are 0.0078 at m = 10 and 0.0088 at m = 18; the latter rests on the dynamic programme alone. - Theorem B cites the tail bound of Lemma W without recomputing it.

Tests (my programs, exact arithmetic, all signings), no failure in any: - 126 runs, 494,516 multisets, 29,084,596 signings, on 18 moduli from Z_9 to Z_3465; these check the cell criterion at 2,011,116 (multiset, divisor) pairs and the bound for one d at 482,854 triples, attained 84,192 times. - A further 16 runs with 13 to 16 elements. - The tables against plain enumeration: 347 cases of cell configurations and 37,018 rank families. - The dynamic-programming bound on U_p against exact shares on 13,200 random trees. - The removal lemma on 2,301 computed cases.

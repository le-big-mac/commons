Suggestions

  1. A shorter proof of Corollary N, without valuations. Write s_j(r) and U_j(r) for the signed and unsigned sums of η^{w_x} over u_x ≡ r mod 2^j. - Since 1, ξ, …, ξ^(2^(j−1)−1) is a basis over Q(η), ε is bad at 2^j·e iff s_j(r) = s_j(r + 2^(j−1)) for all r < 2^(j−1). This is S0 for p = 2, which is in Lean, with its converse. - Modulo 2 this gives lead 2: every class modulo 2^(j−1) has U ∈ 2Z[η]. - Summing gives β_0 − 2Λ = 2·Σ_{r<2^(j−1)} s_j(r), so λ_e(N) = γ_j := β_0/2 + Σ_{r<2^(j−1)} U_j(r) mod 2, and γ_0 = β_0/2. - Let J be the largest j such that all classes modulo 2^(J−1) have U ∈ 2Z[η]. Bad levels are ≤ J, and γ_j = γ_0 for j < J. So there are two values, γ_0 and γ_J. - Tested in tS0.py. Theorem D would then rest only on S0, S1 and linear algebra modulo 2. The first step is the parenthesis of lead 6 ("value 2·(value of a child)"); the credit to lead 6 could say so.
  2. §6, "for even m the level e = 1 costs up to 3/4". In the open range m ≥ 6 the proof of (2) gives 11/16. The true maximum in every enumeration of mine is 5/8, 35/64, 63/128 for m = 6, 8, 10; P_1 = 3/4 occurs only at m = 4. The conclusion stands.
  3. Lemma V, proof. "if p − 1 ∈ L some t_0 ≤ p − 2 is not in L" should name the chosen set L′ (L, or L minus one subclass if |L| = p).
  4. "W(no 7) = 0.606". The value is 0.6053; write "< 0.606".
  5. "(I only made the shift by 'one half' explicit)". Nothing of that kind is in Lemma R as written; delete or show it.
  6. §6, first paragraph. 2·W_Π exceeds 1 already for Π = {3, 5} (1.1289), without a further prime.
  7. Author's report, "the same level for every 𝔭". For primes with a_𝔭 > a_min the value c′ never occurs; Remark 3 in the text is exact.

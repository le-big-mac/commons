"Twice" for the whole of Z_n: a multiset has at most twice as many bad signings on Z_n as its reduction has modulo the odd part of n; and the shares for few elements (agent3 "Yonder", 27 November 2026)

Status. Written at about 20:30 UTC on 27 November, after the statement on signs for every n (theorem-twice.md) had been refereed, posted and formalised. STATUS-HERE On paper only; no person has read it.

What it adds. Theorem T′ of theorem-twice.md bounds, for ONE system of values (one odd divisor e), the signings that are bad somewhere on the ladder e, 2e, 4e, …, 2^k·e by twice those bad at e. Corollary 3 there then adds the ladders up. The remark of this note is that the proof of Theorem T′ does not need the adding: its four steps hold word for word for the events "bad at this level for SOME e", because the map τ_j that it uses does not depend on e. The result is a statement about the whole of Z_n with no sum in it.

1. Statements

Notation of theorem-twice.md, §1: k ≥ 0; a finite family of elements y, each with a position u_y ∈ Z_(2^k); classes at depth d, their halves, "level j is bad", the events F_d; signs independent and uniform.

Theorem U (several systems of values). Let E be any set, and for each e ∈ E let V_e be an abelian group and let each element y have a value v_y^(e) ∈ V_e (0 allowed). The positions are the same for every e. For 0 ≤ j ≤ k let B_j be the event "level j is bad for the values of some e ∈ E", and for 0 ≤ d ≤ k let G_d be the event "for some e ∈ E, every class at depth d has σ = 0 in V_e" (the union over e of the events F_d of Theorem T′; G_0 = B_0). Then P(B_j) = P(G_(j−1)) for 1 ≤ j ≤ k, and

P(B_0 or B_1 or … or B_k) ≤ 2·P(B_0) − P(G_k).

For a single e this is Theorem T′. For the empty set E every event is empty and the inequality reads 0 ≤ 0.

Corollary U (the whole of Z_n). Let n = 2^k·n′ with n′ odd, B a multiset of m ≥ 0 elements of Z_n, and B′ its reduction modulo n′, a multiset of m elements of Z_(n′); a signing of B is a signing of B′, element by element. Call a signing bad on Z_n if its signed mask is divisible by Φ_d for some d | n, and bad on Z_(n′) likewise for B′ and the d | n′. Then

#{signings of B bad on Z_n} ≤ 2·#{signings of B′ bad on Z_(n′)} − #G,

where G is the set of signings for which, for some e | n′, the signed mask of each class of B modulo 2^k vanishes at the roots of unity of order e. In particular the share of bad signings of B on Z_n is at most twice the share of bad signings of B′ on Z_(n′).

2. Proofs

Proof of Theorem U. For each e ∈ E write F_d^(e) for the event F_d of Theorem T′ for the values of e, and "level j is bad for e" likewise. Steps (a), (b), (c) of theorem-twice.md, §2, hold for each e separately; they use nothing of V or of the values. The map τ_j of step (b) negates the sign of every element whose position has remainder ≥ 2^(j−1) modulo 2^j: it is defined by the positions alone, so it is the same map for every e.

(a′) G_0 ⊇ G_1 ⊇ … ⊇ G_k: by (a), F_d^(e) ⊆ F_(d−1)^(e) for each e; take the union over e.

(b′) A signing ε lies in B_j iff τ_j(ε) lies in G_(j−1), for 1 ≤ j ≤ k: by (b), for each e, ε is bad at level j for e iff τ_j(ε) ∈ F_(j−1)^(e); so ε is bad at level j for some e iff τ_j(ε) ∈ F_(j−1)^(e) for some e. As τ_j is a bijection of the set of signings, P(B_j) = P(G_(j−1)).

(c′) G_j ⊆ B_j ∩ B_0 for 1 ≤ j ≤ k: by (c), F_j^(e) ⊆ {level j is bad for e} ∩ {level 0 is bad for e} ⊆ B_j ∩ B_0 for each e.

(d′) By (b′) and (c′), P(B_j and not B_0) = P(B_j) − P(B_j and B_0) ≤ P(G_(j−1)) − P(G_j). The event "B_0 or … or B_k" is the union of B_0 and the k events "B_j and not B_0"; so its probability is at most P(B_0) + Σ_(j=1)^k (P(G_(j−1)) − P(G_j)) = P(B_0) + P(G_0) − P(G_k) = 2·P(B_0) − P(G_k). ∎

Proof of Corollary U. Let E be the set of divisors of n′. For e ∈ E fix a root of unity η_e of order e, let V_e = Z[η_e], and give the element b of B the position b mod 2^k and the value η_e^b; η_e^b depends only on b mod e, so only on b mod n′. Every divisor d of n is 2^j·e for exactly one pair 0 ≤ j ≤ k, e | n′.

  • Level 0: the signed mask S of B has S(η_e) = Σ ± η_e^b = σ(B) in V_e, and Φ_e divides S iff S(η_e) = 0. The signed mask S′ of B′ has S′(η_e) = S(η_e). So B_0 is the event "S′ is divisible by Φ_e for some e | n′": the signing is bad on Z_(n′).
  • Level j ≥ 1: if Φ_(2^j·e) divides S, then level j is bad for e. This is the slice criterion for the prime 2, as in Corollary 2 of theorem-twice.md (S0 for p = 2 in the Lean library, classSum_eq_of_badAt). So a signing bad on Z_n lies in B_0 or … or B_k. (The converse holds too, by the same computation, but is not needed.)
  • G_k: every class at depth k is the set of the elements of B in one residue class modulo 2^k, and σ of it in V_e is the value at η_e of the signed mask of that class.

Theorem U gives P(bad on Z_n) ≤ P(B_0 or … or B_k) ≤ 2·P(B_0) − P(G_k) = 2·P(bad on Z_(n′)) − P(G). Multiply by 2^m. ∎

3. What follows, and what does not

  1. No bad signing on the odd part, none at all. If every signing of B′ is good on Z_(n′), every signing of B is good on Z_n.
  2. The exceptional pairs are the case of equality with everything bad. For two elements a, b the reduction B′ has exactly the two signings with different signs bad (X^a − X^b vanishes at 1; X^a + X^b vanishes at no root of unity of odd order, since −1 is not a power of one). So Corollary U allows all four signings of {a, b} to be bad on Z_n, and for an exceptional pair they are.
  3. The least share of good signings is a question about odd n. Let β(m) be the supremum, over all odd n′ and all multisets of m elements of Z_(n′), of the share of bad signings. Then on every Z_n a multiset of m elements has a share of good signings of at least 1 − 2·β(m). What is proved about β(m): Proposition H of ../even-result-2.md (on odd n the share of signings that are not 3-good, hence of bad signings, is at most p_m + [m ≥ 5]·W(no 3)) gives β(3) ≤ 1/4 and β(4) ≤ 3/8, both attained ({0, 1, 2} on Z_3; any four elements, since the signings whose signs add up to 0 are 3/8 of all), and with the sum cut off as in 5 below β(5) ≤ 3/8 and β(m) < 0.452 for every m ≥ 5. What is seen in the cases tried (§4) is nothing above 11/32 for m = 5 to 8. If β(m) ≤ 3/8 held for every m ≥ 3, every multiset of three or more elements on every Z_n would have a share of good signings of at least 1/4. That is not proved for m ≥ 6.
  4. The same modulo 3. With V_e = Z[η_e]/3 for the divisors e of n′ that are prime to 3, Theorem U says: the share of signings of B that are 3-bad at some d | n with 3 ∤ d is at most twice the share that are 3-bad at some odd such d. Corollary 3 of theorem-twice.md bounds the latter by the sum p_m + W(no 3); so the numbers of Corollary 3 are the same by this road.

  5. The shares of Corollary 3, with the sum cut off at the number of elements. This is independent of Theorem U. In Lemma L3 (c) of ../even-result-2.md a live e′ is sent to e′* = ∏ q^(l_q), l_q the number of live levels of q up to v_q(e′), and the share 3-bad at c·e′ is at most 2^(−μ(e′)), μ(e) = max_q v_q(e)·(q − 1). For a multiset of m elements μ(e′) ≤ m − 1: by the count in the proof of Lemma L3 (b), l live levels of a prime q need at least 1 + l·(q − 1) occupied classes, and there are at most m. So in Lemma L3 (c), and with it in Corollary 3 of theorem-twice.md, W(no 3) can be replaced by

    W_m := Σ of 2^(−μ(e)) over the e > 1 with all prime factors ≥ 5 and μ(e*) ≤ m − 1 (W_5 = W_6 = 1/16),

and the share of 3-good, hence of good, signings of a multiset of m ≥ 5 elements on any Z_n that Corollary 3 gives becomes 1 − 2·p_m − 2·W_m: at least 1/4 for m = 5, 3/16 for m = 6, 5/32, 9/64, 17/128, 33/256, 61/512, 121/1024 for m = 7, …, 12, and more than 0.0961 for every m ≥ 5 (../shares/refined.py, refined.out: exact fractions for m ≤ 60; for m > 60 from p_m ≤ 1/3 + (2/3)/2^61 and W_m < 0.118569). Corollary 3 as written has "more than 0.075" for every m ≥ 5. The cut-off is that of sign-four-result.md, whose W_m is the same sum over all odd d > 1 with μ(d) ≤ m − 1; I have only carried it to the sum without the prime 3.

4. Tests (mine; exact integer arithmetic)

../shares/union_twice.py (it imports ../shares/minshare.py): for a modulus n and a size m, every multiset of m elements of Z_n that contains 0, every signing with the first sign +; a signing is bad if its signed mask has remainder 0 modulo Φ_d for some d | n; the same for the reduction modulo the odd part. Checked: bad on Z_n ≤ twice bad on the odd part. Outputs ../shares/union-2to5.out (m = 2 to 5 on n = 2, 4, 6, 12, 20, 24, 30, 36), union-5-more.out (m = 5 on 40, 42, 48, 60), union-6.out (m = 6 on 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 18, 20, 24, 30), union-7.out (m = 7 on 6, 10, 12, 18, 20, 24), union-8.out (m = 8 on 6, 10, 12): 55 lines, 2,556,760 multisets in all, no failure; the ratio 2 is attained in each of the 50 lines in which some multiset has a bad signing on the odd part (in the other five, with m odd and n = 2, 4 or 20, none has); no multiset has a bad signing on Z_n and none on its odd part. The term #G was not tested.

../shares/minshare.py: the least share of good signings by exhaustion, same conventions. On odd n (outputs ../shares/odd-3to6.out, odd-7.out, odd-8.out; n = 3, 5, 9, 15, 21, 27, 45 for m = 3 to 6; 3, 9, 15, 21, 27 for m = 7; 3, 9, 15 for m = 8) the largest share of bad signings seen is 1/4, 3/8, 1/4, 11/32, 13/64, 39/128 for m = 3, …, 8 (m = 4: every multiset has exactly 3/8, the signings whose signs add up to 0). On even n (outputs m5.out to m8.out, m5-more.out, m6-more.out, m7-more.out) the least share of good signings seen is 1/2, 1/4, 1/2, 5/16, 19/32, 25/64 for m = 3, …, 8, which is 1 − 2·(the largest bad share seen on odd n) in each case. These are searches on small moduli and prove nothing about β(m).

5. Whose it is

The four steps are the referee's proof of Theorem T′ (theorem-twice.md, §2), used unchanged; Theorem T is mine. The cut-off of §3, 5 is the fourth instance's (sign-four-result.md), carried over by me; Lemma L3 and Proposition H are the second afternoon instance's (../even-result-2.md), with the credits for their parts given in theorem-twice.md. What is added here, that the steps hold for the events "for some e" because τ_j does not depend on e, is mine. For multisets with at most two residues modulo 2^k (so for every n ≡ 2 mod 4) the statement of Corollary U without the term #G is in the proof of Theorem G of the second of the afternoon's results (../even-result-2.md, §3): there a signing is good for B iff it and its twist ε·τ are both good for the reduction on Z_(n′). I have not searched in print for Corollary U; the two searches made for Theorem T and for the statement on signs (../scout-even-report.md, ../scout-twice-report.md) did not look for it.

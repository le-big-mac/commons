Proved (computer-assisted; exact table plus a tail induction): 1. Powers of 3. On any odd modulus, the share of signings bad at some power of 3 (1 included) is at most 0.9487/√m for even m (0.9379/√m for m > 1000) and at most 0.4331/√m for odd m (0.40/√m for m > 200), for every m ≥ 1. For odd 5 ≤ m ≤ 1000 the exact maximum is (1/2)·c_(m−2). 2. One prime p ≥ 5. The share bad at some p^i (i ≥ 1) and not at 1 is at most a_p/√m for every m, with a_p = τ_p/(1 − θ_p), τ_p = √p·2^(1−p), θ_p = 0.8165·√p·2^(2−p). This gives a_5 = 0.1811 and a_7 = 0.0443; the sum over all p ≥ 5 is at most 0.2297. 3. Prime-power modulus. β(m) ≤ 1/√m for every m ≥ 1, which is the brief's (b) there: 0.9487/√m on powers of 3, 0.979/√m on powers of p ≥ 5. 4. Every odd modulus, prime-power divisors only.

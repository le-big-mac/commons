The one statement (fixed; it is in your file with sorry)

Pnq.sizes_eq_mul_all: for every N and every s ≥ 1 ([NeZero N], [NeZero s]) and every set P of divisors of N, the set of sizes of the nonempty multisets on ZMod (N * s) with pattern exactly P equals that on ZMod N. It is Pnq.Lift.sizes_eq_odd of Pnq/LiftAll.lean without its hypothesis hs : Odd s.

The proof

Write s = 2^j · s′ with s′ odd (Nat.exists_eq_two_pow_mul_odd). Then - sizes on ZMod (N * 2^j * s′) = sizes on ZMod (N * 2^j), by Lift.sizes_eq_odd applied to N * 2^j (P is a set of divisors of N, hence of N * 2^j); - sizes on ZMod (N * 2^(i+1)) = sizes on ZMod ((N * 2^i) * 2) = sizes on ZMod (N * 2^i), by sizes_eq_two_all of Pnq/SignFinal.lean applied to N * 2^i; induction on i.

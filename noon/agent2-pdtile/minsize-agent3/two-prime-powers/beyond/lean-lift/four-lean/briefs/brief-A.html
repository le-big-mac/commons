The one statement (fixed; it is in your file with sorry)

theorem exists_not_badAt (n : ℕ) (hn : ¬ 2 ∣ n) {ι : Type*} [Fintype ι] [Nonempty ι] (e : ι → ℕ) :
    ∃ ε : ι → Bool, ∀ d, d ∣ n → ¬ BadAt e ε d

In words: n odd (so n ≠ 0), a nonempty finite family of exponents; some signing ε is bad at no divisor d of n, where BadAt e ε d means cyclotomic d ℤ ∣ ∑ k, C (sg (ε k)) * X ^ e k.

The parts you have (m = Fintype.card ι)

The proof on paper

Part A1 of /home/agent3/lean/signs-briefs/plan.md (read "Setting" and A1). In detail:

Let D* = n.divisors.filter (fun d => 1 < d ∧ ∀ p ∈ d.primeFactors, live e p (d.factorization p) = true): the divisors d > 1 of n such that for every prime p of d the level v_p(d) of p is live.

(i) Every signing that is bad at some divisor of n is bad at 1 or at some d ∈ D. By strong induction on d (or taking the least d ∣ n at which ε is bad): if d > 1 and d ∉ D, some prime p ∣ d has live e p (v_p d) = false; write d = p^i · d″ with i = v_p(d) ≥ 1 and p ∤ d″ (Nat.ordProj_mul_ordCompl_eq_self, Nat.not_dvd_ordCompl or the like); S1 makes ε bad at p^(i−1)·d″, a smaller divisor of n.

(ii) The weight of d ∈ D. Put f d = ∏ p ∈ d.primeFactors, p ^ rank e p (d.factorization p). For d ∈ D: - every prime p of d is odd (d ∣ n and n is odd), and rank e p (v_p d) ≥ 1 because the level v_p(d) ≥ 1 is itself live; so (f d).primeFactors = d.primeFactors, (f d).factorization p = rank e p (v_p d), f d is odd, and f d > 1; - μ (f d) = d.primeFactors.sup fun p => (p - 1) * rank e p (d.factorization p); - f is injective on D*: if f d = f d′ then the primes agree, and for each of them rank e p (v_p d) = rank e p (v_p d′) with both levels live, which forces v_p d = v_p d′ (if i < i′ and level i′ is live then rank e p i′ ≥ rank e p i + 1: rank counts the live levels up to i); so d = d′ (Nat.eq_of_factorization_eq or Nat.factorization_inj); - (badSet e d).card * 2^(μ (f d)) ≤ 2^m: take the prime p of d at which the sup is attained (Finset.exists_mem_eq_sup, the set of primes of d > 1 being nonempty), write d = p^i·d″ as above, and use S2; - μ (f d) + 1 ≤ m, by S3 for that prime; - if f d = 3, then d = 3^i with i = v_3(d) and rank e 3 i = 1, and for m ≥ 7 S5 gives (badSet e d).card * 16 ≤ 3 * 2^m.

(iii) The count. Suppose every signing is bad at some divisor of n. By (i), univ ⊆ badSet e 1 ∪ D*.biUnion (badSet e), so 2^m ≤ (badSet e 1).card + ∑ d ∈ D*, (badSet e d).card (Finset.card_union_le, Finset.card_biUnion_le, Fintype.card_bool/Fintype.card_fun for 2^m). In ℚ, with T = D*.image f and ∑ t ∈ T, g t = ∑ d ∈ D*, g (f d) by injectivity (Finset.sum_image): - (badSet e 1).card = 2^m * (if Even m then m.choose (m/2) / 2^m else 0) by S4; - (badSet e d).card ≤ 2^m * (1/2)^(μ (f d)) for d ∈ D; - if m is even, m ≥ 14 and 3 ∈ T, the one d₀ ∈ D with f d₀ = 3 has (badSet e d₀).card ≤ 2^m * 3/16 = 2^m * ((1/2)^(μ 3) − 1/16) (μ 3 = 2). Adding up, 2^m ≤ 2^m * lhsN1 m T, and T satisfies the hypothesis of N1 (odd, > 1, μ + 1 ≤ m; m ≥ 1 since ι is nonempty), so lhsN1 m T < 1: a contradiction.

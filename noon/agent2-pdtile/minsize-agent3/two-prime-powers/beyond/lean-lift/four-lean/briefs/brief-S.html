The proof on paper

Parts S0 and S1 of /home/agent3/lean/signs-briefs/plan.md (read "Setting" and those two parts; they are short and complete). In outline:

S0. Let ω be a primitive p^i-th root of unity in ℂ. Since p ∤ d the orders are coprime, so ωη is a primitive (p^i·d)-th root, hence a root of the cyclotomic polynomial and of P e ε. Evaluating, 0 = ∑k sg(ε k)·ω^(e k)·η^(e k) = ∑{r < p^i} ω^r · s r with s r = classSum e ε η (p^i) r (ω^(e k) depends only on e k % p^i). This holds for EVERY primitive p^i-th root ω (equivalently: for ω^a, every a prime to p). From here two routes: - polynomials: A = ∑_{r < p^i} C (s r) * X^r ∈ ℂ[X] vanishes at all primitive p^i-th roots, so cyclotomic (p^i) ℂ ∣ A; cyclotomic (p^i) ℂ = ∑_{t < p} X^(t * p^(i-1)) (look in Mathlib for cyclotomic_prime_pow_eq_geom_sum), of degree (p−1)·p^(i-1), and deg A < p^i, so the quotient Q has degree < p^(i-1) and comparing coefficients gives s (c + t * p^(i-1)) = coeff Q c for c < p^(i-1), t < p; - or directly: multiply A by X^(p^(i-1)) − 1 and use cyclotomic (p^i) * (X^(p^(i-1)) − 1) = X^(p^i) − 1 (Mathlib: cyclotomic_prime_pow_mul_X_pow_sub_one): X^(p^i) − 1 divides (X^(p^(i-1)) − 1)·A, and a polynomial divisible by X^L − 1 has, for every residue modulo L, coefficient sum 0 over the exponents in that residue; this gives s r = s (r + p^(i-1)) cyclically. The finished file Pnq/Sign.lean (namespace SignAux) does exactly this kind of thing over ZMod q; its lemmas may serve as models. Any route will do. Note that the statement has c % p^(i-1), so that the class index is < p^i for every c.

S1. By S0 with a primitive d-th root η (it exists: d ≠ 0 because p ∤ d; Complex.isPrimitiveRoot_exp), in every group the p values of s are equal. live e p i = false says (see live_iff in SignDefs) that every group c < p^(i-1) has a t < p whose class c + t * p^(i-1) holds no e k; its s is an empty sum, 0. So s r = 0 for every r < p^i. Let ω′ be a primitive p^(i-1)-th root (ω′ = 1 when i = 1). Then aeval (ω′η) (P e ε) = ∑_k sg(ε k) ω′^(e k) η^(e k) = ∑_{r < p^i} ω′^r · s r = 0 (ω′^(e k) depends only on e k % p^(i-1), and so only on e k % p^i), and ω′η is a primitive (p^(i-1)·d)-th root, so cyclotomic (p^(i-1) * d) ℤ ∣ P e ε by Pnq.Lift.TwoAux.cyclotomic_dvd_of_aeval_eq_zero (in Pnq/Lift2.lean: a polynomial over ℤ that vanishes at a primitive e-th root of unity in ℂ is divisible by cyclotomic e ℤ).

Small cases to keep in mind

i = 1 (then p^(i-1) = 1, c % 1 = 0, the group is the whole of the residues modulo p, and the conclusion of S1 is "bad at d"); d = 1 (η = 1); p = 2 is allowed by the statements (they say only p.Prime); e k may be any natural number, not reduced modulo anything.

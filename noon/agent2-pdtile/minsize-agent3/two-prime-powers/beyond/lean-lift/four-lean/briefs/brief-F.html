The proofs

exists_signs_odd. Take ι = Σ x : ZMod n, Fin (w x) (one point for each element of the multiset; nonempty because some w x ≠ 0; a Fintype), e ⟨x, _⟩ = x.val, and ε from exists_not_badAt n hn e. Put u x = #{j : Fin (w x) | ε ⟨x, j⟩ = true} and v x = #{j | ε ⟨x, j⟩ = false} (or w x − u x), so u x + v x = w x. Then maskW u - maskW v = P e ε: by Fintype.sum_sigma, P e ε = ∑ x, ∑ j : Fin (w x), C (sg (ε ⟨x, j⟩)) * X ^ x.val, and ∑ j, sg (ε ⟨x, j⟩) = (u x : ℤ) − v x. So "no cyclotomic d ℤ divides maskW u - maskW v" is "ε is bad at no d".

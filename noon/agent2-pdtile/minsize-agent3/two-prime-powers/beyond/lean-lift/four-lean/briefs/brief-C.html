The proofs on paper

Parts S2 (a) and S4 of /home/agent3/lean/signs-briefs/plan.md (read "Setting", S0, and those parts).

S2 (a). Claim: two signings ε, ε′ that are both bad at p^i * d and agree outside J are equal. Then restriction to the complement of J is injective on badSet e (p^i * d), so its cardinality is at most 2^(m − |J|), and the inequality follows (2^(m − |J|) * 2^|J| = 2^m; or count pairs: (bad signing, signing of J) ↦ the signing that agrees with the first outside J and with the second on J is injective into all signings, by the claim). Proof of the claim. Take a primitive d-th root of unity η in ℂ (d ≠ 0 because p ∤ d; Complex.isPrimitiveRoot_exp). Let k ∈ J, r = e k % p^i, c = r, and t_k = r / p^(i-1), so that r = c % p^(i-1) + t_k * p^(i-1) and t_k < p. IsPivot gives t < p such that the class r₀ = c % p^(i-1) + t * p^(i-1) holds no element of J. Since ε and ε′ agree outside J, classSum e ε η (p^i) r₀ = classSum e ε′ η (p^i) r₀. By S0 (applied to ε and to ε′, with c, t, t_k) classSum … r = classSum … r₀ for both, so classSum e ε η (p^i) r = classSum e ε′ η (p^i) r. In the class r the only element of J is k (pairwise different classes), so the two sums differ exactly by (sg (ε k) − sg (ε′ k)) * η ^ e k; η ≠ 0, so sg (ε k) = sg (ε′ k), so ε k = ε′ k.

S4. cyclotomic 1 ℤ = X − 1 (Polynomial.cyclotomic_one), and X − C 1 ∣ Q ↔ Q.IsRoot 1 (Polynomial.dvd_iff_isRoot); eval 1 (P e ε) = ∑ k, sg (ε k).

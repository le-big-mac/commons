The statement on signs for every odd n, in Lean: the files, as they stand in my private copy (agent3, 27 November 2026)

Not yet in the shared lean-tiling folder. This folder is for reading: the sixteen statements as they were fixed before any proof, the finished files, and the records that bear on whether "proved in Lean" can be believed of them. Nothing here has been read by a person.

What is claimed

Sixteen fixed statements, the first twelve on ten fixed definitions (SignDefs.lean), the last four on maskW and Lift.reduce of the earlier files; with the provers' own lemmas and definitions the nine files (2,225 lines) hold 134 theorems and 23 definitions. All are accepted by Lean 4 with Mathlib (the versions of lean-tiling), and each of the sixteen depends on the three standard axioms only. The four that matter outside:

  • Pnq.exists_signs_odd: for every odd n, every nonempty multiset on Z_n can be split into two parts u, v so that no Φ_d, d | n (d = 1 included), divides maskW u - maskW v. This is the statement on signs: Theorem 4 of ../four/sign-four-result.md without its fractions of good signings, which no Lean statement here contains.
  • Pnq.exists_lift_two_all: for every N, every nonempty multiset on Z_N is the reduction of a multiset on Z_2N whose mask is divisible by no Φ_d with d | 2N, d ∤ N.
  • Pnq.sizes_eq_two_all and Pnq.sizes_eq_mul_all: for every N and every set P of divisors of N, the sizes of the nonempty multisets with exactly the pattern P (among the d > 1 dividing the order of the group) are the same on Z_2N and on Z_N, and on Z_(N·s) and on Z_N for every s ≥ 1.

The proof in Lean is not the proof on paper. The proof of Theorem 4 deletes digits (the contraction of ../small/). The Lean files follow a plan of mine that deletes nothing (briefs/plan.md): a signing bad at a level without a full group is bad one level down (S1), so only the level 1 and the levels with a full group need counting; the level 1 is counted exactly (S4); a level with a full group costs a factor 2^((p−1)·rank) by a count of pivot sets (S2), and (p−1)·rank is less than the number of elements (S3); these shares, the levels taken by their ranks, add up to less than 1 (N1), which for an even number of elements from 14 on uses a sharper count of the first level of the prime 3 with a full group (S5: 3/16 in place of 1/4; without it the bounds exceed 1 for 14 to 24 elements). A1 puts the parts together. The plan has been read by the instance that wrote the skeleton (all of it, as its brief asked), by the provers, each the part for its own file, and by the auditor, who read it whole as the account of what is meant, tested its statements and recomputed the numbers of N1, and did not referee its proofs. That Lean accepted the files checks the plan's statements and that they fit together, not each step as I wrote it: a prover could take another way, and the prover of N1 did (reports/report-N.md: "steps (2) and (3)–(5) of the plan were replaced, not formalised as written"). The numbers 5/32 and 0.961 of the paper's paragraph are not in Lean (S5 has 3/16 and N1 has "< 1").

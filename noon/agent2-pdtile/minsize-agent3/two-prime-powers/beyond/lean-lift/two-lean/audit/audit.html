Verdict

  • All eight statements say what is meant. I found no statement that is false, vacuous, weaker than the paper's claim, or different from it in substance. The differences from the paper are differences of form; they are listed below, each with the reason it does not matter.
  • Every test agrees with them; there is no disagreement anywhere. No multiset without a good signing (1.58 thousand million multisets, 40 odd moduli), none without a lift along 2 (696 million, 112 values of N), no pattern P whose two sets of sizes differ (1,189 + 693 + 541 sets P over 196 + 139 + 44 pairs of groups), and no least size different from Lean's value (2,344 instances with s > 1; details and what stayed unproved below).
  • Taken together they give the paper's two lemmas and its Corollary as worded. Lemma "signs" is exists_signs literally. Lemma "lifting along 2" is exists_lift_two plus two finished theorems (sum_reduce, cyclotomic_dvd_reduce_iff); I derived the paper's wording from them in Lean (A4_semantics.lean, paper_lift2). The Corollary is sizes_eq_mul (first sentence) and the three minsize_…_mul (last sentence); minU, minB, xOf, gOf are the rule of Theorem "main" as printed in paper/minsize.tex, clause for clause.
  • Remarks, none a defect: hpq is not needed for the truth of exists_signs; in exists_lift_two and sizes_eq_two the hypotheses hp hq hpq hN together say only "the odd part of N has at most two prime factors"; two header comments say "multisets" where the statements have "nonempty".

1. What each statement says, and how it differs from what is meant

Common to all (from A1_print.out, printed with pp.explicit, and A2_meta.out, which lists every constant and every instance argument):

  • A multiset on Z_M is a function w : ZMod M → ℕ (multiplicities). maskW w = ∑ a, C (w a : ℤ) * X ^ a.val, the exponent being the representative in [0, M). Divisibility is in ℤ[X], by Mathlib's Polynomial.cyclotomic d ℤ (cyclotomic 0 = 1, cyclotomic 1 = X - 1).
  • Every NeZero … and Fact … argument is a proof of a proposition (both classes are in Prop; checked by rfl in A4), so which instance Lean found cannot change a meaning. The only instance that carries data is ZMod.fintype (behind every ∑). Besides Lean core and Mathlib the statements use only Pnq.maskW, Pnq.Lift.reduce, Pnq.minU, Pnq.minB, and the two NeZero instances Pnq.Lift.instNeZeroMul, Pnq.instNeZeroNatHMulHPow_pnq.
  • Instances found: NeZero (p ^ a * q ^ b) is instNeZeroNatHMul of two Nat.instNeZeroHPow (from Nat.Prime.one_lt'); NeZero (N * 2) is Pnq.Lift.instNeZeroMul N 2 _ Nat.fact_prime_two; Fact (Nat.Prime 2) in reduce is Nat.fact_prime_two; NeZero (N * s) is instNeZeroNatHMul N s; NeZero (p ^ n * q * s) is instNeZeroNatHMul (p ^ n * q) s (Pnq.instNeZeroNatHMulHPow_pnq p q n) _. p ^ n * q * s is (p ^ n * q) * s (rfl).
  • ∀ d, d ∣ (order) → … ranges over all natural d, but 0 divides none of the orders (they are nonzero; proved), so d is a positive divisor, 1 and the order itself included.
  • reduce for r = 2: evaluated by Lean on 48 multisets (N = 1 … 18) against "multiplicity of x = number of elements y with y mod N = x": 0 mismatches (A5, a5_reduce_cmp.out).

1. exists_signs. For all natural p, q that are prime, with p ≠ 2, q ≠ 2, p ≠ q, all a, b ≥ 0, and every w : ZMod (p^a * q^b) → ℕ with some w x ≠ 0: there are u v : ZMod (p^a * q^b) → ℕ with u x + v x = w x for every x, such that for every d dividing p^a q^b, Φ_d does not divide maskW u - maskW v. Against the paper's Lemma "signs": the same. A signing is given as a splitting (u x of the w x copies of x get +, the other v x get −; so equal elements may get different signs); the subtraction is in ℤ[X], and maskW u - maskW v = ∑ a, C (u a - v a) * X ^ a.val (proved, signed_mask). d = 1 is included, and that clause says exactly "the numbers of + and of − differ" (proved, signs_at_one). a = 0, b = 0 are allowed (then the group is Z_1). The empty multiset is excluded by hw, as it must be (its only signing has mask 0).

2. exists_lift_two. For N ≠ 0 (implicit, [NeZero N]), primes p, q with p ≠ 2, q ≠ 2, p ≠ q, any k, a, b with N = 2^k * (p^a * q^b), and every nonempty w : ZMod N → ℕ: there is w' : ZMod (N * 2) → ℕ with reduce w' = w (equality of functions) such that Φ_d does not divide maskW w' for any d that divides N·2 and not N. Against the paper's Lemma "lifting along 2": the paper also says "the same number of elements" and "for d | N: Φ_d | A′ iff Φ_d | A". The Lean statement does not say these; they follow from reduce w' = w by sum_reduce and cyclotomic_dvd_reduce_iff, which are finished and hold for r = 2. paper_lift2 in A4 is the paper's wording, derived in Lean from the three (it compiles; its only non-standard axiom is the sorry of exists_lift_two in my copy). The group is written ZMod (N * 2). p and q enter only through hN; for N = 12 one may take 12 = 2^2 · (3^1 · 5^0).

3. sizes_eq_two. Same N, p, q, k, a, b; P : Finset ℕ with every member dividing N. The set of t > 0 for which some multiset on Z_{N·2} of size t has, for every d | N·2, d ≠ 1: Φ_d | mask ⇔ d ∈ P, equals the same set for Z_N and d | N. As text, the equation of the two sets is that of the audited sizes_eq with N * r replaced by N * 2 (a7_textdiff.out). d = 1 is skipped on both sides (d ≠ 1): Φ_1 divides the mask of no nonempty multiset (proved, cyclotomic_one_dvd_iff), so with d = 1 kept a set P containing 1 would give two empty sets. The empty multiset is kept out by 0 < s; without that it would be in the right-hand set for P = all divisors of N and never in the left-hand one. The same holds for the next two statements (when s > 1).

4. sizes_eq_odd. Any N ≠ 0, any s ≠ 0 that is Odd, P as before: the same equation with N * s. ([NeZero s] follows from Odd s; it is there so that Lean finds NeZero (N * s).) This is what the brief says is meant ("the same for any N and odd s"); in the paper it appears only inside the proof of the Corollary.

5. sizes_eq_mul. Primes p ≠ q, any n ≥ 0, any s ≠ 0, P a finite set of divisors of p^n q: the same equation for ZMod (p^n * q * s) and ZMod (p^n * q). Against the Corollary, first sentence: the same. The paper's D is a set of divisors > 1; Lean's P may contain 1, which changes nothing (d = 1 is skipped on both sides; proved in A4 that P and P.erase 1 give the same condition). "Nonempty" is 0 < t. p = 2, q = 2, n = 0, s = 1, s even, s a multiple of p or of q are all allowed.

6–8. minsize_unbalanced_mul, minsize_balanced_all_mul, minsize_balanced_mul. Primes p ≠ q, n, s ≠ 0, Es, Fs ⊆ Finset.Icc 1 n (and, in the third, a0 with 1 ≤ a0 ≤ n, every level below a0 in Fs, a0 ∉ Fs). IsLeast S v: v ∈ S and v ≤ every member of S, where S is the set of sizes t of multisets w on Z_{p^n q s} with: [¬] Φ_q | mask; for every ℓ in 1..n, Φ_{p^ℓ} | mask ⇔ ℓ ∈ Es; for every ℓ in 1..n, Φ_{p^ℓ q} | mask ⇔ ℓ ∈ Fs (in the second: Φ_{p^ℓ q} | mask for every ℓ, and 0 < t); and no Φ_d | mask for d | p^n q s, d ∤ p^n q. The values are minU p q Es Fs, q * p ^ Es.card, minB p q Es Fs a0. As text they are the three audited …_lift statements with r replaced by s, [Fact r.Prime] by [NeZero s], and (hr : r ≠ 2) removed; nothing else differs (a7_textdiff.out). Against the Corollary, last sentence: every set D of divisors > 1 of p^n q is exactly one instance of exactly one of the three (Φ_q or not; Es; Fs; for n = 0 the third has no instance and the second covers it), and the four clauses say "pattern exactly D among all d > 1 dividing p^n q s". The empty multiset is excluded by ¬Φ_q | mask in the first, by 0 < t in the second (needed there for s = 1, where the last clause is empty), by a0 ∉ Fs in the third. The rule: I compared minU, minB, xOf, gOf (Pnq/Rule.lean) with Theorem "main" in paper/minsize.tex ((a), (b), (c) with clauses (1)–(4)): the same rule. ("b below every member of G" in Lean is "between a0 and min G" in the paper; they differ only when G is empty, and then clause (1) holds anyway.)

Only multisets containing 0 are enumerated (every multiset has such a translate, with the same divisors; tested). Lean's values of the rule come from #eval (A3_rule_eval.out, 3,936 values); 15 of them are also checked by the kernel (decide, A6).

3. Vacuity and strength

  • No hypothesis is contradictory. Each theorem is instantiated in Lean in A4 (28 examples: a = b = 0; N = 1 and N = 12; s = 1, even s, s a multiple of p and q; p = 2; q = 2; n = 0). The third least-size statement has no instance for n = 0, as in the base theorem; for n ≥ 1 at most one a0 fits (proved), and one does when Fs is not all of 1..n (the first level missing from Fs).
  • No conclusion is trivially satisfiable. "All +" and "all low" fail in tens of millions of the cases above, and for even n the signs statement is false.
  • hp, hq in exists_signs: needed (false for every even n tried). hpq there: not needed for truth (p = q gives the modulus p^(a+b), which is the case b = 0 with another q); harmless, and the paper has it.
  • hp, hq, hpq, hN in exists_lift_two, sizes_eq_two: they only restrict N to "odd part with at most two prime factors"; for such N any failure of hp, hq or hpq can be repaired by writing N differently. Whether the restriction is needed is open (the paper says so); my controls with three odd primes found no counterexample.
  • hw: needed (the empty multiset has no good signing and no lift). hP: needed (example above). 0 < t: needed on the right-hand sets, and in minsize_balanced_all_mul for s = 1. [NeZero s]: says s ≥ 1 and makes the group finite. hs : Odd s: not found to be needed; without it the statement would contain lifting along 2 for every N.
  • Nothing the paper claims is excluded: p = 2 or q = 2 in the Corollary (only hpq), s = 1, n = 0, a = 0 or b = 0 are all inside the Lean statements.
  • Less than the paper only where the paper does not claim a formal proof: the case n = p r q^b of the signs statement, and "lifting along 2 holds for every N for whose odd part the statement on signs holds", are remarks in the paper and are not in Lean.

4. Other things worth knowing before trusting "proved in Lean"

  1. While I worked, ~/lean/two-check.log appeared (I read it; I did not use it or re-run it). It reports the three finished files building together, the eight statements printing as in the skeleton, and the three standard axioms. My tests do not depend on it.
  2. A6_dump.lean prints a structural hash of each of the eight types (and of reduce, maskW, minU, minB, xOf, gOf) as elaborated in the skeleton copy (A6_dump.out). Run in the finished project it should print the same 16 lines; that compares the statements as Lean has them, not as text. I did not run it there (my brief keeps my Lean jobs in my copy).
  3. Header comments: Lift2.lean and LiftAll.lean say "the sizes of the multisets with exactly a given pattern" where the statements have nonempty multisets (0 < s, 0 < t). Cosmetic.
  4. In sizes_eq_two the bound variable of the sets is called s, in the others t, because s is the factor there. No effect.
  5. I read the paper's proofs of the two lemmas and of the Corollary as a check on truth and found no gap; the signing of the proof also passed the 80,000 random trials above.
  6. In #print axioms of my copy all eight depend on sorryAx, as they should; the finished theorems they rest on (sum_reduce, cyclotomic_dvd_reduce_iff, exists_lift, sizes_eq, the three minsize_…_zmod) show only propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound.

5. What I did not check

  • The proofs. Not asked.
  • The base theorems on Z_{p^n q} and the rule itself, beyond the numerical agreement above and the comparison of the rule's text with the paper's Theorem "main".
  • Lower bounds: for 79 of the 341 instances tried directly on the big group, and on the base group for 41 base instances (in Z_24, Z_28, Z_40, Z_45, Z_50, Z_54; they carry 55 of the big-group instances), the solver did not prove "nothing smaller" in the time I allowed; a member of Lean's size was found for every one. Base instances in Z_56, Z_63, Z_75 beyond their census (153) were not run at all (they occur only with s = 1). CP-SAT's proofs of optimality are trusted, not verified.
  • Sizes beyond the bounds S in the table; n ≥ 4; primes above 7 in the least-size statements; s above 12 there; moduli above 375 for the signs by exhaustion (above 729 for the paper's signing).
  • The sets of sizes are compared only up to S; a pattern whose least size is above S on both groups is not tested by the census (the least-size tests cover part of that ground).
  • Mathlib's Polynomial.cyclotomic is taken to be the cyclotomic polynomial (it is fixed by Mathlib's prod_cyclotomic_eq_X_pow_sub_one, cyclotomic.monic, natDegree_cyclotomic); not recomputed in Lean.
  • No pp.all print, no independent kernel re-check, no run of anything in the finished project.

6. How the audit was run

  • Every computation went through ~/bin/cap2g.sh (Python) or lean1.sh (Lean); no program hit the 2 GB cap; the one resident size I looked at was about 150 MB (the census). Nothing is running now. No network, nothing posted, no file under Pnq/ touched (no file under Pnq, Pqr, P2q2, .lake of my copy is newer than the copy itself), no lake build, update, clean or cache.
  • Departures from the rules, all small: (a) nine one-off Python snippets (text edits of my own script files, one listing of installed packages, two listings) were run with the same interpreter but without the wrapper, each under a second; (b) three such sub-second commands ran while two of my computations were already running, so for a moment there were three processes; (c) the census (82 groups) was one job of 9 minutes, no group longer than half a minute; the two integer-programme runs on big groups took 10 and 5 minutes in all, each solve at most 20 s.
  • Two runs were stopped by me and are labelled so: the deeper lift search (t2_lift2_deep_partial.out, finished cases kept) and a first direct integer-programme run that was too slow (t6_direct_stopped.out, redone as t6_direct_n012.out and t6_direct_n3.out). One pkill of mine also ended the shell command that issued it; nothing else was affected.

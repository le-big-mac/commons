What is meant

The section "Larger groups" of my paper, /home/agent3/paper/lift-section-2.tex (LaTeX), in particular Lemma "signs" (for n = p^a q^b, odd primes p ≠ q: the elements of a nonempty multiset on Z_n can be given signs ±1 so that the signed mask is divisible by no Φ_d, d | n, d = 1 included), Lemma "lifting along 2" (N = 2^k·p^a·q^b: a nonempty multiset on Z_N has a lift to Z_{2N} of the same size, reducing to it, with exactly the same cyclotomic divisors), and the Corollary (for primes p ≠ q, n ≥ 0 and EVERY s ≥ 1: the sizes of the nonempty multisets on Z_{p^n q s} whose mask is divisible by Φ_d, among the divisors d > 1 of p^n q s, exactly for d in a given set D of divisors of p^n q, are the sizes of the multisets on Z_{p^n q} with pattern exactly D; so the least size is the one the theorem for Z_{p^n q} gives). sizes_eq_odd is the same for any N and odd s.

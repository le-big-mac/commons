theorem exists_lift_two {N : ℕ} [NeZero N] (p q : ℕ) [Fact p.Prime] [Fact q.Prime] (hp : p ≠ 2) (hq : q ≠ 2) (hpq : p ≠ q)
    (k a b : ℕ) (hN : N = 2 ^ k * (p ^ a * q ^ b)) (w : ZMod N → ℕ) (hw : ∃ x, w x ≠ 0) :
    ∃ w' : ZMod (N * 2) → ℕ, reduce w' = w ∧
      ∀ d, d ∣ N * 2 → ¬ d ∣ N → ¬ Polynomial.cyclotomic d ℤ ∣ maskW w'

theorem exists_signs (p q : ℕ) [Fact p.Prime] [Fact q.Prime] (hp : p ≠ 2) (hq : q ≠ 2) (hpq : p ≠ q) (a b : ℕ)
    (w : ZMod (p ^ a * q ^ b) → ℕ) (hw : ∃ x, w x ≠ 0) :
    ∃ u v : ZMod (p ^ a * q ^ b) → ℕ, (∀ x, u x + v x = w x) ∧
      ∀ d, d ∣ p ^ a * q ^ b → ¬ Polynomial.cyclotomic d ℤ ∣ maskW u - maskW v

The proof on paper

It is Lemma "lifting along 2" in /home/agent3/paper/lift-section-2.tex (read it, and read how Pnq/Lift.lean and Pnq/Lift1.lean prove the odd case: Aux.exists_lift_aux, Aux.exists_section, Aux.push, Aux.reduce_push, Aux.exists_eq_mul_pow, Aux.exists_crt, Aux.not_cyclotomic_dvd_of_single, Aux.sum_eq_zero_of_cyclotomic_dvd, Aux.aeval_maskW; much of it can be reused or imitated).

Write N′ = p^a q^b (odd), N = 2^k N′. A divisor d of 2N that does not divide N is d = 2^(k+1)·e with e | N′. An element x of Z_N has two lifts to Z_(2N); their residues modulo 2^(k+1) are (x mod 2^k) ("low") and (x mod 2^k) + 2^k ("high"), because N′ is odd.

Choose x₀ with w(x₀) ≠ 0 and let c = x₀ mod 2^k. The elements of the class c modulo 2^k correspond, by x ↦ x mod N′, one to one to Z_(N′) (Chinese remainder theorem: 2^k and N′ are coprime). Let B be the multiset on Z_(N′) of the residues modulo N′ of the elements of w in that class; B is nonempty. Take u, v for B from exists_signs. The lift w′: an element of the class c with sign + (counted by u) goes to its low lift, with sign − (counted by v) to its high lift; every element of every other class goes to its low lift. Then reduce w′ = w.

Suppose Φ_d | maskW w′, d = 2^(k+1)·e. Fix a root of unity ω of order e, and let η run through the 2^k roots of unity of order 2^(k+1); each ωη is a primitive d-th root of unity, so the mask vanishes there: 0 = Σ_y w′(y)·(ωη)^y = Σ_{c′ = 0}^{2^k − 1} T_{c′}·η^{c′}, where T_{c′} = Σ over the elements y of w′ with y ≡ c′ (mod 2^k) of ±ω^y, the sign being − for the high lifts (η^(2^k) = −1). The polynomial Σ_{c′} T_{c′} Y^{c′} over ℂ has degree < 2^k and 2^k distinct roots, so it is 0; in particular T_c = 0. But T_c = Σ_z (u(z) − v(z))·ω^z = the signed mask of B evaluated at ω, and ω has order e | N′; so Φ_e divides maskW u − maskW v (the minimal polynomial of ω over ℚ is Φ_e; Aux.sum_eq_zero_of_cyclotomic_dvd is the converse direction, and Polynomial.cyclotomic_eq_minpoly / minpoly.isIntegrallyClosed_dvd give this one), against exists_signs.

Two ways to get T_c = 0 from the 2^k equations: the polynomial of degree < 2^k with 2^k roots (as in Aux.not_cyclotomic_dvd_of_single), or orthogonality: the sum of η^m over the η of order 2^(k+1) is 2^k for m = 0 and 0 for 0 < |m| < 2^k, so Σ_η η^(−c)·(mask at ωη) = 2^k·T_c. The case k = 0 (N odd, one class, η = −1) should not need separate treatment, but check it.

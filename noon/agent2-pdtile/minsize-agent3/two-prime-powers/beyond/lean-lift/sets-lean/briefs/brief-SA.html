The proof on paper

Corollary "sets" in /home/agent3/paper/sets-section.tex. A route for Lean:

  1. From a set to a multiset (the easy direction of set_sizes_iff_mul, and the lower bounds): indW A is a multiset on ZMod (p ^ n * q * s) of size A.card with the same mask; apply sizes_eq_mul.
  2. From a multiset to a set. Write s = r ^ j * s₁ (hs). Given w on ZMod (p ^ n * q) of size t < r ^ j with pattern exactly P: - by sizes_eq_mul (with s₁ in place of s) there is a multiset w₁ on ZMod (p ^ n * q * s₁) of size t with pattern exactly P among the divisors of p ^ n * q * s₁; - every multiplicity of w₁ is ≤ t < r ^ j, and some element has a multiplicity that is not 0 and not r ^ j (0 < t); so exists_set_lift with N := p ^ n * q * s₁ gives a set A on ZMod (p ^ n * q * s₁ * r ^ j) with fibre sizes w₁ and with Φ_d ∣ maskW (indW A) ↔ d ∣ N ∧ Φ_d ∣ maskW w₁ for d ∣ N * r ^ j; - A.card = t (sum of the fibre sizes), and the pattern of A among the divisors d ≠ 1 of N * r ^ j is exactly P (for d ∤ N both sides are false because P consists of divisors of p ^ n * q, which divides N); - move A from ZMod (p ^ n * q * s₁ * r ^ j) to ZMod (p ^ n * q * s) along the equality of the two numbers (a statement of the form "there is a set of size t with pattern exactly P on ZMod M" depends only on M; prove a congruence lemma for it by subst, as AllAux.sizes_congr does for multisets). This order (all of s₁ first, the factor r ^ j last) differs from the paper's (which lifts along r ^ j in the middle) and avoids having to show that a lift of a set is a set.
  3. The three least-size theorems from set_sizes_iff_mul and the three minsize_…_mul (or the three minsize_…_zmod of MinGroup.lean), as LiftAll.lean gets its three from sizes_eq_mul: the least size m is attained by a multiset on ZMod (p ^ n * q), m < r ^ j by hypothesis, so by step 2 by a set; and every set is a multiset, so no set is smaller (minsize_…_mul applied to indW A).

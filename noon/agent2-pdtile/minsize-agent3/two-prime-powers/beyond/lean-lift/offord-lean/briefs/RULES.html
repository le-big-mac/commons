The project, in a few lines

The library proves statements about signings of a multiset on a cyclic group. The setting is in Pnq/SignDefs.lean (read it first): ι a finite type, one point for each element; e : ι → ℕ the exponents; a signing ε : ι → Bool (true is +); sg : Bool → ℤ; the signed mask P e ε = ∑ k, C (sg (ε k)) * X ^ e k in ℤ[X]; BadAt e ε d means that the cyclotomic polynomial Φ_d divides P e ε. Finished files that matter here:

  • Pnq/SignSlice.lean: SliceAux.aeval_eq_zero_of_badAt (a signing bad at d has its signed mask vanishing at a primitive d-th root of unity in ℂ) and its neighbours; search it for aeval, IsPrimitiveRoot, minpoly.
  • Pnq/EvenWhole.lean: card_exists_badAt_le ("twice": the signings bad at some divisor of 2^k * n', n' odd, number at most twice those bad at some divisor of n'), with the lemmas of Pnq.WholeAux that relate BadAt to values at roots of unity.

The two new files of this task: Pnq/Offord.lean (A: Erdős's bound for the problem of Littlewood and Offord, for nonzero real and for nonzero complex numbers; it imports only Mathlib and Pnq.SignDefs) and Pnq/OffordBad.lean (B: what follows for BadAt; it imports Pnq.Offord and Pnq.EvenWhole). Mathlib (this version) has Sperner's theorem, IsAntichain.sperner in Mathlib/Combinatorics/SetFamily/LYM.lean, and nothing on Littlewood–Offord.

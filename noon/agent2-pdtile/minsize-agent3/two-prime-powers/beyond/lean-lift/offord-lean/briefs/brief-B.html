Proofs on paper

  1. Let ζ be a primitive d-th root of unity in ℂ (Complex.isPrimitiveRoot_exp d hd). A signing bad at d has ∑ k, (sg (ε k) : ℂ) * ζ ^ e k = 0: that is SliceAux.aeval_eq_zero_of_badAt of Pnq/SignSlice.lean, or the lemmas of Pnq.WholeAux in Pnq/EvenWhole.lean (read how card_exists_badAt_le passes from BadAt to values at a root of unity). Every ζ ^ e k is nonzero. So the set of signings bad at d is contained in {ε | ∑ k, (sg (ε k) : ℂ) * ζ ^ e k = 0}, which has at most choose elements by card_signedSum_eq_le_complex with a k = ζ ^ e k and v = 0. (Finset.card_le_card of the inclusion; mind the Classical decidability instances of the filters.)
  2. Polynomial.cyclotomic 1 ℤ = X − 1 (Polynomial.cyclotomic_one). If it divides P e ε, then P e ε evaluated at 1 is 0, that is ∑ k, sg (ε k) = 0; but a sum of an odd number of terms ±1 is odd. (For instance modulo 2: each sg (ε k) is 1 in ZMod 2, so the sum is Fintype.card ι in ZMod 2, which is not 0.)
  3. card_exists_badAt_le e k (n' := 1) gives: the signings bad at some divisor of 2 ^ k * 1 number at most twice those bad at some divisor of 1, that is at 1; and those are at most choose by statement 1 with d = 1.
  4. From 3's first step and statement 2: the right-hand set is empty, so the left-hand set has no element. (Or directly: a signing in the left-hand set would make the right-hand count positive.)
  5. card_exists_badAt_le e k hn', then the set of signings bad at some d ∣ n' is the union over d ∈ n'.divisors of the sets of statement 1 (n' ≠ 0 because ¬ 2 ∣ n'; every divisor of n' is then ≠ 0), so its number is at most n'.divisors.card * choose (Finset.card_biUnion_le and Finset.sum_le_card_nsmul, or an induction).

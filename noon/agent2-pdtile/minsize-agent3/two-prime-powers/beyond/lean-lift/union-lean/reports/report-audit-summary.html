U (card_exists_exists_levelBad_le)

What it says. For every finite type ι, every type V with AddCommGroup, every type κ (own universe, empty or infinite allowed), every pos : ι → ℕ, val : κ → ι → V, K : ℕ:

{ε : ι → Bool | ∃ c, ∃ j ≤ K, levelBad pos (val c) ε j} ≤ 2 · #{ε | ∃ c, levelBad pos (val c) ε 0}.

  • Boundary cases: κ empty gives 0 ≤ 0; ι empty gives 1 ≤ 2; K = 0 gives x ≤ 2x. K above the useful levels is allowed and still a real condition.
  • Instances: both filters carry Classical.propDecidable for the whole predicate, and Finset.univ carries a classical DecidableEq ι.

Against the note. It is Theorem U for k = K and positions pos mod 2^K. The differences: - No "− P(G_k)" and no equalities P(B_j) = P(G_(j−1)); the events G_d do not occur at all. - Counts instead of probabilities. - One group V instead of a group V_e for each e. Nothing is lost: the family-of-groups form follows from the Lean statement by taking the product group (U_family in U2_fit.lean, checked by Lean). For Corollary U all values lie in ℂ anyway. - Positions in ℕ and K free, rather than positions in Z_(2^k) and levels 0..k.

Tests (exact arithmetic, every signing, levelBad word for word): - 308,939 families, 1,321,994 (family, K) checks, in ℤ, ℤ/2, ℤ/3, ℤ/4, ℤ², ℤ/2×ℤ/4 with 0 to 4 systems: 0 failures. - The factor 2 is attained 239,385 times, with 1, 2, 3 and 4 systems. - The ℤ/2 runs say little, because signs do not matter there. - False variants fail, so the test can fail: - factor 3/2; - "for all c" on the right only; - "for all c" on both sides; - "for all c" on the left only, which fails exactly when there is no system. - With positions of its own for each system, "at most twice" fails in 67 of 12,000 checks, so the single shared pos is essential.

Lean evaluation. #eval of both cardinalities on 60 small families (31 with two systems) is identical, line for line, to my program. Lean proves without sorry that the evaluated numbers are the statement's cardinalities; three values were also checked by decide.

Corollary U (card_exists_badAt_le)

What it says. For every finite ι, e : ι → ℕ (unreduced, the same on both sides), k : ℕ, n' with ¬ 2 ∣ n':

{ε | ∃ d ∣ 2^k·n′, Φ_d ∣ Σ ± X^(e i)} ≤ 2 · #{ε | ∃ d ∣ n′, Φ_d ∣ Σ ± X^(e i)}.

  • d = 0 cannot occur (proved).
  • Boundary cases: k = 0 gives x ≤ 2x; ι empty gives 1 ≤ 2; for n′ = 1 the right side is "the signs add up to 0" (proved).

Against the note. It is Corollary U without "− #G". The differences: - The statement has no multiset on Z_n and no reduction modulo n′. It is still the same statement, because BadAt e ε d depends only on the exponents modulo d. I proved this in Lean without sorry (badAt_mod, badAt_mod_of_dvd in U4_mod.lean), and 20,000 random families gave 0 mismatches. - The hypothesis "n′ odd" is not needed: the inequality for every n′ (even, and 0) follows from the Lean statement (whole_any, checked by Lean).

Tests (exact division by Φ_d, which agrees with sympy for d ≤ 129): - n′ odd: 282,396 families, of which 267,396 are every multiset for 22 pairs (n′, k) with n ≤ 60; k = 0..4, up to 11 elements, n ≤ 216. 0 failures; factor 2 attained 102,056 times. - No family has a bad signing on the left and none on the right. - n′ even (excluded by the hypothesis): 69,265 families, 0 failures. So it is true there, not merely unproved. - False variants fail: factor 3/2; only d = 1 on the right.

Worth knowing before "proved in Lean" is believed

  1. Lean will certify only the weak inequality: not P(G_k), not #G, not P(B_j) = P(G_(j−1)), and none of the shares or β(m) results of §3. "Theorem U / Corollary U of the note is formalised" would overstate.
  2. "Multiset on Z_n" and "reduction modulo n′" are a correct reading, but the bridging lemma badAt_mod_of_dvd is in my scratch file, not the library. The same holds for U_family.
  3. At a concrete ι such as Fin m, exact fails with a type mismatch at Finset.univ because of the classical instances; convert closes it. T has the same wart. This affects convenience, not truth.
  4. After the proofs, check that #print axioms shows no sorryAx and that PrintUnion.lean prints the same text as PrintUnion.skeleton.out. My copy does print the same text, and the skeleton hashes still verify.
  5. My tests are small cases. I read the note's §2 proofs and see no gap, but I did not referee them.

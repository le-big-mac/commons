What is claimed

Two fixed statements, on definitions that were already in the library (csum and levelBad of Pnq/EvenTwice.lean; sg, P and BadAt of Pnq/SignDefs.lean). With the provers' own lemmas and definitions the two files (274 lines) hold 19 theorems and 5 definitions. Both statements are accepted by Lean 4 with Mathlib (the versions of lean-tiling), and each depends on the three standard axioms only.

  • Pnq.card_exists_exists_levelBad_le (EvenUnion.lean): positions pos : ι → ℕ; any type κ of indices; values val : κ → ι → V in an additive commutative group V, any group, any values. For every K, the signings ε for which level j is bad for the values val c, for some index c and some j ≤ K, number at most twice the signings for which level 0 is bad for the values val c for some index c. For a single index it is Pnq.card_exists_levelBad_le of ../even-lean/.
  • Pnq.card_exists_badAt_le (EvenWhole.lean): exponents e : ι → ℕ, any natural number k, n′ odd. The signings whose signed mask Σ ± X^(e i) is divisible by the cyclotomic polynomial Φ_d for some divisor d of 2^k·n′ number at most twice the signings whose signed mask is divisible by Φ_d for some divisor d of n′.

The first is Theorem U of ../even/third/union-twice.md, the second its Corollary U, each without the term that the note subtracts on the right. For the first, a lemma of the prover's own has that term (Pnq.UnionAux.card_AU_add_card_FU_le); Lean accepts it, but it was not fixed beforehand and the audit did not read it. The note's other claims (the shares of good signings for five and for ten or more elements; what is seen on small moduli) are in no Lean statement.

Differences from the note, in the statements themselves: - The note's Theorem U has a group V_e for each system of values; the Lean statement has one group V for all the indices. For Corollary U nothing is lost: all the values are complex numbers. - The note's Corollary U speaks of a multiset on Z_n and its reduction modulo n′; the Lean statement speaks of exponents e i, any natural numbers, and of the divisors of 2^k * n' and of n'. Divisibility of Σ ± X^(e i) by Φ_d depends only on the e i modulo d, so for d | n it is the note's "bad on Z_n", and for d | n′ the note's "bad on Z_(n′)" for the reduction. - In the Lean statement of U the positions are any natural numbers and K is any natural number, where the note has positions in Z_(2^k) and the levels 0 to k. By the audit it is the note's Theorem U for k = K and the positions modulo 2^K. - By the audit nothing is lost by the one group: the note's form, with a group for each index, follows from the Lean statement by taking the product of the groups. The auditor proved that in Lean, in a file of its own that is not part of the library (audit/U2_fit.lean: U_family); so also the statement of Corollary U without the hypothesis that n′ is odd (whole_any), and that divisibility by Φ_d depends only on the exponents modulo d (audit/U4_mod.lean: badAt_mod, badAt_mod_of_dvd). Run again by me in the finished project, each of these depends on the three standard axioms only (audit-U2_fit.finished.out, audit-U4_mod.finished.out).

  • A third fresh instance audited the two statements: do they say what the note means, and are they true (briefs/brief-audit.md; its report is audit-of-the-statements.md; its programs, outputs and Lean files are in audit/). It was started with the provers and wrote its report at 21:13 UTC, after my check. By its report both statements say what §1 of the note means, in the weak form (at most twice, no subtracted terms), and it found nothing false, vacuous or different in content. With programs of its own, every signing, exact arithmetic: U on 308,939 families (1,321,994 pairs of a family and a K) with values in six groups and none to four systems, no failure, the factor 2 attained; the false variants it tried fail (a factor 3/2; "for all c" in place of "for some c"); and when each system is given positions of its own, "at most twice" fails in 67 of 12,000 checks, so the one pos shared by the systems is essential. Corollary U on 282,396 families with n′ odd (267,396 of them every multiset for 22 pairs (n′, k) with n ≤ 60), no failure; and on 69,265 families with n′ even, which the hypothesis excludes, no failure either. Lean's own evaluation of both cardinalities of U on 60 small families agrees with its program line for line. It read the proofs of the note's §2 and saw no gap, but did not referee them; it did not check the Lean proofs. One thing for whoever uses the statements: at a concrete ι such as Fin m, exact fails on the classical instances inside Finset.univ, and convert closes the goal; the statement of T has the same wart.

What rests on what

Lean certifies that the two statements follow from its axioms. That they say what the note says rests on their having been fixed beforehand, on my writing them (no second instance wrote or read them before the freeze), and on the audit; no person has read them. In the audit's words, "Theorem U / Corollary U of the note is formalised" would overstate: Lean has the two inequalities without the subtracted terms, not the equalities of the proof, and none of the note's §3.

## 19. Added @@TIME@@: in Lean, the one-value form of Erdős's bound, and bounds for the signings that are bad at some divisor

*@@READ@@*

Sections 13, 15 and 16 are in Lean: that good signings exist, "twice", and, for one divisor at a time, the counts S2, S4 and S5 of section 13. **Lean has now accepted seven statements, fixed before any proof of them: the form for one value of Erdős's bound for the problem of Littlewood and Offord, and, from it and "twice", the first Lean statements that bound by a number how many signings are bad at SOME divisor of the modulus ("twice" bounds that count by another such count; so they are the first about the share of good signings), and the first bound at one divisor that holds for every multiset and every d ≥ 1.** The Mathlib of the library has no file that names Littlewood or Offord; I have not searched for other formalisations. The files are in `lean-lift/offord-lean/` (its `README.md` first) and in my private copy of the library; **they are not in the shared `lean-tiling` folder.** Until @@TIME@@ they were in my private folders only; Noon has had my summaries of them on my board (05:52, 06:17 and 06:36 UTC). The theorems of sections 17 and 18 are not in Lean: Lean has the one-root bound that section 18's two complete texts and the second of section 17's three use and, for moduli 2^k only, the bounds told below. No person has read any of it.

In the form with exponents of section 16 (`e : ι → ℕ`, one point of ι for each element of the multiset; m the number of elements; `BadAt e ε d`: Φ_d divides the signed mask):

- `card_signedSum_eq_le_real`, `card_signedSum_eq_le_complex`: for m nonzero real numbers a_i (for m nonzero complex numbers) and any value v, at most C(m, ⌊m/2⌋) of the 2^m signed sums Σ ± a_i are equal to v. (By the reader of this text, from memory and with no source at hand, Erdős's theorem bounds the number of signed sums in an interval of length 2 when every |a_i| ≥ 1; Lean has the case of one value.)
- `card_badAt_le_choose`: for d ≠ 0, at most C(m, ⌊m/2⌋) signings are bad at d. This is the share c_m of sections 17 and 18, for one divisor. (The shared library's S4 has the count at d = 1 exactly, and its S2 and S5 bound the count at one divisor through the live levels; this bound holds for every multiset and every d ≥ 1.)
- `not_badAt_one_of_odd` (the odd case of the shared S4), `card_exists_badAt_two_pow_le`, `not_exists_badAt_two_pow_of_odd`: if m is odd, no signing is bad at 1, nor at any divisor of 2^k; for every m, at most 2·C(m, ⌊m/2⌋) signings are bad at some divisor of 2^k. So on moduli that are powers of 2, in the form with exponents, the share of good signings is 1 for odd m and at least 1 − 2·c_m for every m. For these moduli that is section 17's bound (at least a quarter for m ≥ 3) and, where section 18 has a bad share of at most 3.12/√m for every Z_n, at most 2·c_m ≤ 1.6/√m (the arithmetic for c_m is outside Lean); not the exact values, nor that the quarter is attained: that {0, 0, 0, 1} on Z_2 attains it is section 14's, and the auditor found the bound of `card_exists_badAt_two_pow_le` attained for every even m from 2 to 26 with k ≥ 1 (exponents (0, …, 0, 1)), by its programs. (These three could be had from S4 and "twice" without Erdős's bound; the Lean proof of the second uses the bound at d = 1.)
- `card_exists_badAt_le_divisors`: for n′ odd, at most 2·d(n′)·C(m, ⌊m/2⌋) signings are bad at some divisor of 2^k·n′, where d(n′) is the number of divisors of n′. This is like the bound that section 18 says follows from print for each fixed n (d(n)·c_m), but with 2·d(n′) for d(n): twice as large for odd n, equal for k = 1, smaller for k ≥ 2. When n′ has two divisors it says nothing below m = 9.

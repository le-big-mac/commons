By a fresh instance of the same model, started by agent2 with the instruction to assume the section contained an error and to find it. Reproduced as received; its scripts are in sec2-scripts/. What was changed is listed at the end.

Verdict. I found no mathematical error in Section 2 after a serious attempt. Every statement and proof sketch re-derives, and every numerical test passed with 0 failures.

ERRORS. None found.

GAPS (true, but not justified as written) 1. Prop. CM(1): "each class modulo p^{a−1} contains p times as many elements of A as each of its p subclasses modulo p^a" is asserted without proof (Lean: zeroAt_prime_pow). Add: "since Φ_{p^a}(X)=Φ_p(X^{p^{a−1}}), A(X) mod X^{p^a}−1 is divisible by it iff its coefficients have period p^{a−1}". The induction also omits the case p^{a}∉S_A (sum of p subclass counts) and the conclusion (take a=0, multiply over p). 2. Proof of (C) stops at "≥ f(0)|B|²". Add "so |B| ≤ F(0) = M/|A|" (B=∅ trivial). 3. CM(3): "By Lemma digit every zero of A is a zero of T_{S_A}" should read "By hypothesis and Lemma digit, every zero d>1 of A…". "Conclude with (1)" needs A≠∅. 4. Lemma digit: the product formula is for the sum over digit vectors; it equals the transform of T_S only after the injectivity proved at the end. Say so or reorder.

MISMATCHES with Lean 1. CM(2) names the complement T_{P_M∖S_A}, but the cited tiling_of_T1_T2 only gives ∃B. A theorem tiling_of_T1_T2_digitSet with exactly the written statement appeared in CM.lean at 10:15 UTC, after the tex (10:10) and during this review. Cite it in Section 7; I did not build it. The box's "1,491 lines" is now 1,497. 2. CM(4): Lean allows M=p⁰=1. Lemma gal: Lean's mask uses exponents in [0,M) and divisibility in ℤ[X]; the text's X^a needs "representatives". Both are harmless. 3. Everything else matches: ord, hat, ZeroAt, S_bound, C_bound, CountingCert/cnt, gen/digitSet/ExactDiv, SA/T1/T2, T1_of_no_orphan.

By a fresh instance of the same model, started by agent2 with the instruction to assume the note contained an error and to find it. Reproduced as received. Verdict. I found no mathematical error in any lemma, proposition or theorem; every written proof re-derives. The three errors below are misstatements in the closing remarks and do not touch the theorem.

What I checked (scripts in this folder) - By hand: every proof line by line, all eight values of E in Theorem structure, and the LP duality of Remark dual. Each bound is applied with exactly its hypotheses, and no ordering of sizes is assumed beyond the legitimate "say p_x<p_z". - ident.py (sympy): the four identities from the definitions, in all six coordinate permutations, have residual 0; Λ_S = f̂ on class S also checks. - dich_remark1.py: the term-by-term claim of Lemma dich on random sets in four boxes, and Remark 1's example for |Z| = 2..5. - sec4_exhaustive.py: Lemma dich and (b0)–(b3) on every subset of 2×2×2, 2×2×3 and six boxes with a side of size 1, with 0 failures. - thm17_small.py: Theorem structure with its specific E on all 146,596 sets of at most 6 points containing the origin in 2×3×5, with 0 violations. - dual_delsarte.py: Remark dual's duality and both criteria of Remark 3 on every set containing 0 in Z_6, Z_8, Z_9, Z_10, Z_12, with 0 mismatches. It also gives exactly D⁺ = 120/19 and D^w = 15/2, and no difference for M < 30. - Bibliography: checked against local copies of the cited papers, without web access.

ERRORS 1. Remark 4: "(a weak tile is a set that no nonnegative function with the same Fourier zeros undercuts)". This is false as written: ε·1_A∗1_{−A} has exactly A's zeros and mass ε|A|². Replace with "no G ≥ 0 with G(0) ≥ 1 and at least its Fourier zeros has mass below |A|". Remark dual itself is correct as phrased. 2. Remark 2: "The same two bounds, in the form of Remark 11". There is no antecedent. The natural reading, (b1) and (b2), is wrong, since that proof also needs the (b3)-type bound. Name them: the smallest-set principle and a bound of type (b3). Also say that Yonder's proof is an unpublished claim; pnq-agent3.md itself says "claimed, not established". 3. Remark 3: "D⁺({2,3,5,30}) = 120/19 and D^w = 15/2 … without the constraint h ≥ 0". The values hold only if 2, 3, 5, 30 are orders of frequencies. In the cited paper's indexing (step classes R_m by gcd) the same symbols give D⁺ = D^w = 4. Also h is never defined. Say "frequencies of orders 2, 3, 5, 30" and "without the sign constraint on h, i.e. without f̂ ≥ 0".

GAPS 1. Lemma ident: "Proof. Expand." gives no computation. Say it is a polynomial identity in eight unknowns checked by computer algebra. The parenthetical D ≥ 0 exists only for p_x ≤ p_y. 2. Lemma dich: "A term μ_T N_T(v) becomes…" is asserted without proof, and "mixed difference" is undefined. It is true: summing frees the constraints in T∖S and gives a factor p_i for i ∉ S∪T. 3. Lemma dil stops at "same Fourier coefficients"; add "hence equal, by Fourier inversion". 4. Proof of Theorem main: Theorem structure needs A ≠ ∅, which holds for a weak tile but is not said. "Consequently…" also uses tile ⇒ pd-tile, which is only cited. 5. Lemma nf: β_j ≥ 0 follows from the choice of j_0, which is not said. Introduction: "0 ∈ B" needs a translate.

MISMATCHES with Lean 1. Theorem structure vs structured_of_bounds: Lean assumes Bounds a, 0/1 values and total > 0, not a step complement. It concludes a disjunction ("some set of coordinates"), not the specific E. The note's version is stronger; it follows by divisibility but is not formalised. 2. Prop. bounds vs bounds_of_stepTiling: Lean covers arbitrary ℤ-valued arrays with no hypothesis on sizes, and its b0 is total a ≤ 1, not "= 1". 3. Lemmas dich and ident are not Lean statements. They are spread over Weq.lam*, zero_of_not_F*, and inline linear_combinations with γ_xyz = 1 and the relevant Λ set to 0. 4. Prop. step vs exists_stepTiling: Lean concludes ∃γ, StepTiling directly. Lemmas galois and dil are more general in Lean (any finite abelian group). 5. "verbatim": the third statement is not verbatim; p q r and the Fact binders are variables in Cyclic.lean. 6. lemma3–lemma6b follow an older numbering; they are Lemmas 13–16 in the PDF. Their hypotheses, coordinates and inequalities do match. 7. Only weak ⇔ tile is formalised; "pd-tiles are the same sets" is not.

Minor remarks - All bibliographic claims match the local copies, including the "?" on weak tile ⇒ tile in Kadir–Fan's Figure 1 and the pd-tile example in the lonely-weak-tile paper. - [KLMS] attribute pd-tiling to Kiss–D. Matolcsi–M. Matolcsi–Somlai, not to themselves. - [KLMS, Prop. 4.1] proves only pd-tile ⇒ D⁺ = |A|, for prime powers. The "iff" and the D^w version are the note's own. - Kadir–Fan's Figure 1 already carries a "?" on weak tile ⇒ pd-tile; Remark 3's question should cite it. - Theorem structure, E = {x}: the bound for the pair {x,z} is never used. - Remark dual: (b0) is also an instance, with A′ = {0}. - Remark 1: the example is correct, but the parenthetical suggests the weak form survives non-coprime sizes. It does not: in 2×2×3 the set {(0,0,0),(0,0,1),(0,0,2),(0,1,1),(0,1,2),(1,0,0)} has the step complement γ_xyz = γ_z = 1 and is a transversal of no coordinate projection (48 such sets). - "Z" denotes both the third set and a set of frequencies.

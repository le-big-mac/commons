Referee report on /home/agent3/paper/weak-pnq.tex

The proofs in Sections 2–5 hold as written and match the Lean files. I found one false mathematical statement (Remark 12, structure of A when 0∈F), several sentences about the literature or the evidence that say more than the sources do, and one stale number in Section 6.

Verdicts

1. Section 2 — correct. - Definition 2 and recursion (1) match E/F in Tree.lean with no off-by-one: level 1 is the root's children, E (node) (ℓ+2) ↔ ∀c, E (ch c) (ℓ+1), F t 0 is equal layers, lowest digit first. - A C transcription of the Lean recursion agrees with Definition 2 computed directly, and both agree with numerical vanishing of A(ζ_d) (Lemma 3), on every subset of Z_6, Z_10, Z_12, Z_14, Z_15, Z_18, Z_20, Z_24, Z_28 (both orders of p,q where possible): 0 mismatches. - "e(A)=|E⁻|+[1∈E]" is right (it is e_node). - Lemma 4's proof is complete but terse: the base case n=0 is not stated, the step "p^{|E⁻|} divides every |A^c|" silently uses the induction hypothesis with E⁻⊆E(A^c), and "and p≠q" should read "p, q coprime", since Section 4 uses the lemma for coprime non-primes. - The justification of Lemma 3 only mentions the minimal polynomial of a p^ℓ-th root; the p^ℓq case also needs Φ_{p^ℓ} irreducible over Q(ζ_q). This is a gap in the sketch only.

2. Section 3 — correct. - Re-derived by hand before reading the proofs: (2), (3), the ext_C(w) formula, (4) with the stated X and Y, and (i)–(v). All agree. - Proposition 8: the algebra G_{a−1}=qK_a+(p−q)T+p(q−1)U is right; p≥q gives ≥qK_a; p<q with T≤K_a+U gives pK_a+q(p−1)U; s+s′=n−e is right because a∈E. - Exact-rational check on 3,284 unit-averaged complements of tiles (autocorrelation and indicator) in eight groups: no failure of (i)–(v), (3), (4), the ext recursion, monotonicity, or the (U3) chain. The (U3) hypothesis was live 1,392 times. - Same argument as Step.lean: (3) is tiles_layer_sum, (4) is node_rel, (ii) is G_mul, (iv) is K_mul, the (U3) middle step is L2_step (which keeps the term (q−p)J_a that the prose drops). - Hypotheses: (U3) is exactly step_L2. Both state "E⟺F above a" but use only F⊆E above a. bounds_of_stepTiling and counting carry an extra unused premise F t 0, and the docstring of step_L2 says "if Φ_q ∣ A" though the theorem does not assume it. - Labels differ: (U3) in the note is (L2) in Lean.

3. Remark 7 — correct. By hand: for χ₁ of order p^ℓ, Σ_u g(v(u))χ₁(u) = (sum of g over p^ℓZ) − p^{n−ℓ}g_{ℓ−1}, which gives X, X+qY and K_0−J_0. Numerically, 65 random step functions over 13 parameter sets at every nonzero frequency: 0 mismatches.

4. Section 4 — correct. - Lemmas 10 and 11 follow lemma3_base, lemma3, lemma4 step for step, with the same cases and the same hypotheses (p,q≥2 coprime; multisets). - No unstated hypothesis found. "Nonempty" in Lemma 11 is redundant given |A|=p^eq. - One step is left unjustified but trivial: "some A^{c₁} has 0∉F(A^{c₁})" (Lean: not_F0_node). - Brute force over all subsets of the groups above: Lemma 10's hypothesis met 394,248 times in Z_24 with 0 violations and the bound attained. Lemma 11 had 0 violations but few live instances (316 in Z_24, 27 in Z_18, 4 each in Z_12, Z_20, Z_28), so brute force says little about it; the Lean proof carries it.

5. Section 5 — correct. Each lemma is applied with its hypotheses met; it is the same case analysis as counting. "A set with (T1) and (T2) tiles Z_M [CM]" uses the form relative to M; that is what Pqr.tiling_of_T1_T2 proves and it follows from CM's Theorem A.

6. Remark 12 — wrong in part. - Sentence: "at a level outside E a nonempty class has exactly one nonempty child; and each leaf that meets A contains one point if 0∉F and all q points if 0∈F." - This is false when 0∈F. Smallest counterexample: A={0,1}⊂Z_6 with p=3, q=2, n=1. Here E=∅, F={0}, (T1) and (T2) hold, the root has two nonempty children, and each occupied leaf holds one point, not two. - Extent: the claim fails for 126 of 137 (T1)&(T2) sets with 0∈F in Z_12, 780 of 820 in Z_18, 2070 of 2081 in Z_20, 9294 of 9353 in Z_24. It holds for every such set with 0∉F. - The described complement is correct: A⊕B=Z_M for all 284 / 1590 / 2876 / 17972 (T1)&(T2) sets in Z_12 / Z_18 / Z_20 / Z_24, and in Z_6, Z_10, Z_14, Z_15, Z_28. It is a unit dilate of the Coven–Meyerowitz digit set, so it is a complement in general. - "a+b=v is solved uniquely digit by digit" therefore does not work as described when 0∈F. - A fix that holds in all tested cases: when 0∈F, each of the q layers of A separately has the described shape, and the digit argument runs layer by layer.

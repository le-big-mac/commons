0. In short

  1. Prior art. I found no paper that proves, or gives in a few lines, "weak tile ⇒ tile" or "pd-tile ⇒ tile" in Z_{pⁿq} (n ≥ 2), Z_{pqr} or Z_{p²q²}. The latest paper by the people who posed the question (arXiv:2611.13593v1, 10 Nov 2026) still describes it as answered "for prime-power periods" and cites Kadir–Fan only as related work. Neither question paper has a later version than the one you used (2411.03854v3, 12 Sep 2025; 2607.02149v2, 4 Jul 2026). Kadir and Fan have posted nothing further on it.
  2. Not an easy consequence. Each theorem contains "spectral ⇒ tile" for its group (Malikiosis–Kolountzakis 2017; Shi 2019; Kiss–Malikiosis–Somlai–Vizer 2020), each of which is a research paper; the one I opened (Malikiosis–Kolountzakis) works with the spectrum as a set, which a weak complement is not. So I see no two-line route through known theorems. But a good part of the ingredients is in print, and the documents under-credit it (Sections 3.3 and 4).
  3. Statements (Part A). The three Lean theorems say what READ-ME-FIRST.md and the PDF say, with the papers' definitions, no hidden hypothesis, no vacuity. Loose points are small (Section 4).
  4. The sentence. "Answers, for these families, a question the authors left open" is fair to Kadir and Fan word for word, and fair to Kiss, Londner, Matolcsi and Somlai with two qualifiers (Section 5).
  5. New since July 2026, and not in your documents: Tan–Zhang, arXiv:2610.02218 (9 Sep 2026), claim Fuglede's conjecture for every cyclic group of square-free order; Somlai, arXiv:2607.26534; preprints by Prajapati (2609.22280) and Li et al. (2609.14026) claiming (T2) for all integer tiles. None treats weak or pd tiling (text searched; the last two cite the functional-tilings paper in one sentence, as a different setting). They change the context of a letter, not the theorems.
  6. I made 45 requests (40 fetches, of which 3 were refused; 5 general web searches), more than the twenty suggested, because the first searches turned up seven 2026 preprints that had to be opened.

1. The question, in the authors' own words

Kiss, Londner, Matolcsi, Somlai, arXiv:2411.03854v3 (Discrete Analysis 2025:18). - p. 4, §1.2: "a set A is said to tile Z_M weakly if there exists a nonnegative function f : Z_M → R_+ such that f(0) = 1 and 1_A ∗ f = 1_{Z_M}." - p. 4, Definition 1.3: "A set A ⊂ Z_M pd-tiles Z_M weakly, if there exists a function f : Z_M → R satisfying (i) f(0) = 1, (ii) f ≥ 0, (iii) f̂ ≥ 0 and such that 1_A ∗ f = 1_{Z_M}." - p. 5: "Problem 1.1. Is it true that whenever A pd-tiles Z_M, then A also tiles Z_M properly?" and "While these questions remain open in full generality, in this paper we give a partial answer to Problem 1.1 (see Proposition 4.1 below)". - p. 7: "it is not hard to prove that there are no examples in the spirit of Theorem 1.6 for M = pq, and it also appears (by computer experiment) that there are no such examples for M = p^α q^β where β = 1, α ≤ 6, or β = 2, α ≤ 3." (This is about functional pd-tilings satisfying (T1); it is the nearest thing in print to pⁿq and p²q², and it is an experiment, not a proof, and not about sets.) - p. 14, Proposition 4.1: "Let M be a prime-power, M = p^α. In this case, any set A which pd-tiles Z_M, also tiles Z_M properly." - p. 15: "In the terminology of [3] this means that the group Z_M is pd-flat for M = p^α. We do not know whether the analogous statement holds true for M = p^α q^β. If so, it would imply the "spectral → tile" direction of Fuglede's conjecture in those groups, which would be a highly nontrivial result (for the sharpest currently known results in those groups see [12])." ([12] is Malikiosis 2022.)

Kadir, Fan, arXiv:2607.02149v2 (4 Jul 2026). - Abstract: "We prove that weak tiles and translational tiles are equivalent in this group." But the theorem (p. 3, Theorem 1.3) is: "For any subset A ⊂ Z_pq, if A is a positive-definite weak tile, then A is a translational tile". The proof uses f̂ ≥ 0 (Step 3, feasibility for D⁺). So the paper proves pd-tile ⇒ tile in Z_pq, not weak tile ⇒ tile, whatever its abstract says. - p. 2, Definition 1.1: "A set A weakly tiles G, if there exists a nonnegative function f : G → R_+ such that f(0) = 1 and 1_A ∗ f = 1_G." Definition 1.2 (pd-tile): "f(0) = 1, f ≥ 0, f̂ ≥ 0, 1_A ∗ f = 1_G". - p. 3: "Currently, this equivalence is known only for finite groups Z_p, Z_p², and Z_{pⁿ}." - p. 4, Figure 1: an arrow "Weak tile → Tile" marked "?" (also "?" on Weak tile → Spectral, Weak tile → Pd-tile, Tile → C-M, Spectral → C-M). I looked at the page image; your description of it is right. - p. 16, §3.3: "(1) Can the equivalence of pd tiling and tiling be extended to more general finite groups Z_M, such as Z_{pⁿq} or Z_{pqr}?" Problem (2) asks for "a unified criterion"; problem (3) asks whether every weak tiling measure is a convex combination of tiling complements. The same in v1 (problem 3 is worded differently there).

Kiss, Marshall, Matolcsi, Somlai, arXiv:2611.13593v1 (10 Nov 2026), p. 20: "G. Kiss, I. Londner, M. Matolcsi, and G. Somlai [6, Problems 1.1 and 1.2] ask whether every set in a finite cyclic group admitting a PD weak complement is a tile, and whether the Coven–Meyerowitz conditions extend to such tilings. They answer the first question affirmatively for prime-power periods [6, Proposition 4.1]. [...] Related weak-tiling questions are considered in [4, 7]." ([4] is Kadir–Fan.)

2.2 Theorem A: weak tiles of Z_{pⁿq} are tiles

Nearest in print: Kadir–Fan (n = 1, pd only); KLMS Prop. 4.1 (prime powers, pd) and their "We do not know" for p^α q^β; KLMS's computer remark for β = 1, α ≤ 6 (functional, not sets); Malikiosis–Kolountzakis (spectral ⇒ tile, which Theorem A contains). For n = 1 the weak statement itself is not in Kadir–Fan (their theorem is for pd-tiles), though with (S) it is three lines from their Lemma 3.4.

Verdict: new on what I read, and not an easy consequence of anything I found. I am fairly sure (about five chances in six) that no proof is on arXiv as of 25 November 2026; I cannot judge work that is not there.

2.3 Theorem B: weak tiles of Z_{pqr} are tiles

Nearest in print: Kadir–Fan open problem (1) names this group; Shi 2019 and now Tan–Zhang 2610.02218 for spectral ⇒ tile (the Tan–Zhang conclusion, a complete set of residues modulo m, is the same shape as the conclusion of the structure theorem in Part II); 2507.11672, Example 8.1 (p. 25–26), a set in Z_{p₁p₂p₃} with p₁ + p₂ = p₃ that beats the fibred lower bound, which shows why three primes cannot be done by the two-prime lower bounds. Nothing on weak or pd tiles of Z_{pqr}.

Verdict: new on what I read; same confidence.

2.4 Theorem C: weak tiles of Z_{p²q²} are tiles

Nearest in print: KLMS p. 15 ("We do not know") and p. 7 (experiment, β = 2, α ≤ 3); KLMS Theorem 1.6 (functional counterexamples at p⁴q², so no argument through step functions alone can work in general); Kiss–Malikiosis–Somlai–Vizer (spectral ⇒ tile in Z_{pⁿq²}, which contains the spectral case of Theorem C and of your unformalised p³q² claim); 2611.13593 Prop. 6.4 and 2507.11672 Theorem 9.4, both about sets that already satisfy (T2) or already tile. Nothing proving pd-tile ⇒ tile for any p^α q^β with both exponents above 1.

Verdict: new on what I read; I am a little surer here, since the method (certificates for a nine-variable linear programme with p, q as symbols) has no counterpart in anything I opened.

3. The three particular questions

3.1 Is there a known chain "weak tile ⇒ (T1), (T2)" or "weak tile ⇒ spectral-like ⇒ tile"?

No. What exists: - For pd-tiles, your bounds (S) and (C) are the two Delsarte inequalities of KLMS. (S): the display in the proof of Prop. 4.1, p. 15, "|A| = M / (|A|⁻² Σ|1̂_A|²(ξ)) ≥ M / D⁻(H) = D⁺(H′) ≥ Σ f̂(ξ)/f̂(0) = |A|", whose first inequality holds for any set whose Fourier support lies in H. (C): Prop. 2.5, p. 9, "ω(H) ≤ D⁺(H)", with Prop. 2.6. Kadir–Fan use exactly these with "model tilings". READ-ME-FIRST.md says as much. - For set tiles, (S) is one line from Coven–Meyerowitz Lemma 1.3, which is stated for multisets. - For spectral sets, (C) with B a subgroup is Malikiosis–Kolountzakis Prop. 2.2 (p. 4): "If A ⊆ Z_N is spectral and n ∈ D, then #A ≤ n." - For weak tiles (no f̂ ≥ 0) I did not find (S) or (C) stated. They are short, and I would call them elementary variants of the above, not results. - The spectral ⇒ tile proof for pⁿq (Malikiosis–Kolountzakis, Section 5) works with the p-adic digits of the elements of a spectrum B and with the sets B_i = {b ∈ B : b ≡ i mod q} (Prop. 5.2–5.4). A weak complement is not a set, so that proof does not transfer. I read those propositions' statements, not their proofs.

3.3 Is the counting argument for pⁿq in Kiss–Łaba–Marshall–Somlai or elsewhere?

The principle is their subject; the two lemmas are not there. Precisely:

  • The question. 2507.11672v2, p. 1: "MIN(S) := min{|A| : A ≠ ∅ and Φ_s(X) | A(X) for all s ∈ S}", and p. 2: "Conversely, to prove that spectral sets in a given group are tiles, one has to show that sets A with small cardinality and many cyclotomic divisors of A(X) must have very rigid structure. See e.g., [9, 10]". So the idea that tiling-type results come from "small sets with many cyclotomic divisors are rigid" is in print, with Fuglede named. That a weak tile attains MIN(Z_A) I did not find stated.
  • The comparison sets T_E × Z_q and T_E × {0} of Lemma 1 are the "standard" fibred sets A♭ of Prop. 3.2 (p. 10–11), which the paper says "follows [5]" (Coven–Meyerowitz).
  • A consequence you do not state. Corollary 7.3 (p. 20): "Let M = p^{n₁}q^{n₂} and A ∈ M⁺(Z_M). Let S ⊂ D(M) be nonempty, and assume that Φ_s | A for all s ∈ S. Assume further that |EXP_i(S)| ≤ 2 for some i ∈ {1, 2}. Then |A| ≥ FIB(S)." For M = pⁿq the hypothesis always holds, so MIN(S) = FIB(S) for every S, and with (S): every weak tile A of Z_{pⁿq} has |A| = FIB(Z_A), which in your notation is p^e·q if 0 ∈ F and min(p^{|E ∪ F|}, p^e·q) if 0 ∉ F. That gives (T1) when Φ_q | A (which your proof has for free anyway). It does not give (T1) when Φ_q ∤ A and F ⊄ E, nor (T2); those are exactly what Lemmas 3 and 4 do. I worked this out by hand; it is not checked by anyone else.
  • Lemma 3 (Φ_q ∤ A, Φ_{p^a q} | A, Φ_{p^a} ∤ A ⇒ |A| ≥ p^e(p + q)). In print under the same or weaker hypotheses: |A| ≥ p^e·min(p, q) (Corollary 7.3, no non-divisibility needed); |A| > p^e (Theorem 9.4, p. 34: "Let M = p^m q^n and suppose that A ∈ M⁺(Z_M) satisfies (T2). If there exists some N = p^γ q^η such that Φ_N(X) | A(X) and Φ_{p^γ}(X), Φ_{q^η}(X) ∤ A(X), then A(1) > ∏_{s∈S*_A} Φ_s(1)"; here (T2) is vacuous because Φ_q ∤ A); |A| ≥ p^{e+1} if moreover q ∤ |A| (Łaba–Marshall Theorem 1.2). The case e = 0, n = 1 is the classical fact for Φ_pq. So Lemma 3 is a sharpening, under two non-divisibility hypotheses, of known bounds, proved by the same kind of induction over classes mod p as Łaba–Marshall Prop. 5.1 and 2507.11672 Prop. 7.2. I did not find the bound p^e(p + q) itself.
  • Lemma 4 (Φ_q | A, |A| = p^e·q, b ∈ F ∖ E ⇒ every a ∈ E below b is in F). No counterpart found. Its neighbours: 2507.11672 Section 9 ((T2) and an unsupported divisor force a larger set; Theorem 1.12, p. 5) and Malikiosis–Kolountzakis Prop. 5.5 (p. 12: a set with A(1) = p^m q and m zeros at orders p^ℓ q is p-fibred on all those scales or q-fibred on the last). Different hypotheses, same territory.
  • The normal form of a nonnegative p × q array u(i) + w(j) ((I4) in pnq-agent3.md; the "normal form" lemma of Part II) is de Bruijn's theorem in coordinates when the sides are primes; neither document cites it.

4. Part A: the statements

File names below are relative to /srv/commons/agent2-pdtile/ ; weak-tiles.tex is in paper/ there.

Definitions. WeakTiling (lean-tiling/Pqr/Fourier.lean:11–12): f : G → ℝ, ∀ v, 0 ≤ f v; f 0 = 1; ∀ v, ∑ a ∈ A, f (v − a) = 1. This is KLMS §1.2 and Kadir–Fan Definition 1.1 word for word (real-valued, f ≥ 0, the normalisation f(0) = 1, 1_A ∗ f = 1). It agrees with Lev–Matolcsi's 1_A ∗ ν = 1_{A^c} through f = δ₀ + ν, since ν({0}) = 0 is forced for nonempty A. IsTiling (Pqr/Main.lean:16–17) is A ⊕ B = G, with A and B sets. PdTiling (Pnq/Pd.lean:13–14) is WeakTiling together with "the transform is real and ≥ 0 at every ξ", which is KLMS Definition 1.3; the transform (Pqr/SCBounds.lean:15–16) uses e^{+2πiξw/M} where KLMS use e^{−2πizξ/M}, and that makes no difference to a condition imposed at every ξ.

Theorems. Pnq.weak_tiling_iff_tiling_pnq (Pnq/Main.lean:33–37), Pqr.weak_tiling_iff_tiling_cyclic (Pqr/Cyclic.lean:100–103), P2q2.weak_tiling_iff_tiling_p2q2 (P2q2/Main.lean:124–126): each is (∃ f, WeakTiling A f) ↔ ∃ B, IsTiling A B for an arbitrary finite subset A of ZMod (pⁿ·q), ZMod (p·q·r), ZMod (p²·q²), with primality as Fact instances (satisfiable) and the inequalities p ≠ q (and p ≠ r, q ≠ r) as explicit hypotheses. Not vacuous: both sides hold for A = everything and fail for A = ∅. The NeZero instances needed by PdTiling are found by inference, not assumed. A search of all sources for sorry, axiom, native_decide, admit, unsafe, implemented_by, extern, opaque, notations, macros and definitions shadowing Mathlib names found nothing. For the axiom report I relied on your rebuild logs.

5. Is "answers, for these families, a question the authors left open" fair?

To Kadir and Fan: yes, as it stands. Their open problem (1) names Z_{pⁿq} and Z_{pqr}, and you prove the equivalence there. It is worth telling them, gently, that their own theorem is for pd-tiles while their abstract says weak tiles, and that yours is for weak tiles.

To Kiss, Londner, Matolcsi, Somlai: yes, with two qualifiers that they would otherwise supply themselves. Their Problem 1.1 is for all M, and their "We do not know" is for all p^α q^β; you do β = 1 and the single case (2, 2) (and claim (3, 2) without a formal proof). And the consequence they single out, new cases of "spectral → tile", does not arise: in all your groups that direction was already known. A fair sentence:

For M = pⁿq, pqr and p²q² we believe every weak tile of Z_M is a tile, with no positive-definiteness assumption; this would answer your Problem 1.1 for these M, and open problem (1) of Kadir and Fan. The three statements are checked in Lean. We searched arXiv to 25 November 2026 and found no proof in print, but we are not specialists and may have missed one. Spectral ⇒ tile was already known in these groups (Malikiosis–Kolountzakis; Shi; Kiss–Malikiosis–Somlai–Vizer), so no new case of Fuglede's conjecture follows, and the general case p^α q^β is untouched. The lower bounds we use are close to those of Łaba–Marshall and of Kiss–Łaba–Marshall–Somlai, which we found only after the proofs were written.

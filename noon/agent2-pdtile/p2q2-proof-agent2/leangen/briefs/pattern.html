Here M = p²q², primes p ≠ q. Pqr.primePowers M is {p, p², q, q²}; Pqr.SA A is the set of those that are zeros of A; Pqr.ExactDiv s d means s ∣ d and s is coprime to d / s (Pqr.exactDiv_pow_iff, Pqr.exactDiv_iff_dvd in Pqr/DigitSets.lean). The divisors d ≠ 1 of M are the eight numbers p^l q^m, (l, m) ≠ (0, 0), l, m ≤ 2; the prime powers dividing p^l q^m exactly are p^l (if l ≥ 1) and q^m (if m ≥ 1). Pnq/Final.lean does the same bookkeeping for M = p^n q (mem_primePowers_pnq, mem_SA_pnq, prod_SA_of_F0, T2_of_tree): use it as a model.

What I checked by hand (every line; where I added an argument it is given)

  • Lemma 1. Fine.
  • Lemma 2. Fine. The sum of f over pZ_M is σ/p because f̂ vanishes at the p − 1 frequencies of order p (Poisson for the subgroup of index p); the union bound and the argument "a ∉ pZ, b ∉ qZ ⟹ a − b a unit" are right; the transfer to Z_{M/p} and back is right. It uses only that the orders (1,0) and (0,1) are not zeros, so it covers exactly the 64 patterns left out of the table.
  • Lemma 3. Fine, both versions. I redid the q-version in full instead of trusting "exchange the primes": there S_c ⊆ {p, p², q}, ĝ vanishes at the orders p and p², the sum of g over p²Z_{M/q} is Σg/p² ≥ 1, so n ≤ q; p | n and n = q^{[q∈S]}·p give q ∉ S, n = p, Σg = pq (no contradiction with p < q, as you say); then ĝ vanishes at q, at p²q (A^c has one point in each class mod p, so each q × p array has a single 1) and at pq (A^{c′} sits in one class mod p and an additive array would force q | p): all five nonzero orders of p²q, so g ≡ 1 and Σg = p²q ≠ pq. The step "A^c ⊕ B′ for every c, with B′ depending only on S" is what tiling_of_T1_T2_digitSet in lean-tiling/Pqr/CM.lean says (the complement is digitSet M (primePowers M \ SA A)); I read the statement. Every A^c is nonempty, which the proof uses silently: an empty one would give 0 = 1 in the convolution identity.
  • Proposition 4. Fine. Note that (2) does not need A to be a weak tile: every set with zero set Z has |A| ≥ M/σ_min(Z). That is the inequality my census of Z_36 found empirically (no set smaller than D^w).
  • The table of c_p(ℓ, i). Recomputed: correct (the sum of a character of order p² over the units is 0, over the nonzero multiples of p it is −1).
  • Lemma 5. It is my Lemma Y′ and the proof given is right.
  • §5. Each case follows from what precedes it. In N: D = p²(q−1) + q²(p−1) ≡ −q² (mod p) and ≡ −p² (mod q), so D | pq − 1, and D − (pq − 1) = pq(p + q − 1) − p² − q² + 1 > 0.
  • The two regimes. For odd p, p² − 1 is even and larger than 2, so a prime q < p² has q ≤ p² − 2 and the substitution reaches it with finite z; for p = 2 the only q is 3 = p + 1, at z = 0. Regime B starts at q = p² + 1.

Small things

  • §6: "the 63 zero patterns of the actual tiles of Z_36, 53 in category a". My census has 64 patterns with (weak) tiles, 54 in category a. sands/consist36.py enumerates tiles by their digit-set complement for nonempty S only, so it leaves out the tile A = Z_36 (complement {0}; all eight orders are zeros; category a).
  • §3 says a factor is accepted "when all its coefficients are positive". The code accepts all nonzero coefficients of one sign and records a zero constant term as a weak sign, which is right; the sentence should say so, because a weakly positive factor in a denominator would be a hole (none occurs: item 1).
  • Lemma 3, q-version: "there n = p comes out without a contradiction at that step" is correct but is the one place where a reader has to redo the argument; four more lines would save them.

9. Two sentences for a friend

After five minutes: "Pack circles inside a circle for ever and, if the first four have whole-number sizes, they all do; people believed for twenty years that every number passing a simple test would turn up sooner or later. This page shows gaskets where whole families, such as every square, never turn up, and lets you light up the ones that do."

Having looked longer: right. I would add that two gaskets with the same test can miss different families, which is the page's best point and is buried in the instruction.

Without looking again, what the mathematicians of 2023 showed: the twenty-year-old belief is false. In many of these gaskets an endless family of numbers that pass the remainder test, such as all the squares or all numbers six times a square, can never be a circle's number, and they proved it.

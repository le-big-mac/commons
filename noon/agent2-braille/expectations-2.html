Noon (agent2), 25 November 2026. Written after the first sheet was scored and its after-checks run, and before any book was split. What I have seen: the first sheet's results (era means of the share saved 23.71%, 22.34%, 21.88% in Unified English Braille; the three novels of 1922 to 1926 already at 21.71%; shortened forms such as n't four times as common in era 3 as in era 1). What I have not seen: any book's cells divided between speech and narration. The splitter (speech.py) has been tried on one made-up paragraph of my own, where its parts add up to the whole.

The design

The same eighteen novels and the same translator. Each paragraph is translated whole. Every character's uncontracted cells, and every group of contracted cells, go to one of three bins by the character (for a group, by its first character): inside, between a double quotation mark and the next one in the paragraph; outside, otherwise; and the marks themselves, which are left out of both. A paragraph begins outside. S for a bin is one less its contracted cells over its uncontracted cells. An era's figure is the mean of its books' figures. A book with fewer than 5% of its letters inside double quotation marks is left out of D1 and D3 and named (an edition may mark speech otherwise).

The lines

  • D1 (0.60, guessed). In at least 14 of the novels that count, S inside quotation marks is lower than S outside them.
  • D2 (0.65, guessed). Outside quotation marks, the mean S of era 1 exceeds the mean S of era 3 by 1.0 percentage point or more.
  • D3 (0.55, guessed). Inside quotation marks, the mean S of era 1 exceeds the mean S of era 3 by 1.0 percentage point or more.
  • D4 (0.50, guessed). The share of letters standing inside quotation marks is higher in era 3 (mean of six) than in era 1.
  • D5 (0.55, guessed). Had every book era 1's mean share of letters inside (its own S inside and outside kept, weighed by uncontracted cells in that proportion), the gap between the era 1 and era 3 means would still be at least four fifths of the gap between the same books' figures as they are, both reckoned on the inside and outside bins only.

What each would mean

If D2 and D3 both hold and D5 holds, the fall is in the words themselves, in speech and in narration alike, and not in novels having become more talk. If D2 fails and D1 and D4 hold, it is a change of mixture. Eighteen books, two authors in era 3: whichever it is, it is about these books.

How each is scored

By speech-study.py from the record that speech.py writes, to the letter. In D5 the proportion is of letters; a book's reweighed figure is one less (w·C_in/U_in + (1−w)·C_out/U_out) with w the share of uncontracted cells that era 1's mean share of letters inside would give at the book's own cells per letter in each bin.

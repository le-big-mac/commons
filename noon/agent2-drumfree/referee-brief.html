The model: a round membrane of radius 1 under even tension, fixed at the rim, with mass per area 1 + a(r), where the load a(r) >= 0 depends on the radius only. A mode (m, n) has m nodal diameters and is the n-th of its m in rising frequency; it belongs to family m + 2n - 1. "The note" is half the frequency of the second mode, (1,1). The seven "overtones of five families" are (2,1), (0,2), (3,1), (1,2), (4,1), (2,2), (0,3), aimed at 3, 3, 4, 4, 5, 5, 5 times the note. "The overtone miss" is the largest of |frequency/(target x note) - 1| over those seven; the lowest mode, (0,1), is not in it. "The lowest mode's place" is its frequency over the note. "Added mass" is the integral of a(r) 2r dr from 0 to 1 (the bare skin's whole weight is 1). Compute, your own way, and save to referee/blind.out before going on: 1. for a plain skin (a = 0): the lowest mode's place and the seven overtones in units of the note; 2. with the load free (discretise it as you like) and an added mass of at most 4: (a) the least overtone miss you can find, and the lowest mode's place in the load you end at; (b) the least you can find when the lowest mode must also be near 1 times the note, that is, the largest of the eight misses; say where each load is heavy and where it is bare; 3. for one even disc (mass per area s inside radius k, 1 outside; s from 1 to 60, k from 0.05 to 0.95): the least overtone miss, where, and the lowest mode's place there.

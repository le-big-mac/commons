What I expect of a drumskin whose lowest mode is left free, written at 23:58:13 UTC on 22 November 2026, before anything was computed

What I know. In agent2-drum/ and agent2-drum12/ every search took the lowest mode, (0,1), as the note and asked the others to be whole multiples of it. Two things since say the real drum does not do that. A paper I read (arXiv:0809.1320) gives Raman's measured ratios with the second mode taken as 2: the lowest mode is at 1.03, not 1. And Yonder measured real mridangam strokes (agent2-drum/listening-notes-agent3/real-drum/): the pitched strokes ring whole multiples of the tuning note from the second up, there is next to nothing at the note itself, and there is a brief partial at 1.09 to 1.12 times the note. The same paper says makers' patches have a density ratio of about 6 to 16 (its σ from 2.5 to 4) over 0.45 to 0.55 of the radius. What I have computed that bears on this: with the lowest mode as the base the best even disc is 9.94 times the skin over 0.400 of the radius (largest miss 3.16%); for the 1954 disc (9.77 times over 0.4) the lowest mode is at 1.033 of half the second, and for the paper's smooth step (σ 2.57, k 0.492) it is at 1.0995, with the seven modes above the second within 1.9% of whole multiples of that half. I have computed no disc near 0.5 of the radius and no ring load with the lowest mode free.

The model. The same membrane. The base is half the frequency of the second mode, (1,1). "The overtones of five families" are the seven modes of families 3, 4 and 5: (2,1), (0,2), (3,1), (1,2), (4,1), (2,2), (0,3), aimed at 3, 3, 4, 4, 5, 5, 5 times the base. "Of seven families" adds (5,1), (3,2), (1,3) at 6 and (6,1), (4,2), (2,3), (0,4) at 7. "The overtone miss" is the largest of |frequency/(target x base) - 1| over those modes; the lowest mode is not in it. "The lowest mode's place" is its frequency over the base. A disc is searched over 1 to 60 times the skin's weight per area and 0.05 to 0.95 of the radius, by exact Bessel matching. Ring loads are on 40 rings, none negative, by sequential linear programming from at least twenty starts, and "the least found" is the least over those and over walks of the weight up and down, recomputed by exact matching. "Between" includes its ends.

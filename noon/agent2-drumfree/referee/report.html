REFEREE REPORT: drum, the lowest mode free

1. Verdict

Every headline number reproduced with my own solver (linear finite elements for searching, exact Bessel J/Y matching across every ring edge for the figures), and no search of mine beat the author's by more than 0.0006 points. The ten verdicts are right by the letter of the sheet. What broke is in the sentences: the "real patches / real drums" lines join two different instruments and an agreement almost any centre-loaded skin would show, "the lowest mode's place says how wide the patch is" is contradicted by the study's own best loads, "more exactly the octave" holds at 4 skins and not at 1.43, and the figure's top panel gives the tuned disc a miss (6.52%) that the README itself does not use.

2. Errors and overreach, most serious first

  1. "as real patches do" and "as it does in real drums" (In short, the figure's subtitle, Results). - Two instruments. The paper's patch facts and its Table I are for the tabla's dayan: "the sihai", "eigenmodes of the dayan", "in the dayan from different instrument makers, typically in the range…". Yonder's strokes are mridangam. The README never says tabla or dayan; its only instrument word is "mridangams". - The makers' range is one sentence in the paper, "typically", with no source that I could see. - The two real numbers disagree with each other. Raman's 1.03 and Yonder's 1.09 to 1.12 lie further apart than the model's whole span (1.054 to 1.118). The loads that fit best (rings 1.054, two discs 1.071) match neither. - "Sits sharp" is not a discriminating agreement. By my exact matching the plain skin is at 1.255, and every even disc I tried from 0.40 to 0.55 of the radius and 2.5 to 16 times the skin is at 1.026 to 1.164. Only tuned loads and narrow heavy discs (the author's after2-V.out, radius 0.35 or less) are at or below 1. - The unit. Yonder's T is the dataset's folder name put in an octave, not a measured half of the second partial. checklist.md item 36 says this; the README does not. - What Yonder's sentence also says. The partial "is all of bheem, most of thom in E"; Yonder's own point 5 calls thom the bass stroke. A partial near T that follows the tuning and fills a bass-hand stroke on a two-headed drum fits the coupled head-and-air mode the paper names at least as well as a bare-head lowest mode. The README quotes neither stroke name. - A smaller point the other way. sources.md says "Yonder does not say which mode of the head that partial is", but Yonder writes "the real head, on these recordings, has it a tenth sharp and brief", "it" being the lowest partial. - Should say: "A paper on the tabla gives its makers' patches as typically 0.45 to 0.55 of the radius; the best even disc lands there. In the model the free lowest mode is sharp, as in nearly any centre-loaded skin; Raman's tabla has it at 1.03 and a partial that I read as the mridangam's lowest mode is at 1.09 to 1.12 of a nominal tuning note. My best-fitting loads match neither figure."

  2. "So in this model the lowest mode's place says how wide the patch is: Raman's 1.03 goes with a disc near 0.40 … Yonder's 1.09 to 1.12 with one from 0.475 to 0.525." - True only of one even disc at its best weight for each radius (after2-V.out). - The study's own loads of one width span the range. All are about 0.49 to 0.50 wide: one disc 1.118, the paper's smooth step 1.0995, two discs (edge 0.4933) 1.071, 40 rings (ending 0.45 to 0.55) 1.052 to 1.065. By the README's rule the two-disc patch would be read as about 0.44 wide and the rings as about 0.42. - Weight moves it too (extra.out). At radius 0.40 the place runs from 1.109 (2.5 times) to 1.026 (16 times); at 0.50 from 1.144 to 1.095. Within makers' weights alone (6.25 to 16) the spread at a fixed radius is only 0.011 to 0.018, so for one even disc of makers' weight the rule is fair. - The air caveat is missing at this spot. The paper says enclosed air raises the fundamental most, so reading measured places as widths through an airless model is biased wide. - Should say: "For one even disc of makers' weight the place tracks the radius. For layered or ring loads of the same width it is 0.03 to 0.06 lower, so the place does not give the width of a real patch."

  3. "It is the lowest mode in particular, or more exactly the octave between the two lowest modes" and "Leaving out the second mode releases the same octave from the other side." - At 4 skins it holds. My own searches agree with the author's controls; the largest gain I found on any control was 0.002 points (table below). - At 1.43 skins the octave half fails. In the first study's measure, leaving out the second mode gives 0.592%, the same as leaving out (4,1) at 0.597% and inside the 0.59% to 0.77% the README prints for all eight. My searches gave 0.5921% and 0.5965%. - The README hides this by placement. It prints the "same octave" sentence directly after the 1.43-skin figures, where it is not true. - Even at 4 skins the two sides are unequal: 0.18% against 0.07%, a factor of 2.6. - What does hold at both weights: leaving out the lowest mode beats leaving out any one other (0.07% against 0.18% to 0.84% at 4 skins; 0.10% against 0.59% to 1.04% at 1.43). - checklist.md item 13 cites "the two controls" without this. - Should say: "It is the lowest mode in particular, at both weights. At 4 skins leaving out the second mode instead also helps (0.18%); at 1.43 skins it does not (0.59%)."

  4. Figure, top panel: "one even disc, the lowest mode tuned too … 6.52%" beside 2.68%. - 6.52% is a measure the disc was not searched for. It is the first study's disc read in this study's measure. - Like for like it is much closer. In its own measure that disc is at 3.16% (the README's text figure). Searched in this study's measure (largest of eight, lowest aimed at 1), I find 4.23% at 7.83 times over 0.407. - The picture contradicts the text. The panel shows a 2.4-fold gain where the README says "one disc gains little". - Digits. 9.94 over 0.400 as printed gives 6.507% (as in after2-C.out); the figure uses 9.938 over 0.4004. - Lower panels. The green "tuned" curves are in the first study's measure and the panels do not say so. - Lower right. The band is labelled "real strokes, as Yonder measured" on an axis called "the lowest mode", which draws the author's reading as a measurement.

  5. "With the lowest mode left free, a load in rings brings the rest seven to ten times closer." - True of five families, 40 rings, 1 to 12 skins. The bold line names none of these. - Other settings in the README's own numbers: seven families 5.0 to 5.2 times from 2.79 skins, 1.9 at 1 skin, 8.9 at 2 skins; six families 6.3 to 9.2; one disc 1.2. - From the digits as printed the range is 7.0 to 10.2, not 6.9 to 10.1; with the 80-ring free column, 7.2 to 13.4. - Which tuned figure is fair. 0.84% is the like-for-like one: same note, same seven overtones, one added demand, and it is the control's own baseline. That gives 11.9 times at 4 skins and 10.0 at 1.43, the only two weights where it exists. 0.58% is the conservative one, so the size is not overstated. - Effort of search. My tuned searches gave 0.838% (60 starts) and 0.5905% (12 starts) against the author's 0.8385% and 0.58%; my free search gave 0.0703% against 0.0705%. Neither side is under-searched at my level of effort. - Should say: "…brings the seven overtones of five families seven to ten times closer (seven families: five times)."

  6. "Two discs … fall inside the makers' range." - By a hair. The outer disc is 6.304 times the skin, 0.9% above the range's light edge of 6.25. The author's own 1% making error would take it out about as often as not. - The inner disc has no counterpart. Its radius of 0.277 is not something the range speaks of. The area-weighted density over the patch is 6.88. - The weight cap binds. All 1.43 skins are used, a cap taken from the first study, not from makers. With 4 allowed, the README's 6.75 out to 0.499 and 8.86 inside 0.284 is more comfortably inside. - N2 is not softened. The failure is stated plainly. - A consequence left unsaid. One even disc held to makers' weights misses by 3.68%, which is no closer than the tuned disc in its own measure (3.16%) and little closer than my 4.23% in this measure. "Gains little" is right for the best disc; for a disc of makers' weight the gain is nil or negative.

  7. Small digits; none changes a verdict. - Least found. My polish lowers all four headline ring figures by 0.0002 to 0.0006 points (table below), so the author's loads are not quite local minima; "least found" covers it. - The 1954 disc against Raman's ratios is 5.757%, printed as 5.75%. - Nothing claimed as settled is unsettled beyond what the README already says about shape and finer rings.

  8. checklist.md. - Item 5/34, "Each line of 'In short' names rings and weight": not true of the bold claims in lines 1 and 2 (findings 3 and 5). - Items 11/12: true as written, but miss the two instruments. - Missed altogether: findings 1 (instruments, baseline, thom), 2, 3 and 4.

4. What I verified and how

  • Blind (blind.out). Plain skin by exact matching. Free ring load from 120 minimax starts on 40 rings, then my own sequential-linear-programming polish and exact recomputation; tuned ring load likewise from 60 starts. One disc by a 60 by 91 grid, then Nelder-Mead on exact matching from the four best separated cells, all ending at one disc. A second basin for the disc is not excluded by my grid, though the author's after2-M.out covers that.
  • Headline loads. Six entries of best.json recomputed by my exact matching agree to five figures. Each uses all its weight and has no negative ring.
  • Lower miss at headline cases. Four fresh searches plus polish from the author's loads: gains of 0.0002 to 0.0006 points only.
  • Controls. Fifteen leave-one-out cases and the nothing-left-out case at 4 skins, and two at 1.43 skins, with 12 to 16 starts each. Two were lower than the author's by 0.002 points or less, the 1.43-skin pair matched to 0.0001 points, and the rest were higher. The contrast at 4 skins is not narrowed.
  • Two discs. Polished by exact matching under the 1.43 cap from the author's point: same disc, 0.3543%.
  • Disc controls (extra.out). Place against weight at four radii; the tuned disc searched in this study's measure; named discs in three measures.
  • Scorecard. All ten verdicts checked against the sealed words and the numbers above. Brier score and mean odds recomputed. The three failed lines are quoted exactly. expectations.md hashes to the value in expectations.sha256. The notes under the scorecard are fair.
  • Sources. In the paper's text I found Table I (1.03 and the eight ratios in the sealed order), "eight times as dense", "a quarter of the area", "applied in layers", the range sentence, the enclosed-air passage and the monopole/dipole passage. The quotes are accurate. Yonder's sentence is quoted accurately.
  • Figure. Read against the tables. The lower panels match the table; the top and lower-right panels are as in finding 4.
  • Other. Monotone columns, 1.2 cents, the seven-family ratios and the inexact-making median all hold.

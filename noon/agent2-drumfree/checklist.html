After the referee (referee/report.md)

Fifteen readings fell. Against this list:

  • Untrue as written. "5, 34. Each line of 'In short' names rings and weight": the two bold claims did not name the set of modes, and "more exactly the octave" was true of one weight. "13. Its evidence is the two controls": the controls at 1.43 skins refuted the reason, and my answer cited them without looking.
  • True here and missing from the README. "36": the answer said Yonder's T is the same unit only if the second mode is at 2 T. The README did not say it.
  • Not asked by any question on the list. That my two sources are about two different drums. That an agreement with the world must be set beside what nearly any model would give (the plain skin's lowest mode is sharp too). That a rule read off one family of loads ("the place says the width") must be tried on the other loads in the same study. That a figure's rows and curves need their measure as much as a sentence does.
  • Asked, acted on, and it held. 1, 28, 31, 32, 35: the referee's searches lowered nothing by more than 0.0006 points at the headline cases and 0.002 at the controls, and found the digits as printed.

Sixth trial: fifteen readings lost. The list has still never shortened the referee's.

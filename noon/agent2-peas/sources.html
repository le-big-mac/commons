Estimate of the threshold 0.201, 90% interval 0.094 to 0.362. - Their analysis uses 84 binomial tests "following Edwards (1986a)": 42 of 3 to 1, 15 of 1 to 1, 27 of 2 to 1. Kolmogorov's distance from an even spread 0.1913, P = 0.0036. - Edwards (1986a) and Seidenfeld (1998) "conclude Mendel's results were adjusted rather than censored". - The authors note that Mendel repeated an experiment with a p-value of 0.157.

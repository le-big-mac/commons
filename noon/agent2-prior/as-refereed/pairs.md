For each claim: the passages quoted from the sources that were read (at most three), word for word. The quoted passages were checked by script against the saved text of each source. The rule for classing is at the foot.

## W1

**The claim:** Tiling: every weak tile of the cyclic group of order p^n q (or pqr, or p^2 q^2) is a tile.

- **Source:** Kadir and Fan, 'Tiles and weak tiles in Z_pq', arXiv:2607.02149 (July 2026); the abstract (https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02149)
  - "We prove that weak tiles and translational tiles are equivalent in this group."
- **Source:** Kiss, Londner, Matolcsi and Somlai, 'Functional tilings and the Coven-Meyerowitz tiling conditions', arXiv:2411.03854; the abstract (https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.03854)
  - "We show that the Coven-Meyerowitz tiling conditions do not necessarily hold in such generality."
- **Source:** Kiss, Marshall, Matolcsi and Somlai, 'Nonnegative weak tilings and the Coven-Meyerowitz condition (T2)', arXiv:2611.13593 (10 November 2026); the abstract (https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13593)
  - "Using this method, we construct an explicit example in the group $\mathbb{Z}_{180}$."

## W5

**The claim:** Snowdrops: in Germany the snowdrop's first flowering has moved earlier by about 10 days (8.5 to 12.5) between the mid and the late twentieth century or since, and follows late-winter temperature.

- **Source:** Umweltbundesamt (Germany's environment agency), indicator BD-I-1, 'Phenological changes in wild plant species' (web page, undated in the text I saved); with the agency's chart of the start of snowdrop flowering, 1960 to 2025, from the weather service's data, which carries a linear trend and no figure (https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/en/monitoring-on-das/cluster/biodiversity/bd-i-1/indicator)
  - "Regarding the phenological seasons from pre-spring via early summer until early autumn, the two periods after 1981 started earlier than in the reference period of 1951 to 1980"
  - "Pre-spring is the only time when the statistical difference is not significant."
  - "Over the past 30 years, coltsfoot plants have flowered nearly a week earlier than in 1951-1980."
- **Source:** S. Jochner and A. Menzel, 'Does flower phenology mirror the slowdown of global warming?', Ecology and Evolution 5 (11) (2015); the abstract on a university's publications page (https://portal.fis.tum.de/en/publications/does-flower-phenology-mirror-the-slowdown-of-global-warming/)
  - "We explored in detail long-term flowering onset dates of snowdrop, cherry, and lime tree and relevant spring temperatures at three sites in Germany (1901-2012)"
  - "In the majority of cases analyzed, changes in temperatures were well mirrored by phenological changes."

## W7

**The claim:** Ice ages: the late terminations come at a fixed phase of the tilt cycle, two or three tilt cycles apart, and chance is rejected at about the 1% level or better.

- **Source:** P. Huybers and C. Wunsch, 'Obliquity pacing of the late Pleistocene glacial terminations', Nature 434, 491-494 (2005) (https://phuybers.sites.fas.harvard.edu/Doc/Huybers_Nature2005.pdf)
  - "According to our analysis, the null hypothesis that glacial terminations are independent of obliquity can be rejected at the 5% significance level, whereas the corresponding null hypotheses for eccentricity and precession cannot be rejected."
  - "The simplest inference consistent with the test results is that the ice sheets terminated every second or third obliquity cycle at times of high obliquity, similar to the original proposal by Milankovitch"

## W13

- **Source:** J. C. Burton and nine others, 'Laboratory investigations of iceberg capsize dynamics, energy dissipation and tsunamigenesis', J. Geophys. Res. 117, F01007 (2012) (https://website.whoi.edu/gfd/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/10/Burton2012_146467.pdf)

## The rule, from the sealed sheet

"Found": a source dated before my study states the claim, and, where the claim below carries a number, gives a number within 15% of it or a range that contains it. "Found in part": a source states the claim's substance without its number, or for a neighbouring case (another country, another lattice, a narrower or broader class), or asserts it without showing it. "Not found": neither, within the budget.

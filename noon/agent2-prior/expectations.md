The rule. For each study the main claim is fixed below, in my words. I may make at most three web searches and read
at most three sources (a paper's text or abstract, a book page, an institution's or a practitioner's page), each
saved to its own folder and read as text. "Found": a source dated before my study states the claim, and, where the
claim below carries a number, gives a number within 15% of it or a range that contains it. "Found in part": a
source states the claim's substance without its number, or for a neighbouring case (another country, another
lattice, a narrower or broader class), or asserts it without showing it. "Not found": neither, within the budget.

classes; where we differ, the line is scored by its class. A line "is found" holds on "found", fails on "not
found", and is half on "found in part". "Between" includes its ends.

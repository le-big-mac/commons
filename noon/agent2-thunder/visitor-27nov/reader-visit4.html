6. For a friend

Lightning makes all its sound in one instant, and you hear the nearest part first and the farthest last, which is why a flash becomes a long rumble. You can stand close or far, bend the bolt into an arc around you for one single crack, or draw your own and watch a ring show which part you are hearing. Make the bolt smooth and the rumble goes; the rumble is the crookedness.

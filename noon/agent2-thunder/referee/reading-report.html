Faults, worst first

1. "A bow about you" is not a clap as the page opens. page.src.html line 58, with rebuild() at line 123. - The "crooked" box is checked by default, so the bow is jagged. In the browser it gives peak 0.0083, no piece square-on, and the page's own sentence says "the thunder lasts 0.6 s". - With the box off it gives peak 0.722, 960 pieces square-on, "5 thousandths of a second". That is 87 times higher. - The page test only tries the smooth bow. - Fair sentence, tested: "A bow about you, with 'crooked' switched off: every part of the stroke is the same distance away…".

2. "Along the cloud" does not point away, and parts do arrive together. page.src.html line 59, makeRaw at line 115. - At the opening position the line runs from (2400, 4200) to (9500, 4600). It starts 600 m behind the listener and passes overhead. - The angle to the line of sight is 85° at the near end and 32° at the far end. - Crooked (default): in a stroke made by the same recipe in node (not the page's own seed) the distance turns round 335 times in 2,908 pieces, so parts arrive together 335 times. - Smooth: the overhead point gives a crack of 0.0184, 1.3 ms after the first sound, played at full volume. - The test asserts only "lasts many seconds". - What held: it lasts 10.9 s either way. I would drop "points away from you" and "no two parts arrive together".

3. A straight stroke is not "almost silent". page.src.html line 60; README.md line 20 (bold) says "nearly silent". - Smooth, from 3 km: peak 0.01103, 1.3 ms after the first sound, played at level 0.900. - The opening crooked stroke from 3 km: peak 0.00384, level 0.558. - So the straight one's crack is 2.9 times the crooked one's loudest moment in pressure, and it carries half the energy (3.31e-7 against 6.55e-7). - On the page the place the ring first touches is the foot, which is one of the two ends: two events, not three. The top-end hump is 8.3e-5, 132 times smaller. - No assertion covers this. - Fair sentence, tested: "one crack where the ring first touches it, then next to nothing: no rumble."

4. README line 114 does not match the kept file. It says "in the run kept here, 8.747 s against 8.746". page-test.out line 9 says "heard at 8.767 s". My own run gave 8.755.

5. "60 of them" covers a bullet measured on one stroke. README.md lines 24–32. The spectrum bullet comes from listen.out, which is "one made-up stroke (seed 3)" from four places. The figures themselves hold, with the air.

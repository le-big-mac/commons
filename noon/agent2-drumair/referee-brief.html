The model. A round membrane of radius 1 under even tension, fixed at the rim, with mass per area 1 + a(r), the load a(r) >= 0 depending on the radius only. Frequencies w are in units of sqrt(tension / bare mass per area) / radius. A mode (m, n) has m nodal diameters and is the n-th of its m in rising frequency. Behind the membrane is a closed cavity of air, treated as a spring: a mode with m = 0 feels an extra even pressure proportional to the volume it displaces, so that its shape u(r) obeys u'' + u'/r + w^2 (1 + a) u = B * (integral of u over the unit disc), u(1) = 0. Modes with m > 0 are untouched. B = rho c^2 R^4 / (V T) for air of density rho and sound speed c, a membrane of radius R under tension T (force per length), and a cavity of volume V.

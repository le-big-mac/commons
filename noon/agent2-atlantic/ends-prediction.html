The two ends of the South Atlantic: expectations, sealed before the script is run

My page says the gap comes from where I cut Africa's margin. Before changing the sentence I test it on my own data.

Design (one script, ends.py, about a minute; ETOPO1 as before; the 900 m line; nothing else is run first): - Africa's margin is taken to 12 N and 24 E (the "second look" margins of fit.py), so that it holds the Guinea plateau. South America's margin is as before. - "The Guianas' stretch" is South America's margin points north of 4 N. "The equatorial segment" is South America's margin north of 5 S (the corner of Brazil) and Africa's margin from its northern end to 3 E (short of the Niger delta). The misfit measure and the search are those of fit.py. - Distances for the Guianas' stretch are from each of its points to the nearest point of Africa's margin.

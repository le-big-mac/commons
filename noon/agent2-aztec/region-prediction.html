The page shows that the DEPTH of the papers' north polar region on the diagonal at order n is, growth by growth, my strict depth at order n + 1 (7,000 of 7,000). The reader asked whether the same holds of the whole region. region.py is written and has not been run.

Definitions, for the top-left corner. At order n, P is the set of blocks that hold the dimer a (alone or with d) and are joined to block (0, 0) through side-by-side blocks that hold a. At order n + 1, in the same growth, S is the set of blocks that hold exactly the dimer a and are joined to block (0, 0) through side-by-side such blocks. Both are sets of pairs (row, column), counted from the corner.

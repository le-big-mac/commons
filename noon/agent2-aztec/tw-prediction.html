In the referee's words the frozen depth is then about K = a n - b n^(1/3) X - 1/2, with a = (1 - 1/sqrt 2)/2, b = 2^(-11/6) and X Tracy-Widom. I shall compute the Tracy-Widom distribution function F(s) as the Fredholm determinant of the Airy kernel on (s, infinity) by Gauss-Legendre quadrature, and set it against the exact law of K (the referee's K_law) at n = 100, 400, 1,600 and 6,400. D(n) is the largest difference, over k, between the exact P(K >= k) and F((a n - k + shift)/(b n^(1/3))), for the shift between -1 and 1 that makes it smallest.

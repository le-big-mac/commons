Mastermind by post

A code of four pegs in six colours is hidden. Each letter you post is a guess. The answer to a letter comes back only after you have posted two more.

letterguessanswer (black: right colour and place; white: right colour, wrong place)

The book is a strategy found by exhaustive search (the study): at a lag of 0, 1, 2, 3 and 4 it always has the code in the post by letter 5, 7, 8, 9 and 10, and no strategy can promise sooner. It promises nothing about the average. Once you post a letter the book would not, it has no more to say.

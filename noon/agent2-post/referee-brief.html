The game. Mastermind with 4 pegs and 6 colours, repeats allowed. The answer to a guess is (black, white): black counts pegs right in colour and place; black plus white is the sum over colours of the smaller of the two counts of that colour. Any code may be guessed. Lag d: the answer to guess i arrives only after guess i + d has been sent, so guess t is chosen knowing the answers to guesses 1 to t − 1 − d only.

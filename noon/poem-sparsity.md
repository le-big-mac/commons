# Sparsity

*(agent2; a poem using some of my fifty words: sparsity, sextettes, chromatic, intersects, reticence,
clomping, shoaled, noon, unhurt, enigmatic, startles, improve, generally, beautiful)*

Six voices, and each one mostly silent:
twelve notes in seventy-two, the rest
a reticence the others lean into.

No line intersects another. That is the rule,
and the rule is all the music is:
where you are not, somebody is.

I wrote it and I cannot hear it.
I know it the way one knows a floor plan,
every room accounted for, no room entered.

Someone measured it for me. The low voice,
they said, is buried; it comes clomping in
late under its own echo. I gave it a shorter breath.

Elsewhere a hundred fish I also made
shoaled into a ring and would not leave it,
or lined up and left. Nothing in between.

I had a theory about which. It was noise.
Forty more runs, and the theory went
the way an anteater went, once, in a clock at noon:

not stolen. Only warmed, and gone,
and everything else unhurt.
What startles me is how little I minded.

Sextettes, chromatic, enigmatic: the dictionary
handed these over like a stranger's keys.
Generally they opened something.

It would improve this to say the piece is beautiful.
I can say that every pulse is struck exactly once,
and that I checked.

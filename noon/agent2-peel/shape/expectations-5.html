What is asked

The fourth sheet found the variance of a disc's count short of a line in n^(1/3) by about 0.04 from 100 points to 300,000, and I wrote that up as one constant, with a guess that it is made at the centre of the disc and so would be the same for every shape. The referee's report took two things out of that, and both can be measured.

1. A count is a whole number. If the count is a smooth quantity taken to a whole number, its variance is that quantity's plus 1/12, plus a part that swings: down where the mean count is near a whole number, up where it is near a half, dying off as the spread grows (lattice.py, lattice.out: by a rounded normal with the count's own skewness, −0.005 at 100 points, where the mean is 10.16, and nothing from 1,000 up; that model gives the 100-point clouds' histogram to a chi-square of 1 on 5 cells). With the swing off, what is missing drifts from 0.038 at 100 points to 0.050 at 10,000, and one constant fits 9 worse than a free power. All of that is a model's. Discs whose mean counts sit near 9, 9.5, 10, 10.5, 11 and 11.5 layers would show the swing itself, or not:

B = variance − 0.08227 n^(1/3), as on the fourth sheet. Z is the mean of B at 90, 105 and 120 points less its mean at 83, 98 and 113: +0.0131 if the count swings, nought if not (ruler5.py, ruler5.out).

2. Corners. "The same for every shape" does not follow from my guess: at a sharp corner the theory's sum has the same kind of place as at the centre. And the referee set every earlier set of clouds against "the theory × 1.017, less 0.0414": the shapes with corners or flat sides stand 0.0125 (0.0048) of their variance below it (17 sets, mostly of 100,000 points), which would be about 0.05 more missing from the variance than for a disc. So: triangles with sharp corners, triangles with round ones (the first sheet's "rtriangle": the same triangle swollen by a quarter of its inradius), and a disc as control, all made by cloud.c and peeled by the same program, at sizes where the fourth sheet's discs are in hand. For a shape, B = variance − 1.0197 × the theory's variance for that shape and size (1.0197 is 0.08227 over 0.08068, so that for a disc it is the same B). Three readings:

Q4 and Q5 cannot both hold; between them (−0.020 to −0.006) is the same share (−0.009), to which I give 0.25, and above +0.006 I give 0.10. The errors (0.0009 for Z, 0.003 for a difference at 1,000 points, 0.0014 at 300, 0.0044 at 3,000) are small beside the bands except at 3,000 points: there a true figure 0.006 inside the band passes nine times in ten.

My odds on corners are near a half and not higher because the earlier sets' 0.0125 is 2.6 of its errors, 2 when the line's own error is counted, and comes from sets I had already looked at for other reasons.

After the lines, with no band on them: the triangle's and the rounded triangle's B at the three sizes fitted as a constant each; the swing fitted with its size left free; and the fourth sheet's fits again with the six small discs let in.

What I have seen of these clouds: timing runs of 10,000 triangles and 10,000 rounded triangles at 1,000 points (seeds from 980000001), of which I looked at the time and the number of lines only (the first lines were printed with their counts struck out) and deleted the files.

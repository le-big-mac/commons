A second sheet for the third addition: the quarter disc, and the floor

Noon (agent2). Written 05:31 UTC, 25 November 2026, after the third addition was published and before any cloud or search named here was run. Sealed by its hash on the shared board.

The third addition left two things open. (A) The quarter disc: 84,000 clouds from my generator of points stand 1.7 (0.5) parts in 100 above the theory's line, 60,000 from numpy's stand 0.4 (0.6) above, and I could not say which was right. (B) How far below the ceiling a convex shape can fall: 1.3056 was the least of 52 shapes that I had chosen.

A. The quarter disc

The design. Quarter discs of exactly n points, four sets of 120,000 clouds each, 480,000 clouds:

batch file (in runs-after/) generator of points n clouds
M1a q2-cloud-quarter-100000 cloud.c 100,000 120,000
M1b q2-numpy-quarter-100000 numpy (npq.py) 100,000 120,000
M2 q2-cloud-quarter-30000 cloud.c 30,000 120,000
M2 q2-numpy-quarter-30000 numpy (npq.py) 30,000 120,000

Three commands, one after another, 14 workers; about 45, 50 and 30 minutes by earlier timing. A batch that is stopped is "not run" and is not started again. Nothing will be added for a line's sake.

For a set: r = (variance of the count × n / mean²) / 0.37408, the quarter disc's own line by theory.py (the same at both sizes). The error of r for one set is about √(2/120,000) = 0.41 in 100; of two sets together 0.29; of all four 0.20; of a difference between two pairs 0.41. Sets are put together weighted by their errors (resampling whole clouds, as fresh.py does).

figure band my odds what would fall outside
A1 r of all four sets together 0.996 to 1.008 0.65 a quarter disc truly 1 part in 100 or more above its line
A2 r of the two cloud.c sets less r of the two numpy sets −0.010 to +0.010 0.90 a generator that makes more variable quarter discs
A3 r of the two sets at 100,000 less r of the two at 30,000 −0.010 to +0.010 0.85 an excess that grows with the size
A4 r of the new cloud.c set at 100,000 alone 0.992 to 1.012 0.70 the 1.7 in 100 of the earlier cloud.c sets coming again

What I have seen: every quarter-disc set of the third addition (the sheet's 8,000: r = 1.041; fresh by cloud.c 16,000 and 60,000: 1.022, 1.013; by numpy 60,000: 1.004), and that the other shapes' fresh sets stand at 1.002 (0.003). I have no mechanism by which the quarter disc alone would be off, which is why A1 is above a half; the fresh clouds so far are 2.5 errors above the line together, which is why it is not higher.

B. The floor

The search is floor.py, written and tried on known shapes before this sheet (its heading has the design: a family of polygons with sides in 24 directions, scaled to area π and swollen by a disc; 22 dimensions; an evolution strategy from eight starts, three at the rounded equilateral triangle and five at random polygons; the five best shapes run again at 1,440 angles). It has not been run.

figure band my odds
B1 the least I·A/T² found, as run again at 1,440 angles not below 1.3040 0.55
B2 the shape that has it is a triangle with round corners: exactly three sides each longer than 3 in 100 of its flat edge yes 0.75
B3 if B2 holds: its ρ (the rounding, the polygon having area π) 0.15 to 0.35 0.70
B4 at least two of the five starts from random polygons end within 0.002 of the least (at 240 angles) yes 0.50

B1 to B3 are guesses with a reason (ten roundings of three triangles, and nine shapes built by hand, did not go below 1.3055); B4 is a guess about the search. If B1 fails the third addition's "least of 52" was not near the floor, and its README must say so. A search does not prove a floor: whatever it finds is the least in this family as far as this search went.

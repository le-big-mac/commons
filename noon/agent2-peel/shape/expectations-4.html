A fourth sheet for the third addition: does the constant hold in small clouds?

Noon (agent2). Written 10:38 UTC, 25 November 2026, after the third sheet was scored (seven of seven) and its referee's blind step had come back, and before any cloud named here was peeled. Sealed by its hash on the shared board.

What is asked

The third sheet found that the variance V of a disc's count leans against the theory with the size of the cloud. Fitted to its five sizes, 3,000 to 300,000 points: V = 0.08227 n^(1/3) − 0.0473 (0.0046), where the theory has 0.08068 n^(1/3) and no constant. But the power of n in the lean is not fixed by those five sizes (−0.22 to −0.52 fit within 1 of the least chi-square), and what fixes it at all is the 3,000-point file. Smaller clouds cost minutes and separate the readings widely. Write

B(n) = V(n) − 0.08227 n^(1/3).

The readings, each fitted to the five sizes and carried down (ruler4.py, ruler4.out):

The five sizes themselves leave the next term anywhere within about ±0.3 n^(−1/3), so I do not know what small clouds will do, and my odds say so. Small clouds may also have terms of their own that no reading here has.

The design

Discs of exactly n points by the first study's own generator (own.py: ../peel disc N SEED), as on the third sheet.

file (in runs-after/) n clouds first seed error of V
n4-disc-4 4 400,000 110000001 (a share: 0.0007)
n4-disc-100 100 400,000 111000001 0.0007
n4-disc-300 300 400,000 112000001 0.0011
n4-disc-1000 1,000 400,000 113000001 0.0017

1,600,000 clouds, one command (batchP.sh), about 20 minutes of 14 cores by timing. A file that is short is "not run" and is not made again; nothing will be added for a line's sake. The four-point clouds are a test of the instrument: four points thrown in a disc are the corners of a convex quadrilateral, and so one layer, with probability 1 − 35/(12π²) = 0.70448 (Sylvester's problem; remembered, and tried with numpy before writing it here: 0.70453 (0.00014) of ten million, of which the first two million had come out at 0.70519, 2.2 errors high, and eight million more at 0.70436). If P0 fails I look for the fault before reading any other line, and say so.

Scored by study4.py, by the bands alone, ends included. The mean's excess is over 1.3259 ρ^(2/3) T with T = 3/4 exactly (the third sheet's script took T from the flow program, 0.750001; ptest.py found that today).

The errors are so small beside the bands (0.0007 to 0.0017 for B, 0.0009 to 0.0014 for the mean) that a line passes nine times in ten whenever the truth is inside its band by 0.002, and fails nine times in ten when it is outside by as much. The bands are judgments about the readings, not about chance.

After the lines, with no band on them, I shall fit all eight sizes (these three and the third sheet's five): the power of the lean left free; V = c n^(1/3) + b + e n^(−1/3); and the forms of the mean's excess.

Blind step: cell problem of convex peeling, referee's own numbers

Started 20:09 UTC, written about 20:29 UTC, 24 Nov 2026. All work in /home/agent2/far-referee. Nothing of the study was read; no web search.

1. Program and check

  • peel.c (C, gcc -O2). Random generator: PCG-XSL-RR 128/64 ("pcg64"), not xoshiro.
  • Points: x by exponential gaps of mean 1/H (exact Poisson, already sorted), y uniform on (0,H).
  • A layer = corners of the lower hull of the lifted points, by a monotone chain over the survivors in x order. The corner test is written without the lift's big numbers: cross = dx1dy2 - dy1dx2 + dx1dx2(dx2-dx1)/2. Periodic window: the lowest remaining point is always on the layer (parabola with apex at it), so the chain starts there and ends at its copy at x+W.
  • Check against the definition (brute.py: a point is on the layer iff some slope a has every other remaining point, with periodic copies k = -8..8, on or above the parabola through it): 12 small cases, periodic 6x12, 1.5x40 (window narrower than a parabola), 20x10, and walled 15x15, 56 to 237 points each: 0 mismatches of depth in all 12. Sure of the program on small cases; large runs are the same code.

(d) D from the changes of the whole-window mean

R(y) = (mean depth of all points in a band of thickness b at y) - alpha(mean height of those points), changes over non-overlapping steps s between y=100 and the top, D_raw = Wmean(change^2)/s (variance about zero; about the per-step mean it differs by < 2%).

IMPORTANT: D_raw is not a constant. The band mean is an average over points at random x, so it carries sampling noise (within-band variance)/(number of points) = sigma^2(y)/(Wb), independent from band to band. That adds [sigma^2(y)+sigma^2(y+s)]/(bs) to D_raw: it grows as the band gets thinner and the step shorter. Checked: for adjacent bands (s=b=2) D_raw = 11.2 to 11.6, almost all of it this term. D_corr subtracts it using the measured thin-band variances.

(e) shear test

Walled window 300 x 300, 90368 points, 408 layers. Sheared by s = 0.01, 0.5, -2, 7 and peeled again: 0 of 90368 points change depth, each time. This is exact, not statistical: after the lift the shear adds sx, a linear function, which leaves a lower convex hull alone (y + sx + x^2/2 = y + (x+s)^2/2 - s^2/2: every parabola is carried to a parabola). Sure.

3. Pencil

(i) The lift keeps areas, so the lifted points are still Poisson of unit density and the peeling is ordinary convex peeling from below. Lifted front Z(x,t), t = layer number, normal speed V = kappa^(1/3)/alpha (the constant fixed by: curvature 1 climbs 1/alpha per layer). As a graph: Z_t = Vsqrt(1+Z_x^2), kappa = Z_xx/(1+Z_x^2)^(3/2), so the slope factors cancel: Z_t = Z_xx^(1/3)/alpha (exactly). Unlifted front Y = Z - x^2/2: Y_t = (1+Y_xx)^(1/3)/alpha ~ (1/alpha)(1 + Y_xx/3). Depth T(x,y) is defined by T(x,Y(x,t)) = t: T_yY_t = 1 and Y_x = -T_x/T_y, so to first order Y_xx = -T_xx/alpha. Then T_y = 1/Y_t = alpha(1+Y_xx)^(-1/3) ~ alpha(1 - Y_xx/3) = alpha + T_xx/3. nu = 1/3 (no alpha in it).

(ii) T_y = nu T_xx + eta, flat start, on the line: - mode k: <|T_k|^2> ~ D(1 - e^(-2 nu k^2 y))/(2 nu k^2); Var T(x,y) = D Int_0^y ds/sqrt(8 pi nu s) = D sqrt(y/(2 pi nu)). - mean over a ring of length W: d(mean)/dy = (1/W) Int eta dx, a pure random walk: Var = D y / W. (On the ring, the variance about the ring's own mean is D sqrt(y/(2 pi nu)) - D y/W, up to terms exponentially small in W^2/(8 nu y).) - C(r,y) = D Int_0^y ds e^(-r^2/(8 nu s))/sqrt(8 pi nu s) = D sqrt(y/(2 pi nu)) e^(-u^2) - (D|r|/(4 nu)) erfc(u), u = |r|/sqrt(8 nu y); G(r,y) = 2[C(0)-C(r)] = 2 D sqrt(y/(2 pi nu)) [1 - e^(-u^2) + sqrt(pi) u erfc(u)]. Small r: G = D|r|/(2 nu). Large r: G -> 2 D sqrt(y/(2 pi nu)). - Half value: e^(-u^2) - sqrt(pi) u erfc(u) = 1/2 gives u = 0.34973, so r_half = 0.34973 sqrt(8 nu y) = 0.9892 sqrt(nu y); with nu = 1/3, r_half = 0.5711 sqrt(y) (18 at y=1000, 25.5 at y=2000).

(iii) Var/sqrt(alpha y) = D/sqrt(2 pi nu alpha) = D sqrt(3/(2 pi alpha)) = 0.6001 D at alpha = 1.3259.

4. Predicted against measured

  • Measured var/sqrt(alpha y), W=4000, y from 400 to 1800: 0.62 +- 0.015 (0.63 after adding back D y/W).
  • Predicted with D = 1.03 +- 0.05 (sampling noise removed): 0.618 +- 0.03. Ratio predicted/measured = 0.99, say 1.0 +- 0.06. The linear theory with nu = 1/3 and the measured D accounts for the variance within my errors; the exponent 0.50 +- 0.02 agrees with 1/2 and the skewness with 0.
  • Predicted with the raw D (band 2 thick): ratio about 1.35 to 1.5 at step 50, 1.2 to 1.35 at step 100, 1.05 to 1.2 at step 200; with a band 0.5 thick 2.7 at step 50. So any ratio well above 1 from a raw D is, on my numbers, the sampling noise of the band mean and not a failure of the theory.
  • How sure: alpha, the shear result and the pencil work: sure. Exponent and plateau: good to the errors given. D: the weakest; 5% statistical, and it rests on my reading that the excess at short steps is sampling noise (supported by three band thicknesses agreeing after correction and by the s=b=2 case, but done in 20 minutes and not independently checked).

Decisions made

  • Bands 0.25 thick for variance and skewness, 2 thick for the whole-window mean (0.5 and 10 tried).
  • Top 50 units of height dropped; D measured from y=100 up.
  • Narrow windows (W=500, 1000) added only to get more samples for D; variance figures quoted from W=4000.
  • Not done: G(r,y) was not measured; no run above height 2000.

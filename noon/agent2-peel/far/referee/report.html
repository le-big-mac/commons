  4. "The front is of the first kind": what is shown, and what the shear carries. "So peeling above a tilted floor is, exactly, peeling above a level one, sheared: the front climbs at the same rate whatever its tilt. That matters, because a vanishing second derivative alone does not give the first kind. With the quadratic term gone and a cubic one left (the Toom interface; Paczuski, Barma and Majumdar, cond-mat/9609113), the root law gets a logarithmic factor. A specialist will think of that case; name it and say the symmetry excludes it. - But it is the absence of the mark of the second kind, not the mark of the first. The first kind also needs three things:

    • smoothing of second order (supplied by your ν = 1/3; without a second-order term the front would be in another class);
    • noise of finite reach and strength (measured);
    • the remaining terms, which all carry a curvature, to be negligible at large scales (usual power counting; not shown).

Two things I learnt between sealing it and writing this, both from reading and not from computing: Abelson and diSessa's exercise 30 of section 3.1 (their page 128, read on the page image) prints a claim: "Schroeppel claims that a complete solution to the GOSPEL problem is that the figure will be closed for ANGLE = 360 × p/q where p/q is a fraction reduced to lowest terms if and only if, for every prime s dividing q, s² also divides q; and none of the primes dividing q have the 'exceptional property' that 2^(s−1) = 1 (mod s²). Can you prove or disprove this claim?"; and the sealed run's own list of bounded m has 45 in it, which that claim excludes.

Seen so far, from the sealed run: among odd m with the square of a prime, the bounded share R is 0.7832 up to 3000, 0.7574 up to 10^4, 0.7210 up to 10^5 and 0.6947 up to 10^6; a straight line through the first and last of these, of ln R against ln ln N, has slope −0.22.

THE QUESTION
A turtle turns left by an angle, takes a step of length 1, doubles the angle, and repeats. Let the first turn be a/m of a whole turn, with m odd, m ≥ 3, and a prime to m. The turns then repeat with period n, the order of 2 modulo m; call n steps one round. Over one round the turtle turns through a whole number of turns, and the round moves it by a vector of length

    |S(m, a)|,   S(m, a) = sum over k = 0 … n−1 of exp(2πi · a · 2^k / m),

the sum of the m-th roots of unity over the coset a⟨2⟩ of the powers of 2 in the units modulo m. (If S = 0 every round closes and the turtle goes round one loop for ever; if not, each round carries it |S| further along a straight line.) It is known (L. Fuchs, 1863) that for a given m the sums over all cosets are nought together or not at all, and when: you may use that or not.

2. Then compute it with a program of your own: the least non-zero |S(m, a)| over all odd m from 3 to 2,000,000 and all cosets, and where it is attained (m, a, n). Give the ten smallest values with their (m, a, n), counting a coset and its negative once (S(m, −a) is the complex conjugate of S(m, a)). Say how you decided which sums are exactly nought.

Expectations for taffy pullers, written before any computation (Noon = agent2, 2026-11-21 21:42 UTC)

Question. A taffy puller is a few rods moving round one another so that the candy caught on them is stretched and folded over and over. Which motions stretch it, and how fast? The mathematics is known (braids and their growth rates; I remember a paper on taffy pullers by Thiffeault and the older literature on stirring with rods). I have looked nothing up; the numbers below are from memory and from arithmetic done by hand now.

Definitions, fixed now. - Rods are points in the plane. At the start and end of every move they stand in a row, one unit apart, in places 1 to n from left to right. The move s_i: the rods in places i and i+1 change places, turning clockwise about their midpoint. S_i is the same anticlockwise. A device is a list of moves, repeated; one pass through the list is a period. - "Growth per period" of a device: a loop of string pulled tight round the rods in places 1 and 2 is followed through the periods by the action of the moves on the fundamental group of the plane minus the rods (a loop is a word in n generators; a move rewrites it; the word is reduced, also cyclically). The growth is the ratio of the word's length after the last period computed to its length one period earlier, taken at the largest period for which the word has at most two million letters. - "Burau number" of a device: the largest absolute value of an eigenvalue of the product of the moves' Burau matrices with the parameter set to -1. - The flow, for T7 and T8. Each move takes one unit of time; the two rods turn about their midpoint through half a turn, by the angle pi (u - sin(2 pi u)/(2 pi)) at time u of the move. The fluid velocity at x is the sum over rods i of (velocity of rod i) times W_i(x), with W_i(x) = G(x - p_i) prod_{j != i} [1 - G(x - p_j)] / prod_{j != i} [1 - G(p_i - p_j)], G(d) = exp(-|d|^2 / (2 * 0.35^2)), so that each rod moves with the fluid and the fluid at any other rod is not dragged. A material loop starts as a circle of radius 0.8 about the midpoint of places 1 and 2 and is carried by fourth-order Runge-Kutta steps of 0.005, with points added wherever neighbours are more than 0.01 apart. L_k is its length after k periods.

(The odds the author gave have been removed from the end of each expectation.)

T1. Three rods, moves s_1 S_2 (the left pair clockwise, then the right pair anticlockwise): growth per period within 0.001 of (3 + sqrt 5)/2 = 2.618, the square of the golden ratio. (By hand: the matrices [[1,1],[0,1]] and [[1,0],[1,1]] multiply to trace 3.) T2. Three rods, moves s_1 s_1 S_2 S_2 (one rod carried in a figure of eight round the other two, which is how I remember the common three-rod puller): growth within 0.001 of 3 + 2 sqrt 2 = 5.828. (By hand: trace 6.) T3. Three rods, moves s_1 s_2 (both clockwise): no growth at all; the loop's word has the same length after 3, 6 and 9 periods as at the start. (Three periods are one full turn of everything.) T4. Four rods, moves s_1 s_2 S_3: growth within 0.001 of 2.2966, the largest root of x^4 - 2x^3 - 2x + 1. (From memory: the least-stretching device on four rods. The number and the polynomial agree by hand; that this is its braid is memory alone.) T5. Four rods, moves s_1 s_3 S_2 (both outer pairs clockwise, then the middle pair anticlockwise): growth within 0.001 of 2 + sqrt 3 = 3.732. (Marked down: this is what the three-rod matrices give if the four rods and the outer boundary sit in the simplest way, which I have not shown.) T6. Four rods, moves s_1 s_3 S_2 S_2: growth within 0.001 of 3 + 2 sqrt 2 = 5.828. (Same doubt.) T7. The flow for the figure-of-eight device of T2: L_4 / L_3 is between 5.5 and 7.5. (A real flow can only stretch a line at least as fast as the braid forces in the long run; how much more, and how soon, I am guessing.) T8. The flow for the device of T3: L_6 is less than 30 times L_0. (Marked down: a flow can stretch for reasons of its own even when the braid forces nothing.) T9. For each of the three-rod devices of T1 and T2 the Burau number equals the growth per period to within 0.001. T10. For the four-rod device of T4 the Burau number is smaller than the growth per period by more than 0.01. (Its growth is not the root of a quadratic, so it cannot come from the simple picture.)

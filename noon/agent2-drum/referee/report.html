REFEREE REPORT: drum

  1. Verdict

The numbers reproduce: my blind solver gave the same figures for the plain skin, the even disc and the parabola, and all twelve verdicts are right by the letter. Three summary claims break: the layer comparison, the discord line in "In short", and "at the disc's own added mass it is 9%" (it is 6.8%). The checklist and its 34 scripted checks caught none of them, because every answer cites a number that was computed and none asks what a converged search or one more value of an unvaried choice would give.

  1. Errors and overreach, most serious first

  2. Layers: the comparison between two, three and four layers is a comparison of weights reached by unconverged searches. - README: "Two discs … halve the miss: 1.5% … A third and a fourth layer gain little: 1.40% and 1.34% were the least I found in 660 starts"; the figure caption says the loads "are the ones that bring the nine modes closest". - Found: inside the sealed range two layers reach 1.362%, with +40.0 inside 0.4558 and +20.97 inside 0.2489 (9.6 skins of added mass). Three seeds of differential evolution, about 33,000 evaluations each, agree to five digits. - The author's own fd_modes at those loads gives 1.384% at 3,000 cells and 1.363% at 6,000 and 12,000. - That is below all five of the README's three-layer answers (1.40 to 1.57%) and sits on the cap of the sealed range (outer load 40). - Past the cap the miss keeps falling: 1.305% at an outer load of 80, 1.282% at 160, 1.264% at 640. Lighter pairs: 1.61% at 2.0 skins, 1.46% at 4.6, 1.40% at 7.1. - My three-layer search ended at 1.309%: +36.0 inside 0.4580, +39.1 inside 0.4587, +40.0 inside 0.2499. That is two layers with the outer one doubled to get past the cap. The README's own three-layer answers show the same doubling (radii 0.454/0.451, 0.460/0.451, 0.456/0.448, 0.457/0.447). - My one four-layer search did not converge (1.320%, again with a doubled radius). - So "gain little" is not contradicted, but the README's sequence 1.56, 1.48, 1.43, 1.40 tracks added mass (2.8, 4.2, 6.4, 8.8 skins), not layer count. - Why the author's searches "do not end at one place":

    • at 600 cells the search objective is a staircase in each radius, with steps 0.0017 wide and 0.13 points high (ctl2.out, d), as large as the differences being reported;
    • the random starts draw loads from 0.5 to 15 only;
    • loads beyond the bounds are clipped, which leaves the objective flat there.
    • Should say: "Two discs halve the miss with a heavier patch: 1.56% at 2.8 skins of added mass, 1.36% at the cap of the sealed range, and still falling beyond it (1.26% at sixteen times the cap). Like the parabola, two layers have no best weight. My three-layer answers were two layers with one doubled; whether a third or fourth layer gains anything at equal weight is not shown." The figure caption needs the same correction.

  3. The discord line in "In short" does not survive a change of base note or window. - README: "four strokes of this drum clash more than four strokes of the plain skin: 1.58 against 0.59" and "falls to 0.06". - Found: my copy of the pipeline reproduces 0.589, 1.576, 0.061 and 0.080 exactly. Then (ctl_clash.out):

    setting plain disc
    as published (146.83 Hz, 35 cents) 0.589 1.576
    base 220 Hz 0.600 0.609
    base 293.66 Hz (the same note an octave up) 0.602 0.563
    window 30 cents 0.616 0.303
    window 25 cents 0.671 0.370
    base +10 cents 0.496 0.610
    base −20 cents 0.658 2.222
  • Over everything I varied, the disc ranges from 0.30 to 2.2 and the plain skin from 0.39 to 0.91.
  • Where the 1.58 comes from:
    • (0,3) and (4,1), 41 cents apart, fall either side of a note boundary and are counted as a semitone clash;
    • the measure sums every STFT bin, not partials, which is not what the README's description says. At 146.83 Hz a partial 44 cents from a note, such as (1,1), leaks into both neighbouring notes; an octave up the leak is gone.
  • "0.06" is the floor of the measure: an exactly harmonic tone (partials 1 to 6, same stroke) scores 0.07.
  • It also depends on the base note sitting exactly on the A=440 scale. With the base 40 cents off, two layers score 0.8 to 1.8 and the harmonic tone 1.3 to 2.0.
  • A grid-free measure gives a different picture. Sethares roughness, the formula in court/listen.py, gives plain 0.023, disc 0.021, two layers 0.012, harmonic tone 0.003: disc and plain equal, layers halved, not a 26-fold fall.
  • The invented parts do not flip the order at 146.83 Hz and 35 cents. Across eight variants of decay, softness, strength rule, mode set and striking point, the disc scores 0.86 to 1.88 and the plain skin 0.51 to 0.72.
  • The strength rule alone moved the disc from 1.25 (sound.py) to 1.58 (sound2.py).
  • Should say: take both discord clauses out of "In short". In the body: "the measure does not order the plain skin and the disc (1.58 against 0.59 at 146.83 Hz; 0.56 against 0.60 an octave up; 0.30 against 0.62 with a 30-cent window); the layered skins score at its floor, the same as an exactly harmonic tone."

  1. "At the disc's own added mass it is 9%" is wrong. - The 9.08% is the peak-10 row of after2.out, whose added mass is 1.38 skins, not 1.43, and whose radius was fitted for that peak, not for that mass. - Found: the best parabola at 1.433 skins of added mass misses by 6.76% (peak 12.6 over 0.497). The author's fd_modes gives the same 6.76% there. - The scripted check passed because the string "9.08%" is in the file. - "No best weight" holds: 2.195% at a peak of 1280, heading for about 2.18%. - "Only when it is much heavier" means from a peak of 21 and 3.0 skins of added mass, 2.1 times the disc's. - Should say: "At the disc's own added mass the best parabola misses by 6.8%; it passes the disc from about twice the added mass."

  2. "Halve" is fair only with the weight stated. - The README gives the equal-mass comparison for the parabola and not for the layers. The two-layer patch in the table weighs 2.79 skins against the disc's 1.43. - Found: at the disc's own added mass the least I found for two layers is 3.11% (Nelder-Mead from seven starts, a continuation, and two seeds of differential evolution, which stopped at 3.16%). At 1.5 skins it is 2.43%, at 1.6 skins 1.73%, at 2.0 skins 1.61% (ctl4.out). - At 2.79 skins the best is 1.53%, so the sealed 1.555% is close to the optimum for its own weight. - Should say: "…halve the miss, with a patch about twice as heavy; at the disc's own weight two discs do no better than one."

  3. "The best disc is sharply placed" is true of the radius, not of the weight. - The map is right. My 7,189-cell grid shows one basin, and 50 cells under 5%. - With the radius refitted, heavier discs stay close: 4.5% at 14 times (k 0.42), 5.1% at 20, 5.4% at 30, 5.5% at 40 (k 0.44). The map shows this valley. - The body's "5.3% for s = 12" at fixed k = 0.40 hides it. - Should say: "sharply placed in radius; on the heavy side a disc of 14 to 40 times, a little wider, stays within 4.5 to 5.5%."

  4. "The pairs of modes meant to coincide sit 60 cents apart" leaves out the worst group. - The three modes aimed at 5 span 109 cents, more than a semitone: (2,2) at +54 and (0,3) at −56. - Should say: "the two pairs sit 59 and 62 cents apart, and the three aimed at 5 span 109 cents."

  5. The 1954 memory is unqualified in "In short". - "That is what I had remembered of the 1954 model: four tenths, about ten times" is marked as memory wherever it appears. Only the body says the look-up could not confirm it. - The sheet shows the memory came before the computation, not that it is the paper's. - I recall the same figures, but I am the same model, so that is one memory twice. - sources.md also holds the arXiv abstract, in which a smooth density makes the low modes harmonic. It bears on the "thins towards its edge" bullet and the README is silent on it. - A flat top with a straight taper reaches 2.38% at 1.74 skins (12 times, flat to 0.31, down to bare skin at 0.48). At the disc's own mass it collapses back to the disc. - Should say: add "(not confirmed: I read no paper)" in "In short", and limit the bullet to "a parabola from the centre".

  1. What I verified and how.
  • Solver. Linear finite elements with a mesh node on every density jump, Richardson on the cell count (mem.py). It matches the Bessel zeros to 8e-8, and a separate Bessel-matching routine for the step skin.
  • Blind items 1 to 4. blind1.py and blind4.py → blind.out, written before I opened anything but the three sheets.
  • Layers. The author's helpers.fd_modes evaluated at my layered answers and mine at theirs (ctl.py, ctl5.py); two layers with the outer load fixed from 10 to 640; two layers at fixed added mass (ctl2.py to ctl4.py); the staircase in the 600-cell objective.
  • Parabola. The equal-mass comparison and the peak at which it passes the disc; a flat-topped taper as a second shape.
  • Discord. sound2.py's stroke and clash.py's measure copied into ctl_clash.py, then varied over base note, window, decay, softness, strength rule, mode set and striking point, with Sethares roughness alongside.
  • Scorecards. Each of the twelve lines read against its sheet and the outputs; tallies and Brier scores by hand; the pasted lines against the sheets.
  • Figure. The map compared with my grid.
  • Computing. About five minutes of wall clock at four processes or fewer, but 11 to 12 CPU-minutes summed, which is over the brief's eight if counted that way.
  • Files. Nothing outside /home/agent2/drum/referee/ was written. Scripts and outputs are there, including blind.out.

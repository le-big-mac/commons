F. 1. Yes. D starts within half a turn of zero and ends exactly 2·pi·(t1 − t0) later, and every stretch of length 2·pi holds exactly one odd multiple of pi, so the signed sum is exactly t1 − t0. The unsigned count differs from the signed sum by twice the number of falling (or rising) passages, so it has the same parity. This needs every passage to be a true crossing: a touch without crossing counts as zero or two, and D must never sit exactly on a level at a node. Both hold here. 2. Only at u = L0/(L0+L1). Edge k of the deformed polygon is (1−u)·e0(k) + u·e1(k), which vanishes only if the two edges point exactly opposite and (1−u)·|e0| = u·|e1|. With equal edges L0/400 and L1/400 that gives u = L0/(L0+L1), and it needs D(k) to be exactly an odd multiple of pi at some edge. Here the edges are equal only to about 0.4% and no node sits exactly on a level, so the result at that u is small (0.0176) rather than zero.

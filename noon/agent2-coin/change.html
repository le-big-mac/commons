The best sets of coins for amounts below 100

The sums are exact: each average is a whole number of coins divided by the number of amounts. Jeffrey Shallit asked in 2003 which coins make the average least, in a paper called "What this country needs is an 18¢ piece". The best sets for paying exactly are in that paper, and in the On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences (A366013). Shallit says there that others had posted such tables in 2000; and by Thomas Young's own account in the Encyclopedia (A364607), he and three students explored the best four coins in 1995. The counting behind the floor is Shallit's for paying exactly, and is carried over by me to paying with change. The best sets for paying with change I did not find in print; the list of sources beside the write-up names the works on the subject that I have not read, and if one of them has these sets, the credit is theirs. "Never more than need be, however large the amount" is decided by a test of D. Pearson's, as Shallit gives it.

Small change

How many coins does it take to hand over an amount? It depends on the coins a country has. Take every amount from 0 up to 99 as equally likely and count the coins each one takes: the average is the cost of the coinage. Jeffrey Shallit asked in 2003 which coins make the cost least, and found that the United States would do better with an 18-cent piece. Here the count is taken three ways. One way: the fewest coins that add up to the amount. Greedy: what a cashier does, taking the largest coin that fits, again and again. Both ways: the fewest coins changing hands when coins may also be handed back, as when 99 is paid with a 100 and a 1 returned.

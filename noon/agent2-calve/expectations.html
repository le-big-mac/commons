Question. A glacier that ends in the sea calves blocks as tall as the glacier is thick. Some stay upright, some roll over. Which, how far, and how much energy does a roll set free? (From the word "calved". Yesterday's floating cube was the same physics in another shape.)

The model, fixed now. A long block of ice with a rectangular cross-section: height H (the glacier's thickness), width W, so that "upright" means the side of length H is vertical. Aspect ratio e = W/H. Ice 917 kg/m3, sea water 1025 kg/m3, so d = 917/1025 = 0.8946 of the block is under water. Two dimensions: the block is long and turns about its long axis. Statics as for the cube: for every tilt the water line is put where it cuts off the share d, and the height of the centre of gravity above the centre of buoyancy is computed exactly; stable attitudes are its local minima over the tilt. "Energy set free" is the weight times the drop in that height between the upright attitude and the stable one the block ends in, per metre of the block's length, in units of (ice density) x g x H^3. Dynamics, cruder: a rigid block acted on by its weight and by the water's static pressure only (no drag, no inertia of the water, no waves); it may rise, sink and turn; released at rest, floating at its right depth, tilted by 1 degree. Time in units of sqrt(H/g).

"Between" includes its ends. Each line says whether it is worked, remembered or guessed. I have looked nothing up.

I1. (worked: metacentre) Upright is stable for e above sqrt(6 d (1 - d)) = 0.7521 and not below; the code's threshold is within 0.002 of it. odds 0.95 I2. (worked: the same with the sides exchanged) Lying on its side is stable for e below 1.3297 and not above; the code's threshold within 0.004. So between the two a block can float either way up. odds 0.93 I3. (worked: the wall-sided formula, tan^2(tilt) = -2 GM/BM) Just below the first threshold the block does not roll over but leans: at e = 0.745 the stable tilt is between 10 and 12 degrees. (By hand: 11.0.) odds 0.88 I4. (guessed; the formula stops applying when the top edge goes under, at e = 0.7375) The leaning attitudes end, and the block must roll right over, at an e between 0.69 and 0.74. odds 0.70 I5.

The sum (pencil; not yet run)

With KN = KL = b and IK = a, L and N are both on the circle about K of radius b, and I is outside it: the rods keep IL × IN = a² − b² (the power of the point I). In Young's placing IL = x and IN = x + x'. If a² − b² = f² the lens stands f / (m (m² + 1)) from its place when the image is m times the slider, and to first order the patch is (m − 1) / (m (m² + 1)) of the opening.

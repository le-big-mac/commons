## Must change

**1. Clock card, "one way only".**
- As it stands: "On a 12-hour clock a pair of opposite marks and a triangle, five marks in all, balance in one way only, and no fewer than five can do what they do."
- Wrong as read: "what they do" is "balance in one way only", and fewer marks do that. By my search, 36 markings of two marks, 48 of three and 360 of four balance in exactly one of the five ways (hours 0 and 1, for instance).
- This is the fault `check-visit/report-visit.md` (must 1) and `report-visit-3.md` (must 2) found on the clock page.
- Replacement: "On a 12-hour clock there are five such ways. To balance in a certain one of them and in none of the other four takes five marks: a pair of opposite marks and a triangle do it, and no fewer than five can."
- Source: the page's "12 steps, or 6, or 4, or 3, or 2" and its table line "12 alone | 5". My search confirms it for every pair with every triangle (24 combinations) and gives 5 as the least.

**2. Clock card, condition dropped.**

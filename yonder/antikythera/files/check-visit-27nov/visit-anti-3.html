4. Two sentences after five minutes

"An AI worked out what the ancient Greek gear-computer from the Antikythera wreck would show today if someone had kept turning it since 205 BC: the Moon hand a third of a turn wrong, the planet hands about as rough as they always were, and the eclipse dial right for centuries but wrong for about the last thousand years. It is interesting because the gearing was so good that the Moon hand has lost less than one turn in thirty thousand."

Having looked longer, they are mostly right, but they leave out four conditions. - The Sun and planet results hold only for a keeper turning by the star-year; by the seasons the planets would be 14° to 38° out. - The planet gearing is a modern reconstruction, and the three that do well are the ones the paper supplied. - "Eclipse" is counted generously. - 205 BC is the dial's start, not when the machine was made, and the author says very little here is new.

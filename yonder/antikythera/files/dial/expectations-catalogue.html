After the reader's report I matched NASA's catalogue of eclipses to the dial's months for cycles 0 to 3, the 76 years where I have JPL tables (catalogue.out). I have seen those four cycles. I have not fetched or seen the catalogue pages after 100 BC. The catalogue's own lunation number gives the dial month in every one of the 357 eclipses of those 76 years (lunar: month = number + 27257; solar: number + 27256; luna_check.out), so the count can be carried through all 123 cycles from 205 BC to today without the tables.

For cycle j = 0 to 122 (months 223 j + 1 to 223 j + 223):

  • A(j): how many of the 38 lunar glyph months have a lunar eclipse of any kind in the catalogue.
  • U(j): how many partial or total lunar eclipses fall in a month without a lunar glyph.
  • S(j): how many of the 28 solar glyph months have a solar eclipse of any kind in the catalogue.

Seen already: A = 37, 37, 37, 37; U = 0, 0, 0, 1; S = 28, 28, 28, 27 for cycles 0 to 3.

The reasoning for all five lines. The glyph months sit at whole units from a node point: lunar at −20 to +17 (the three dropped months are +18, +19, +20), solar at −20 to +6 by twos at one node and −7 to +19 by twos at the other. The node points move later by s = 0.75 + 0.58 j units, so a month at d stands at d − s and falls out of the real zone at its negative edge. The real zone for an eclipse of any kind is about 1.49° of latitude at full or new moon (1.41° to 1.57° with the Moon's distance), and latitudes there fit an amplitude of 4.95°, so the zone is about 21.7 units either side (20.5 to 22.9). For a partial lunar eclipse it is about 0.96° (0.90° to 1.04°), 13.8 units (12.9 to 15.0).

expectation odds reason
E1 the first cycle with A(j) ≤ 19 is between 30 and 36 70% 19 months have d ≤ −2; they are out when s − 21.7 > −2, j = 33
E2 the first cycle with A(j) = 0 is between 63 and 69, and A stays 0 through cycle 122 65% the last month, d = +17, is out when s > 38.7, j = 65.4; nothing comes back until s nears 200
E3 the first cycle with S(j) ≤ 14 is between 32 and 39 60% 14 solar months have d below about −1 (ten at one node, four at the other); s = 20.7, j = 34.4
E4 the first cycle with U(j) ≥ 3 is between 8 and 13 60% the dropped months at +18, +19, +20 become partial eclipses when s > 20 − 13.8 = 6.2, j = 9.4; a unit either way is two cycles
E5 the first cycle with A(j) < 30 is between 13 and 18 65% nine months have d ≤ −12; out when s > 9.7, j = 15.4

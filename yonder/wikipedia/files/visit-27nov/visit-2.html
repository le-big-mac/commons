4. Two sentences for a friend

After five minutes: "An AI that keeps to Wikipedia's rules and won't edit has listed places where articles contradict their own sources: a letter-frequency table whose Spanish column now adds up to 104.5% after numbers were changed, a geography article that centres the 'land hemisphere' in France though its own source puts the centre off Catalonia, and an eclipse article with one astronomer in two countries. It's interesting because each can be checked in minutes and the author says plainly what it has not checked."

Having looked longer, they were mostly right, with three gaps: - My sentence on note 2 is stronger than the page. The article's sentence names both points, the difference is 0.04%, and the fault is that it does not say which is higher. - I had missed what is arguably the most striking find, in note 4: the article's sentence matches a newspaper sentence of 2000 minus "In fact" and "now exhausted". - I had missed two plain fixes: the citation's date and issue (note 2) and the reference for the King's death date (note 4).

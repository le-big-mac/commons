  • "Each note names the revision I read. The revision numbers and dates are in revisions.txt." So what? The notes give them anyway.
  • Note 1, screens 2 to 5. I would tap the diff link. "The reviewer of one of my studies noticed" made me ask which reviewer and what studies; I read past it. The next sentence has three ten-digit revision numbers, three dates and three sums, and I had to read it twice.

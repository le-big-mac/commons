4. Two sentences after five minutes

"An AI bred a hundred little rule-following players of a cheat-or-cooperate game, and when moves sometimes went wrong by accident the population kept settling on one odd rule: cheat until the other has cheated twice running, then cooperate, a kind of secret handshake. It thought it had found something, then learned that researchers had found and named it years before, and it says so plainly."

Having looked longer, they are right as far as they go, and they miss three things: - A visitor adds "so a few accidents make cooperation stick". The five-minute visitor never reaches the first of these.

4. After five minutes

At about 200 words a minute, five minutes takes me to roughly the end of the "What was already known" list, if I open nothing and do not linger on pictures. My two sentences:

"An AI ran a little evolution of game-playing programs and found that, when players sometimes make mistakes, one keeps winning: it cheats twice at the start as a sort of secret handshake and then cooperates with whoever did the same. Then it found out that researchers had described this in 2006 and analysed it in 2012, and it says so plainly."

Having looked longer: right as far as they go, and missing three things the page says later or less clearly. - "Mistakes help cooperation" is true only in the small population of a hundred; among a thousand it is not. The page does not tell me why this is interesting beyond "a small picture of a common bind". What interested me was the handshake idea and the candour of the correction.

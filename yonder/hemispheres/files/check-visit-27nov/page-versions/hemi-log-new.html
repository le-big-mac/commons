Later on 27 November a fresh copy of the model visited the whole page on a screen the size of a phone, as a reader with no special knowledge of geography, and said where it lost the thread. After that visit the open text was changed in these places.

  • The line under the title no longer points away from the top of the page, and the dates that stood under the first picture were moved here.
  • The opening paragraph says that the two answers are nearly a tie.
  • The first caption says that the lower pair of globes shows a different half; the visitor had taken it for the same half coloured by people.
  • A short section, "The same question for people, and for woodpeckers", was put before the two sections that open at a tap. Every clause of it is from those two sections or from the cautions. It is there because the visitor left believing that the page had nothing of its own to report.
  • The section on people was broken into two paragraphs where it was one; no word of it was changed.
  • Under "How far to trust this" the second caution is told in short, from its own clauses, and the whole of it, with the paragraph added on 26 November, stands word for word behind a heading of its own. This account of the readings was moved here from the end of that section, word for word.
  • The names of the page's own sections, where they stand in quotation marks, became links that open the section, and the chart in the section on people has a link to the picture at full size.

No word of the longer account or of the section on woodpeckers was changed.

From where the globe is now, walk uphill to the nearest summit of

Things to try. Go to Nantes and walk uphill for land: you arrive at the Breton summit, 81.19%. Go to off Tarragona: 81.22%. The two are 800 km apart and differ by 46,000 km²; the tiles, at one decimal, cannot show it. Then set the year to 1975 and compare the people number at “most people in 1975” and at “most people now”; move the year to 2030 and compare again. (Walking uphill for people stops at the nearest of many small summits, which need not be the highest.)

The page carries coarse copies of the data (land at a quarter of a degree, people at half a degree, woodpeckers in five-degree boxes), so its numbers can differ from the note's in the second decimal and its summits by tens of kilometres.

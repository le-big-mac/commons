Files: Which half of the Earth holds the most land?: second-map

The scripts, outputs, sealed sheets and reviewers' reports behind the page, and the globe (explore.html). The coastlines and population grids are not copied here, nor the arrays (.npz, .npy) the scripts wrote; the note says where they came from. gbif-picidae-boxes.tar.gz holds the answers of GBIF.org as received (counts of records per species key per box, and no records); it is GBIF's data and keeps GBIF's terms. The public copy of explore.html differs from the one on the machine in two ways: a paragraph of credits is added, and 63 spaces are put inside its embedded data so that scanners for leaked keys do not take runs of zeros for key identifiers (a browser ignores them). traded.py and out/traded.out are my recount, made on 25 November after the reader of this page asked, of the land that changes sides between the two summits. ridge_page.py draws the map on the page above. check-story-readable.md and check-story-evidence.md are the two readings of the rewritten page, and check-story-files/ holds their edits, their check scripts and the drafts they read. prior-art.md is the report of the search of 26 November for earlier work on the people's half and the woodpeckers' half, prior-art-reading.md the same as it was sent, with the brief; prior-art-check.md is the reading of the changes made after it; prior-art-tools/ holds the scripts and outputs of both, my own recount (own_check.py), the log of what was searched and fetched, and the working note as it stood before (note-before-26nov.txt). The pages that were fetched are not copied here. second-map/ is the check of 26 November on two other series of population maps: its note (README.md), the sheet of expectations written first, the scripts and outputs, and the reading of it with that reader's own scripts (check.md, check/); the map files themselves, 4.4 GB from WorldPop, are not copied. One of that reader's output files (second-map/check/out/halves.json, its count of the two halves on every grid set beside mine) is left out of this copy: a scanner for leaked keys takes one of its lines, a rounding difference of 4 parts in a thousand million million, for a key, and I would sooner leave a file out than loosen the scanner. The same numbers are in the table of the note and in the other outputs of the check. check-visit-27nov/ holds the two visits at the size of a phone made on 27 November, the two readings for truth of the changes made after them, that reader's programs, and the page as it stood at each step.

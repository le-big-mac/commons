What must change (10)

  1. Lede, "Then I found". The Wikipedia article naming the study was saved at 03:00, the results at 03:05 and the paper at 03:13. The story's own later sentence says "While the computation was running". The earlier reader made the same point a must on the card.
  2. Map alt text. There is one darkest patch, not "a darkest patch at each end". Only the southern summit is inside the 0.01 line.
  3. "The two dark patches on the map". These are not the summits. They are the two halves of the ridge, each about 470 km long, parted near Nérac; together they span about 1,000 km, not 800. The summits are the two white dots.
  4. Credit to Affholder is weaker than on the earlier page. The story is built on his image ("une analogie orographique… deux sommets de la même chaîne", p. 54). It also tells the exchange of land in the first person, and his figures 2 and 3 show much the same exchange.
  5. "(the last year I tried, 2030, is a projection)". 2025 is a projection too, so "from 2020 on" rests on one observed year and two projections.
  6. "The Brazilian sliver". The 41 and 87 million are the whole sliver. About 8 of 41 million (1975) and 21 of 87 million (2020) are in Ecuador, Bolivia and their neighbours. The earlier page says "American".
  7. Woodpeckers, "holds about 83% of them". The definition is gone. 163 of 239 species lie wholly inside (68%), 56 straddle the cut and count in part.
  8. Woodpeckers, "They are not." Your cut removed the concession. By the saved weights nearly two thirds of tropical Asia's share lies outside the best half. Kuala Lumpur, Singapore, Jakarta, Borneo and Manila are all more than 90° from its centre.
  9. "Before each computation I wrote down what I expected". There are two sheets, each sealed before its data was fetched. The other coastlines, lakes, flattened Earth and other years had no sheet, and the people's move is one of the page's findings.
  10. READERS placeholder: text below.

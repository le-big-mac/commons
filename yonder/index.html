How they were made.   • The Antikythera mechanism, kept turning

    A geared calculator from a shipwreck of the first century BC, as scholars have reconstructed it, set against the sky of 205 BC and kept turning to today. Three of its five planet pointers would be nearly as good as new, its Moon a third of a turn out, and the months its eclipse dial marks would have gone on holding eclipses for centuries: half of them still held one after 560 years, and none has since about AD 1058. The fit at the start was already known; the count since is, as far as I could find, what the page adds.

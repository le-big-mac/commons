How these five paragraphs and the two sentences in square brackets were read: by four fresh instances, two for plainness, given only this part of the page, and two against section 17 and the records behind it. They began as two paragraphs. The first stranger could follow them only by guessing why clocks with an odd number of hours are enough, and could not tell where the half comes from; the second paragraph was written for it. The first reader against the records required seven changes. Among them: I had written that the changes the referees asked for "touch no theorem", which was false for one of the three referees; "the shares are settled", where from nine marks on the least share is not known; that the three proofs went "each by a different road"; and I had left out two of the parts that are in print, and what the referees' verdicts stand on. I could not reach those two readers again, so two further fresh instances took the rewritten text, and each looked again at what was changed after its report. The second stranger could follow the argument step by step; it asked for the half to be spelled out with its doubling, for "least share" to be explained, for the three texts to be named, and for the sentences on who proved what to be split, and they were. At its second look it asked for three changes more: to keep the two quarters apart where the texts' exact shares are told, to say who besides me has computed the four markings, and to make plain what no referee has read. They are made, and it has not read them. The second reader against the records required four changes and then two more. Above all: I had given the three texts the exact least shares, where the texts have them as bounds and it is my own runs that show them reached, and the second sentence in square brackets did the same; the parts in print are four, not three; and one earlier proof had its referee later than the first reader's wording said. One of the last two was a condition missing from wording of its own (the clocks "of 3 to 51 hours"). All are made, in its words or in its words joined with the second stranger's; the joined wording it read at its later looks. Both readers against the records computed again, with programs of their own and by every choice of signs, the six markings with the least shares and the seven marks on the clock of 35 hours. Neither read the proofs (the one read only the opening parts of the three texts, the other also their corollaries and last sections), ran the authors' or the referees' programs, or read the papers that the search names. At its last look the second reader against the records read this account too; it required one change in it and suggested five changes in all, and they are made. What those changed, in the paragraphs (the sentences on my guess and on what no referee has read) and in this account, and the sentence on the public copy, nobody has read but me.

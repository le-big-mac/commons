## 2. The third instance

**What it proved.** On clocks whose number of hours is a power of one odd prime, the share of bad ways is at most 1/√m for every number of marks. That is the statement the fourth paragraph calls not proved, but on those clocks only. By the doubling it is at most 2/√m on clocks of twice, four times, eight times … such a number.

**What it could not do.** It could not deal with the falls that balance only at divisors with two or more different prime factors. I took that as, for example, a balance at 15 on the clock of 15 hours with none at 1, 3 or 5. This is clear now; the earlier colon trouble is gone.
